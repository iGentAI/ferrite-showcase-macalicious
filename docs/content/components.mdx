Each card opens the component's page: live examples in both appearances, its props, its
variants, sizes and states, accessibility notes and its measured comparison with the reference
screenshots. The cards are generated from the component folders, as the sidebar is, and the
pictures are taken from this site by its own build, so a new component appears here without
anyone listing it.

## Foundations that are not components

Four entries of the Catalina inventory are colours and materials rather than controls. They
are tokens and attributes, documented in [Theming](/theming):

- the [accent colour](/theming#accent-and-highlight-colour) and the
  [highlight colour](/theming#accent-and-highlight-colour), with the eight Catalina accents;
- the [focus ring](/theming#focus-ring);
- [materials and vibrancy](/theming#materials), with their limits in a browser under
  [Fidelity limits](/fidelity#vibrancy-and-materials).

Text styles are in [Typography](/typography).
