<Example name="basic" />

Press a recommendation's button: Store in iCloud…, Optimize… and Turn On… carry the
recommendation out and the row then says Completed, and Review Files opens Documents. Select a
category in the sidebar for its files, sort them by a heading, select one and press Delete…:
the category's size in the sidebar and the free space in the window's title follow. The
recommendations and the sidebar are those of the reference screenshots; the files are
invented, the icons are plain stand-in shapes, and nothing on the machine the page runs on is
read or changed.

## What it shows

- A [Window](/components/window) with the plain 22 pt [Title Bar](/components/title-bar): the
  [Window Controls](/components/window-controls), all three enabled, and a title computed from
  the disk's name, the free space and the capacity.
- The sidebar: a [Sidebar](/components/sidebar) set with its custom properties to 28 pt rows
  that touch, 20 pt glyphs and a 6 pt label gap. Recommendations comes first, a
  [Separator](/components/separator) under it, then the categories that can be opened, each
  with its size as the row's accessory, a second separator, and the categories that cannot:
  System and Other are disabled rows, dimmed and skipped by the pointer and the keyboard. The
  selection is emphasised while the sidebar has the keyboard focus in the key window.
- A [Split View](/components/split-view) with a fixed divider: the sidebar keeps its 200 pt.
- Recommendations: a list in a [Scroll View](/components/scroll-view). Each row has an
  [Image View](/components/image-view) as the stand-in for its 44 pt icon, a bold title and
  12 pt text set with [Labels](/components/label), a 127 pt [Push Button](/components/push-button)
  centred on the row, and a separator under it. A recommendation that was carried out shows a
  check mark and "Completed" in place of its button.
- A category: a [Table View](/components/table-view) of its files with the columns Name, Kind,
  Last Accessed and Size, whose every heading sorts (sizes as numbers, dates by day), over a
  bar with the number of files or the selected file and the Delete… button. A category with no
  files says so with an [Empty State](/components/empty-state). The order is shared by all
  categories, and each keeps its selected file while another row is shown.

<Example name="documents" />

Storage Management has no toolbar and no search field, so this demo has none either. Catalina
asks for confirmation before it turns a recommendation on or deletes a file; the demo has no
confirmation sheet: it acts at once and then tells its host, which cannot cancel the change.

## Hosting the window

`StorageManagement` renders the library's `Window` as its root and passes every `Window` prop
to it, so it does not assume it fills the page. Place it with `style`; a window manager gives
it `state`, the size, and eight more props that the component hands to the window controls and
the title bar. These are the props a [Managed Window](/components/managed-window)'s `render`
form supplies, so the component can be spread with them as they come.

```tsx
import { ManagedWindow } from "macalicious/managed-window";
import { storageManagementApp } from "./app";

<ManagedWindow
  id={storageManagementApp.id}
  defaultFrame={{ x: 80, y: 60, ...storageManagementApp.defaultSize }}
  minWidth={storageManagementApp.minSize.width}
  minHeight={storageManagementApp.minSize.height}
  render={(props) => <storageManagementApp.component {...props} />}
/>;
```

| Prop | Type | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `categories` | `StorageCategory[]` | The rows under Recommendations, each with its size and files; the sidebar of the light screenshot by default. |
| `diskName`, `capacity`, `available` | `string`, bytes, bytes | The window's title: "Macintosh SSD", 249.85 GB and 195.81 GB by default. |
| `selectedId`, `defaultSelectedId`, `onSelectedIdChange` | `string` and a function | The selected row, controlled or not: `"recommendations"` or a category's id. An id that names no row that can be opened shows Recommendations. |
| `material` | `"translucent" \| "opaque"` | The sidebar's surface; translucent by default, opaque on a plain page. |
| `onRecommendation` | `(id) => void` | A recommendation's button: `"icloud"`, `"optimize"`, `"trash"` or `"clutter"`. |
| `onDelete` | `(file, categoryId) => void` | Delete… removed a file. |
| `onClose`, `onMinimize`, `onZoom` | functions | Passed to the `WindowControls`. |
| `zoomRole`, `minimizeDisabled`, `zoomDisabled` | as on `WindowControls` | Passed to the `WindowControls`. |
| `onDragRegionPointerDown`, `onDragRegionDoubleClick` | functions | Passed to the `TitleBar`: a press or a double click on the bar that is not on a control. |
| `width`, `height` | `number \| string` | The frame; 801 by 532 by default, as captured. |
| the rest | `WindowProps` | `state`, `fullScreen`, `style`, `className`, `ref`, pointer events and so on. |

`app.ts` exports the descriptor a desktop page reads, as `storageManagementApp` and as `app`:
`id`, `name`, `component`, `defaultSize` (801 by 532), `minSize` (640 by 320), `resizable` and
`menus`, the menu titles after the Apple menu. The [Desktop](/demos/desktop) finds it there
and launches the window from its Dock.

The content is data (`storage-management-data.ts`): a category has an `id`, a `name`, a `size`
in bytes, optional `files` and `description`, and `selectable: false` when it cannot be opened.
`defaultCategories` is the sidebar of the light screenshot and `darkCaptureCategories`, with
`darkCaptureAvailable`, that of the dark one. The pure functions are there too: `formatSize()`
and `formatDate()`, `sortFiles()`, `deleteFile()`, `categorySize()`, `availableSize()`,
`windowTitle()` and `resolveSelection()`. A size that is nothing, negative or not a number is
written "Zero KB"; a category is taken to be at least as large as the files it lists; a
category whose id is taken, or is `"recommendations"`, is not listed.

## On the desktop's Ferrite application

When the `macalicious-desktop` Ferrite application serves the desktop (`ferrite-apps/desktop`),
`DesktopStorageManagement` (`storage-management-desktop.tsx`) reports the signed-in person's
**Ferrite Drive** instead of the captured Mac. The title gives the free space of the drive's
quota (128 MiB a person). The sidebar lists Documents, Photos, Music, Movies and Other Files in
the drive, each content counted once however many copies name it, the Trash, **Large Files** (5
MB or more, largest first), **File Browser** (every file of the drive with its folder) and **App
Data**, the documents the desktop's apps keep for the person in Ferrite's store, which do not
count against the quota; a **Kept by** row for each app shows what it holds, and Unsupported Apps
is dimmed, as a browser runs no Mac applications. Recommendations offer Empty
Trash, Review Large Files and Reduce Clutter, each carried out for real: Empty Trash and
Delete… on a drive file go through the drive's own routes (`/api/files/ops`), and Delete… on
an app's document through that app's own `DELETE` route. The report comes from
`GET /api/storage-management/usage` (`ferrite-apps/desktop/src/apps/storage_management`).

Show in Finder, on a drive file, asks Finder to show it through the desktop's open-file channel
(`demos/finder/finder-open.ts`, app `finder`) and opens Finder, which shows the file's folder
with the file selected. Delete… and Empty Trash ask first, as macOS does: a drive file and the
Trash are erased for good. Old Messages attachments are not offered: this build keeps none.

## Accessibility

- The window is a `region` named by its title, so the free space is part of its name.
- The sidebar is a `tree` named "Categories" and one tab stop: the up and down arrow keys move
  the selection, Home and End go to the first and last row, and typing selects by name. A
  category's row is named by its name and its size ("Documents 6.92 GB"). System and Other are
  `aria-disabled` and are never selected. The glyphs and the lines are decoration.
- The recommendations are a list named "Recommendations"; each title is an `h2`. A
  recommendation that was carried out announces "Completed" as a `status`.
- A category's pane is a `region` named after it. Its table is a `grid` named after the
  category ("Documents files") and one tab stop; each heading is a button, and its column
  reports the order with `aria-sort`. The line beside Delete… is a `status`: the number of
  files and their size, or the selected file.
- Delete… is disabled until a file is selected. The fixed divider is not a tab stop.

## Fidelity

`storage-management.visual.json` compares the whole window with the Storage Management
screenshots in both appearances, each as captured and again as `-layout` with the text and the
stand-in artwork masked. The dark screenshot lists Podcasts and Trash as well, has Other alone
under the second line and 193.78 GB free, so it has an example of its own. The accent
selection of the screenshots needs the keyboard focus, which the comparisons give the sidebar
with a real click. Scores from `npm run visual -- storage-management/` in Chromium on Linux
with the fallback font: the share of pixels that differ and the mean colour difference out of
255.

| Comparison | Mismatch 1x | Mean Δ 1x | Mismatch 2x | Mean Δ 2x |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `window-light` | 7.50% | 7.83 | 6.22% | 7.45 |
| `window-light-layout` (masked) | 0.51% | 0.46 | 0.02% | 0.33 |
| `window-dark` | 7.67% | 8.33 | 6.29% | 7.83 |
| `window-dark-layout` (masked) | 0.52% | 1.07 | 0.01% | 0.88 |

The masks of `window-light-layout` cover 31.6% of the crop and those of `window-dark-layout`
32.8%: the title, the glyphs, names and sizes of the sidebar, the icon and text of each
recommendation, and the button titles. A fifth comparison, `documents-light`, checks a state
no screenshot shows: after a click on Documents and one on its first file it reads the
emphasised selection of the table, the grey selection of the sidebar that gave up the focus,
the sidebar's fill where Recommendations was selected, and the bar under the table.

Measured in the screenshots and reproduced: the 801 by 532 pt frame, the 22 pt title bar, the
200 pt sidebar and the 1 pt divider; the 28 pt rows with Recommendations directly under the
title bar, the glyphs 12 pt from the window's edge, the names 38 pt from it and the sizes
ending 9 pt before the divider; the two lines of the sidebar, 3 pt from the rows around them
and 8 pt from each edge, in their measured colour; the name and glyph colours of System and
Other, whose sizes are not dimmed; the recommendations 113 pt apart (128 pt with three lines
of text) with their lines 21 pt and 22 pt from the pane's edges; the icons 35 pt from the
pane's edge, the text 18 pt after them, breaking where the capture breaks it; and the 127 pt
buttons 34.5 pt from the window's edge, centred on their rows.

What differs, and why:

- **The icons and glyphs** are Apple's artwork and are not reproduced: the four 44 pt icons of
  the recommendations are plain rounded squares, the glyph of Recommendations a plain disc and
  those of the categories plain rounded squares, in neutral token colours. The `-layout`
  comparisons mask them.
- **Text.** Set in the fallback font off Apple devices, which is nearly all of the unmasked
  scores.
- **The fills.** The sidebar is the library's opaque sidebar colour (`#e7e8e8`, `#2c2d2f`)
  where the captures hold `#e7e7e5` and `#2b2c2d`, and the dark content is `#1e1e1e` where the
  capture, tinted by the desktop picture, holds `#1e1e20`: the mean difference of the masked
  comparisons.
- **Not in a screenshot (provisional):** a category's pane (its description, table, columns,
  bar and Delete… button, and the files themselves), a completed recommendation, the sidebar
  when it does not have the keyboard focus, the window when it is not key, and every size
  other than the captured one.
