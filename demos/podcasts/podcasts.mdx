<Example name="basic" />

Click the episode under Up Next: the display names it, the play button becomes a pause button
and the progress line starts to move. Nothing is heard. The transport is a clock that advances
one second per second, and the line can be dragged. Choose a place in the sidebar, open a
show from Browse and subscribe to it, or type in the search field. Every show and episode is
invented for the demo, and the covers and icons are original stand-in drawings.

On the desktop served by the `macalicious-desktop` Ferrite application (`ferrite-apps/desktop`),
`app.ts`'s component is a real podcast player for the person signed in
(`podcasts-desktop.tsx`, through `podcasts-server.ts`). The search field asks Apple's podcast
directory, Browse and Top Charts list Apple's chart, and a show's page lists the episodes of
its own feed, which the server reads. A show whose feed is on a host the server cannot reach
says why, and cannot be followed. Episodes play in the browser from their publisher, and the
bar's buttons, progress line, volume and speed act on that audio. An episode's menu has Go to
Episode, its own page with the feed's notes. While the window is open the server looks for new
episodes and delivers each to Notification Center once (`GET /api/podcasts/watch`). The shows followed, the
episodes played, Up Next, the episode loaded, the speed and the settings come back after a
reload; episodes published since the person last looked are marked and announced. The place in
an episode is kept only while the window is open: an episode opened again after a reload
starts at 0:00.
No episode is downloaded there: Downloaded and Preferences say so. Everywhere else the window
is this demo, and nothing is kept.

## What it shows

- A [Window](/components/window) without a title bar, filled by a
  [Media App Chrome](/components/media-app-chrome) in its `podcasts` variant: the sidebar
  column runs the height of the window and holds the
  [Window Controls](/components/window-controls) and a sidebar
  [Search Field](/components/search-field); the 55 pt playback bar sits over the pane.
- The bar: skip back, play or pause and skip forward; a
  [Now Playing Lcd](/components/now-playing-lcd) whose progress line is a slider; the volume
  slider; and two toggle buttons that open the loaded episode's notes or the queue beside the
  content.
- The places: a [Sidebar](/components/sidebar) of two sections, each row with an icon.
- The content: a [Scroll View](/components/scroll-view) of
  [Card Grids](/components/card-grid). Listen Now is a shelf of `episode` cards, Browse and
  the library's shows are `square` cards, and the charts and episode lists are `lockup` rows
  whose accessory is a [Store Pill Button](/components/store-pill-button). The section headers
  take Podcasts' type through the card grid's variables. An
  [Empty State](/components/empty-state) stands in when a search finds nothing.
- Preferences: a [Preferences Window](/components/preferences-window) with a
  [Preferences Toolbar](/components/preferences-toolbar) of three panes, whose rows are
  [Pop-Up Buttons](/components/pop-up-button) and [Checkboxes](/components/checkbox).

| Place | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| Listen Now | Up Next: the new episodes of the shows you follow, and every episode you have started and not finished. A click on a card plays it; See All goes to Episodes. |
| Browse | Four featured shows on a shelf, then every show. A click on a show opens its page, with its episodes and a Subscribe button. |
| Top Charts | The shows ranked, each with a button that follows or unfollows it, and the newest episode of each. |
| Recently Updated, Shows | The shows you follow, by their newest episode or by name. |
| Episodes | Every episode of the shows you follow, the newest first, each with a Play button. |
| Downloaded | What the General preference says to download: only the new episodes, every unplayed one, or none. Nothing is stored; the list follows the setting. |

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Skip back, skip forward | Move the position by the seconds shown in the glyph, 15 and 30 unless the Playback preferences say otherwise. Unavailable while nothing is loaded. |
| Play, Pause | Runs or stops the clock. With nothing loaded, Play starts Up Next. |
| Progress line | Drag it, or focus it and use the arrow keys, to move the position. |
| Volume | Kept, and applied to nothing. The audio-output button is unavailable: there is no output to choose. |
| Episode Notes | Opens a panel with the loaded episode's description; again closes it. |
| Up Next | Opens a panel with the episodes that follow in the queue. |
| Search | Lists the shows whose name, publisher or category contains the text, and the episodes whose title or description does. |

An episode that reaches its end is marked as played and leaves Up Next. With Continuous
Playback on, the next episode of the list it was started from begins; otherwise the player
empties. An episode that was left part of the way is taken up where it was left.

## Preferences

<Example name="preferences" />

Podcasts' Preferences are modeless: the window opens beside the player, which stays usable and
becomes the main window while Preferences are key. A press on either window brings it to the
front, over the other; Preferences that the player covers come back with the command that
opens them. Command-comma or Control-comma in the
Podcasts window opens them, and `preferencesOpen` lets a menu command do it. The settings take
effect at once: the Downloaded row follows Automatically Download Episodes, the skip buttons
follow the two pop-up buttons of the Playback pane, and Continuous Playback decides what
happens when an episode ends. Refresh Podcasts and the Advanced pane's checkbox are kept and
change nothing, since nothing is fetched.

## Hosting the window

<Example name="hosted" />

`Podcasts` renders the library's `Window` as its root and passes every `Window` prop to it, so
it does not assume it fills the page. Place it with `style`; a window manager gives it `state`,
the size, and eight more props that the component hands to the window controls and to its drag
region: the playback bar and the top of the sidebar, since the window has no title bar. These
are the props a [Managed Window](/components/managed-window)'s `render` form supplies, so the
component can be spread with them as they come.

```tsx
import { ManagedWindow } from "macalicious/managed-window";
import { app } from "./app";

<ManagedWindow
  id={app.id}
  title={app.name}
  resizable={app.resizable}
  defaultFrame={{ x: 80, y: 60, ...app.defaultSize }}
  minWidth={app.minSize.width}
  minHeight={app.minSize.height}
  render={(props) => <app.component {...props} />}
/>;
```

| Prop | Type | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `shows`, `episodes` | `PodcastsShow[]`, `PodcastsEpisode[]` | The catalogue; the eight invented shows and their sixteen episodes by default. A show's `subscribed` and an episode's `unplayed` say how the library starts. |
| `defaultSection`, `onSectionChange` | `PodcastsSection`, function | The sidebar row selected at first, `"listen-now"` by default, and a report of each choice. |
| `defaultEpisode` | `string` | The `id` of an episode that is loaded at first, paused at its start, as in the Dark Aqua screenshot. |
| `defaultVolume` | `number` | The volume at first, 0 to 100. |
| `material` | `"translucent" \| "opaque"` | The sidebar's surface: vibrant over a desktop, or opaque on a plain page. |
| `defaultSettings` | `PodcastsSettings` | What Preferences start with; the settings of the screenshots by default. |
| `preferencesOpen`, `defaultPreferencesOpen`, `onPreferencesOpenChange` | `boolean`, `boolean`, function | The Preferences window, controlled or not. |
| `preferencesContainer` | `HTMLElement` | Where the Preferences window is rendered; the Podcasts window's offset parent by default. |
| `onClose`, `onMinimize`, `onZoom` | functions | Passed to the `WindowControls`. |
| `zoomRole`, `minimizeDisabled`, `zoomDisabled`, `controlsProps` | as on `WindowControls` | Passed to the `WindowControls`. |
| `onDragRegionPointerDown`, `onDragRegionDoubleClick` | functions | A press with the primary button, or a double click, on the playback bar or the top of the sidebar, not on a control in them. |
| `width`, `height` | `number \| string` | The frame; 1137 by 764 by default, as captured. |
| the rest | `WindowProps` | `state`, `fullScreen`, `style`, `className`, `ref`, pointer events and so on. |

`app.ts` exports the descriptor a desktop page reads, as `podcastsApp` and as `app`: `id`,
`name`, `component`, `defaultSize` (1137 by 764), `minSize` (900 by 420), `resizable` and
`menus`, the menu titles after the Apple menu. It also exports `PodcastsPreferences`, the
Preferences window on its own, which holds its settings or takes them with `settings` and
`onSettingsChange`.

The library, the queue and the positions live in the component's state for as long as it is
mounted; a reload brings the catalogue back as it was. `DesktopPodcasts`, the component
`app.ts` names, is this demo except on the Ferrite desktop.

## Accessibility

- The window is a `region` named "Podcasts". The playback bar is a `toolbar` named "Playback
  controls" and one tab stop; the arrow keys move between its buttons.
- Every button of the bar shows only a glyph and is named with `aria-label`. The skip
  buttons' names carry their seconds, and Play becomes Pause with the glyph. Episode Notes and
  Up Next are toggle buttons.
- The display is a `group` named "Now Playing"; its progress line is a `slider` named
  "Playback position" whose value is read as the position and the length.
- The sidebar is a `tree` named "Podcasts": one tab stop, the arrow keys move the selection
  and typing selects by name. The search field is a `searchbox`; Escape clears it.
- Each section of the content is a `region` named by its heading, and its cards are a list.
  An episode card of a shelf and a show's card are buttons. A lockup is not: its Play or
  Subscribe button is, named with the episode or the show it acts on.
- A search without results is announced through a `status`.
- The Preferences window is a `region` named after its pane; its toolbar is a `tablist`.
- The position is not announced while it advances: a live region that spoke every second
  would be noise. The progress line gives it on request.

Artwork is masked in every comparison, the ones that keep the text included: the show's 252 pt
cover, the column of the seven sidebar icons and the display's well in the window, and the
three toolbar items' icons in Preferences (their labels are compared; the layout comparisons
mask the labels too, with the rest of the text). The demo's artwork is abstract
placeholders, not drawn after the pictures in the captures, so comparing it would say nothing.

Measured in the screenshots and reproduced: the 1137 by 764 pt frame; the 260 pt sidebar with
the window controls, the search field, the two headers and the seven rows in their 26 pt
pitch under a 28 pt selection; the playback bar and everything in it, which is the media app
chrome's own measured layout; the large title 40 pt from the pane's edge with the line under
it; the shelf's header; the 252 pt episode card with its caption, title, three lines of
description and duration; and in Preferences the 383 by 176 pt frame and the two pop-up
buttons, 117.5 pt wide and 27 pt apart, 25.5 pt from the right edge.

What differs, and why:

- **Artwork.** The show's cover, the seven sidebar icons, the mark in the display's empty
  well and the three Preferences icons are abstract placeholders: a gradient between two
  system colours with a broadcast mark, plain glyphs on a rounded square, plain shapes. None
  is drawn after the capture's artwork, and all are masked. The Apple logo that the idle
  display shows in the capture is not drawn.
- **Text.** Set in the fallback font off Apple devices, and the show and its episode are
  invented, so the strings differ from the capture's. The Aqua capture has text typed into
  the search field while Listen Now is still shown; the demo searches as you type, so its
  field is empty there.
- **Paging chevrons.** Podcasts draws a dimmed chevron in each margin of a shelf. The card
  grid does not, and the demo does not add a control of its own; a shelf scrolls with the
  pointer and with the arrow keys.
- **Audio output.** The capture shows the AirPlay button enabled. The demo has no output to
  choose and disables it, and the drawing is not Apple's mark.
- **Sidebar rows.** The library's rows are 24 pt high with 2 pt under them; Podcasts'
  selection is 28 pt high in the same 26 pt pitch, which the demo sets through the rows'
  `style`.
- **Not in a screenshot (provisional):** every place but Listen Now, a show's page, the
  search results, the two side panels, the Playback and Advanced panes, the caption of a
  started episode in Aqua, the window when it is not key, and every size other than the
  captured one.
- **Not built in the demo:** sound, downloads, the Account and Controls menus' commands,
  episode notes with links, playback speed, the sleep timer, sharing, stations and the mini
  player. The Ferrite desktop's Podcasts plays sound and has a speed button; it offers no
  downloads either.
