<Example name="basic" />

Switch between People and Devices, select a row to bring its marker to the middle of the map,
change the map type, zoom, show the current location or share it. The map is an abstract
placeholder and the devices are invented; nothing is located or shared anywhere.

## What it shows

- A [Window](/components/window) with no title bar: `topHighlight` draws the two highlight
  rows along its top edge. Inside it, [Catalyst Chrome](/components/catalyst-chrome) with
  `bar="none"`: the [Window Controls](/components/window-controls) float over the sidebar
  where a 22 pt title bar would have them, in the chrome's strip, which is the drag region.
- The sidebar: the chrome's `CatalystSidebar`, 261 pt wide with its plain one-point line, in
  Find My's own lighter fill.
- People and Devices: the [Catalyst Segmented Control](/components/catalyst-segmented-control),
  a radio group whose selected segment is a raised pill.
- A [Separator](/components/separator), then a [List View](/components/list-view) of 48 pt
  rows with an inset line, each a disc (an [Avatar](/components/avatar) for a person, an
  [Image View](/components/image-view) with a glyph for a device), a name and a second line;
  an [Empty State](/components/empty-state) stands in for a list without rows.
- "Share My Location": an [Image Button](/components/image-button) in its row layout, used as
  a toggle.
- The map, held by an Image View that names it, with the map controls over it: the map types
  at the leading corner, and at the trailing one a `MapButton` in its `rounded` variant beside
  the `MapCompass`, whose 3D button is dimmed as in Find My, over the `MapZoomControl`.
- The Legal link, a [Borderless Button](/components/borderless-button), and the map's note, a
  [Label](/components/label) that is a `status`.

| Command | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People, Devices | the sidebar | Shows one list or the other. Each keeps its own selection. |
| Select a row | the list | Moves the map so that the row's marker is in its middle and sets its name under the marker; the map's note names it. "Me" is the dot of the current location. |
| Share My Location | the sidebar's foot | Switches sharing: the second line of "Me" says so, the dot gets a halo, and the note reports the change. |
| Map, Hybrid, Satellite | the map | The map type: the satellite view darkens the placeholder's ground and takes its bands away, the hybrid view lays them over the dark ground. |
| Current location | the map | Moves the map so that the dot is in its middle. |
| Compass | the map | The ring turns the map north up. The 3D button is unavailable. |
| Zoom | the map | Nine zoom levels, from 25% to 400%; a half at its limit is unavailable. |
| Legal | the map | Says that the map is a drawing of the demo's own; a second click takes the note away. |

## Source walkthrough

The demo is six files in `demos/find-my/`.

**`find-my-data.ts`** is what the demo knows: the user ("Me", "Memphis, TN", the two lines of
the captures), three invented devices, a point of the abstract map for each, the captured
middle of the view, the zoom limits, and `project(point, center, scale, heading)`, which says
how far from the middle of the view a point of the map is drawn.

**`find-my-artwork.tsx`** holds the drawings, all of them original and all placeholders: the
map, the glyph of the location button, the plus on a disc of "Share My Location" and the three
device glyphs. The map is one tile repeated in every direction and placed by the view's
middle, scale and heading, so it needs no measuring:

```tsx
<pattern id={id} patternUnits="userSpaceOnUse" width={560} height={420}
         patternTransform={`rotate(${-heading}) scale(${scale}) translate(${-center.x} ${-center.y})`}>
  {/* two wide and two narrow bands, a disc, two blocks, a stripe and two short bars */}
</pattern>
```

**`find-my.tsx`** is the window. Its state is the list shown, the selection of each list, the
map's type, middle, zoom and heading, whether the location is shared, and the map's note:

```tsx
const items = list === "people" ? people : devices;
const selected = items.find((item) => item.id === selection[list]) ?? null;
const scale = scaleOf(zoom);
```

Find My draws no title bar and no toolbar. `CatalystChrome` with `bar="none"` holds the
window controls in a 22 pt strip, the sidebar for the window's whole height, and the map as
its content:

```tsx
<Window aria-label="Find My" topHighlight width={1135} height={723}>
  <CatalystChrome
    bar="none"
    controls={<WindowControls onClose={onClose} onMinimize={onMinimize} onZoom={onZoom} />}
    onDragRegionPointerDown={onDragRegionPointerDown}
    onDragRegionDoubleClick={onDragRegionDoubleClick}
    sidebar={
      <CatalystSidebar aria-label="People and devices" width={261} divider="plain" style={SIDEBAR}>
        {/* the switch, the rule, the list, Share My Location */}
      </CatalystSidebar>
    }
  >
    <div className="mac-find-my-map" data-map-type={mapType}>{/* the map */}</div>
  </CatalystChrome>
</Window>
```

The sidebar's fill and line are Find My's own colours. A demo's stylesheet may not set
another component's variables, so they go in the sidebar's `style`:

```tsx
const SIDEBAR = {
  "--mac-catalyst-chrome-sidebar-fill": "var(--mac-find-my-sidebar)",
  "--mac-catalyst-chrome-sidebar-divider": "var(--mac-find-my-divider)",
};

<CatalystSegmentedControl aria-label="Show" value={list} onValueChange={showList}>
  <CatalystSegment value="people">People</CatalystSegment>
  <CatalystSegment value="devices">Devices</CatalystSegment>
</CatalystSegmentedControl>
```

The list is the library's, with a row drawn from its parts:

```tsx
<ListView aria-label="People" count={items.length} rowHeight={48}
          separator="inset" separatorInset={49.5} renderRow={renderRow}
          value={selected ? [selected.id] : []} onValueChange={([id]) => select(id)} />

// renderRow
<ListViewRowLeading>
  {"kind" in item ? (
    <ImageView mask="circle" width={29} height={29}><DeviceGlyph kind={item.kind} /></ImageView>
  ) : (
    <Avatar>{isMe(item) ? undefined : initialsOf(item.name)}</Avatar>
  )}
</ListViewRowLeading>
<ListViewRowTitle>{item.name}</ListViewRowTitle>
<ListViewRowDetail>{detailOf(item, sharing)}</ListViewRowDetail>
```

The map controls are the library's too. The cluster at the trailing corner is a vertical group
nested in a horizontal one, as the Map Controls page describes for Find My:

```tsx
<MapControls placement="none" aria-label="Map controls" className="mac-find-my-controls">
  <MapButton variant="rounded" aria-label="Show Your Current Location" onClick={locate}>
    <LocationGlyph />
  </MapButton>
  <MapControls placement="none" orientation="vertical" aria-label="Compass and zoom">
    <MapCompass heading={heading} onNorthUp={() => setHeading(0)} pitchDisabled />
    <MapZoomControl onZoomIn={zoomIn} onZoomOut={zoomOut}
                    zoomInDisabled={zoom >= MAX_ZOOM} zoomOutDisabled={zoom <= MIN_ZOOM} />
  </MapControls>
</MapControls>
```

A marker is a point moved from the middle of the map by `project`; a device has a disc with
its glyph, and the selected row's marker has the name under it. The user has no marker: the
dot of the current location, which is part of the drawing, is theirs.

**`find-my.css`** places the strip, the sidebar's column and what lies over the map, and
colours the placeholder by the map's type. **`find-my.tokens.css`** holds the colours no
shared token has: the measured ones (the sidebar's fill and line, the land, the track of the
switch, the dot and its ring) and the demo's own for the placeholders.

**`app.ts`** is the descriptor the [Desktop](/demos/desktop) reads.

## Hosting the window

`FindMy` renders the library's `Window` as its root and passes every `Window` prop to it, so
it does not assume it fills the page. Place it with `style`; a window manager gives it
`state`, the size, and eight more props that the component hands to the window controls and
to the strip along its top edge. These are the props a
[Managed Window](/components/managed-window)'s `render` form supplies, so the component can be
spread with them as they come.

<Example name="hosted" />

| Prop | Type | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `people` | `FindMyPerson[]` | The rows of the People list; the user alone by default. The person with the id `"me"` is the user: the dot of the current location stands at their coordinate. A device with that id is a device like any other. |
| `devices` | `FindMyDevice[]` | The rows of the Devices list; the demo's three by default. |
| `defaultList` | `"people" \| "devices"` | The list shown at first; `"people"`, as in the captures. |
| `defaultSelectedId` | `string \| null` | The row of that list selected at first; `null`, as in the captures. |
| `defaultMapType` | `"map" \| "hybrid" \| "satellite"` | The map type selected at first; `"map"`. |
| `defaultCenter` | `{ x, y }` | The point of the abstract map at the middle of the view at first; the captures'. |
| `defaultZoom` | `number` | The zoom level at first, from -4 to 4; `0`. |
| `defaultHeading` | `number` | The direction that is up at first, in degrees clockwise from north; `0`. |
| `defaultSharing` | `boolean` | Whether the location is shared at first; `false`. |
| `onSelectionChange` | `(item: FindMyPerson \| FindMyDevice \| null) => void` | Called with the person or device whose row is selected when it changes, also when a person and a device have the same id. |
| `onSharingChange` | `(sharing: boolean) => void` | Called when "Share My Location" is switched. |
| `onClose`, `onMinimize`, `onZoom` | functions | Passed to the `WindowControls`. |
| `zoomRole`, `minimizeDisabled`, `zoomDisabled` | as on `WindowControls` | Passed to the `WindowControls`. |
| `onDragRegionPointerDown`, `onDragRegionDoubleClick` | functions | A press or a double click on the 22 pt strip along the top edge that is not on a window control. |
| `width`, `height` | `number \| string` | The frame; 1135 by 723 by default, as captured. |
| the rest | `WindowProps` | `state`, `fullScreen`, `style`, `className`, `ref`, pointer events and so on. |

`app.ts` exports the descriptor a desktop page reads, as `findMyApp` and as `app`: `id`,
`name`, `component`, `defaultSize` (1135 by 723), `minSize` (680 by 360), `resizable` and
`menus`, the menu titles after the Apple menu. Its `component` is `DesktopFindMy`
(`find-my-desktop.tsx`), which renders `FindMy` in every build but one, described next.

## On the desktop's Ferrite application

When the `macalicious-desktop` Ferrite application serves the desktop
(`ferrite-apps/desktop`), `DesktopFindMy` shows a working Find My instead of the demo. Nothing
of it runs on this documentation site, which asks no server anything.

- **The map** is OpenStreetMap's standard map, the same one Maps shows
  (`../maps/maps-tiles.tsx`), with this browser's location as the blue dot.
- **This browser** asks for its location with the Geolocation API when the window opens, and
  reports it to the `find-my` module as one of the person's devices, again after a minute or
  after moving 50 m. A refusal is explained in the window. Each report carries the time this
  browser made it, and the module keeps a device's newest report by that time: a report that
  reaches the server after a later one (a retry, or a second tab of the same browser) changes
  nothing, so a device never moves back to an older position. A report dated more than ten
  minutes ahead of the server's clock is refused, which bounds how long a fast clock that is
  then set right can hold its device.
- **Devices** lists the person's browsers, read every ten seconds while the window is open,
  each with its last position and when it was seen. Play Sound asks a device for its sound;
  that device's own browser plays it with Web Audio the next time it reads the list, and
  clears the request. Remove This Device forgets another browser; this one cannot remove
  itself while it reports.
- **People** shows the person and the accounts they share their location with or follow, with a
  card for each: Contact, Message, Directions, Share or Stop Sharing My Location, Ask To Follow
  Location, a notification when they arrive at or leave your current location or a place you name, a notification to them when you do, and Remove.

Devices are kept per person: every browser signed in to an account is one of its devices, and
before anyone signs in, the browser's own session is the person. Nobody sees or changes another
person's devices, even by naming one's id. An account sees another's newest position, with no
device name or history, only while that account shares it with them; stopping takes effect at
once.

## Accessibility

- The window has no title, so it is a `region` named "Find My" by `aria-label`. The window
  controls come first in it; the strip that holds them is a pointer-only drag region.
- The sidebar is a `navigation` landmark named "People and devices".
- People and Devices is a `radiogroup` named "Show": one Tab stop, and the arrow keys move
  the selection.
- The list is a `listbox` named "People" or "Devices"; it keeps the focus and names the
  current row, the arrow keys move the selection and typing selects a row by its name. A row
  reads as its name and its second line; its disc is decorative.
- "Share My Location" is a toggle button whose `aria-pressed` says whether the location is
  shared.
- The map is an image whose text alternative says that it is a placeholder, and names the map
  type, the zoom and the heading, so a change of any of them can be read.
- What the map does is reported in a `status`: the row that was selected with its second
  line, "Current Location", the change of sharing, and what the Legal link says. While it has
  nothing to say the status is empty and without its plate, but it stays in the page, so that
  its first message is announced.
- The map types are a `radiogroup` named "Map type". The groups of map controls are named "Map
  options", "Map controls" and "Compass and zoom", and their buttons "Show Your Current
  Location", "Face north", "3D" (disabled), "Zoom out" and "Zoom in". A zoom button at its
  limit stays reachable.
- The map has no keyboard commands of its own for panning: the rows, the location button and
  the zoom pair move it.
- The placeholder drawings are decorative; the elements around them name what they stand for.

### Known differences

- **The switch's titles and shadow.** People and Devices is the library's
  [Catalyst Segmented Control](/components/catalyst-segmented-control), measured on these
  captures: its titles are 10.5 pt Medium in the fallback font off Apple devices, and the
  pill's shadow is fitted to the Aqua capture.
- **The sidebar is flat.** The captures' sidebar is a translucent material that lets a little
  of what is behind the window through; here it is the colour it has in the captures.
- **The row's selection.** Catalyst lists select in grey; the library's list selects in the
  accent colour while it has the keyboard. No capture shows a selected row.
- **The zoom pair** is 56 by 20 pt in the library, as measured in Maps; Find My's is 54 by
  19 pt. The demo keeps the pair's trailing edge and the location button where the captures
  have them, so the pair's leading edge and its divider stand 1 to 2 pt further left.
- **The compass** is the library's original drawing: its ticks, the gap about the 3D button
  and, in Dark Aqua, its face (a few levels lighter than the captures' translucent one)
  differ from Find My's.
- **The map about the controls.** `controls-*` leaves 2 pt of the map around each control and
  the corners of the compass's square unmasked: the captures have a park, a road and water
  there, the demo its flat land. That and the two items above are most of its scores.
- **Text** is set in the fallback font off Apple devices: the difference between the sidebar
  comparisons with and without their text.
- **Not in a screenshot (provisional):** the Devices list, its rows and their markers; a
  selected row and the name under its marker; sharing, its halo and the row's second line
  while it is on; the hybrid and satellite views; a map that is not north up; the zoom
  levels; the map's note; what the Legal link does; the minimum size; and the Aqua window when
  it is not key or the Dark Aqua one when it is.
