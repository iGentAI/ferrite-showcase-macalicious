<Example name="basic" />

Click a picture and try Favorite and Rotate, drag the zoom slider, type "disc" in the search
field, choose Favorites under "Showing", and double-click a picture to open it: Back returns to
the album. The window opens as the reference screenshots show it, on the album "Wallpapers"
with its 17 pictures, under the title "My Albums".

**The pictures are original generated photographs.** The captures show Apple's desktop
pictures. The toolkit reproduces Catalina's chrome, not those pictures, so the demo does not
ship them and does not imitate them: each picture is an invented landscape, travel, food or
pet photograph in `art/` (a 480 px thumbnail and a 1200 px version for the picture opened on
its own), with no people in it. It keeps the name of the grey placeholder it falls back to
without art ("Disc 1", "Bands 2"). What is kept from the captures is the shape of each
thumbnail, 4:3, 16:10, 16:9 or square, so that the grid has the captured cells. As a page of
its own nothing is read from or written to a photo library.

## On the Ferrite desktop

On the desktop served by the `macalicious-desktop` Ferrite application
(`ferrite-apps/desktop`), the window the desktop opens (`DesktopPhotos` in
`photos-desktop.tsx`) is the person's own photo library, kept on Ferrite:

- **Import** with the + button in the toolbar, by dropping pictures on the pane, or by dragging
  them from a Finder window (JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP pictures; MP4, MOV and WebM movies, which this
  browser must be able to play). Each original is uploaded to the person's
  drive, into Pictures, in pieces under
  the gateway's 256 KiB request limit (`photos-drive.ts`, through the drive's helper
  `demos/desktop/desktop-files.ts`), so Finder shows it. The date taken and the camera are read
  from the picture's EXIF data in the browser (`photos-exif.ts`), and a thumbnail is made in a
  canvas before the upload.
- **Browse** the Photos row by Years, Months or Days (the toolbar's segmented control and
  Command-1 to 4; a year's or a month's title opens it in the finer view) or as All Photos, and
  Favorites, Recents (added in the last 30 days), Imports, Photo Booth, Places (a map of the
  photos with a location, on OpenStreetMap tiles, or the same photos as a grid), Recently Deleted,
  Hidden when View shows it, Media Types when a kind exists (kinds a browser can't read never
  show, as an empty row does not on a Mac; the Shared rows show dimmed and say why), and the
  person's albums, folders and smart albums in the sidebar. The search field matches a photo's name, title, caption, keywords,
  place, month and year, camera, albums, and "favorite" or "edited".
- **Describe** photos in the floating Info window (Command-I): title, caption, keywords, date,
  place and camera settings, for one photo or several. The Keyword Manager (Command-K) keeps the
  list of keywords.
- **Name people.** While the page is idle, each photo's thumbnail is searched for faces with a
  model served by the desktop itself: no picture leaves it, and only the boxes go to its server.
  People and Info offer each face a name, or Not a Face. A person is the faces and photos given
  their name, since a face is not matched to another; a face under about a twentieth of a
  thumbnail's long side is not found.
- **Find what a picture shows.** While Photos is open, each thumbnail is read by a second model on
  the desktop's own assets (EfficientNet-Lite0, Apache 2.0), about a second a picture, in a worker.
  Only its words reach the server. A search finds a picture by them (beach, dog, plane), the search
  field lists them under Scenes, and six pictures of one scene on more than one day make a memory.
  It names sixteen scenes, from ImageNet's classes, not everything Photos on a Mac can name.
- **View** a photo full size with a double click or Space; Previous and Next in the toolbar,
  or the arrow keys, step through what the pane lists, and Back, Space or Escape returns. Z zooms,
  Option-S shows a strip of thumbnails, F opens the full-screen viewer, and a slideshow plays
  from View or the header.
- **Edit** without changing the original, with Return on a selection: Adjust (Auto Enhance, and
  the Light and Color sliders, Black & White, Definition, Sharpen, Noise Reduction and Vignette),
  Filters (Vivid, Dramatic, Mono,
  Silvertone, Noir and their warm and cool kinds) and Crop (dragging the corners, an aspect,
  Straighten, Rotate and Flip). An edit is kept as a recipe and drawn from the original in a
  canvas (`photos-edit.ts`, computed on the pixels so every engine draws it alike); Revert to
  Original forgets it.
- **Favorite**, **Rotate**, **Hide**, **Delete** (to Recently Deleted, where a photo stays 30
  days and can be recovered or, after a confirmation, deleted immediately; in an album, Delete
  only takes it out of the album), **albums**, **folders** and **smart albums** (from the File
  menu, the sidebar's row menu or New Album; dragging photos onto a row adds them, from another
  album it moves them and with Option adds them), **Export** to the drive or as a download, with
  a format and a size, **Share**, and **Set
  Desktop Picture**, from the Share menu or a right click. Set Desktop Picture draws the photo with
  its edit and hands it to the desktop with the ticket of the person's choice
  (`takePictureTicket` in `demos/desktop/desktop-personal.ts`); the desktop's own settings keep
  the choice, and Photos keeps no picture of its own.

The library's records, thumbnails, edits and albums are kept per person by the module
`ferrite-apps/desktop/src/apps/photos` (`photos-library.ts` is the page's side of it). Photo
Booth's pictures are added to the same library.

## What it shows

- A [Window](/components/window) whose [Title Bar](/components/title-bar) hides its title
  strip: the window controls and the [Toolbar](/components/toolbar) share one 38 pt row, and
  the title is centred over it.
- The toolbar: [Toolbar Buttons](/components/toolbar-button) for Back, Favorite and Rotate,
  the last two unavailable until a picture is selected; a small [Slider](/components/slider)
  between two glyphs; Info, which opens a [Popover](/components/popover); the
  [Share Menu](/components/share-menu); and a
  [Toolbar Search Field](/components/toolbar-search-field).
- A [Split View](/components/split-view) of the [Sidebar](/components/sidebar) and the
  pictures. The sidebar has four sections, and two outline rows with their
  [disclosure triangles](/components/disclosure-triangle): My Albums, expanded, and My
  Projects, collapsed. The selection is the grey of a sidebar that does not have the keyboard.
- The album's header: [Labels](/components/label) for the date range and the count, two
  [Borderless Buttons](/components/borderless-button) in the link colour, and a
  [Pull-Down Button](/components/pull-down-button) without a bezel whose menu holds
  [radio items](/components/menu-item). In a pane too narrow for one line, as in the
  narrowest window with the widest sidebar, the row under the title wraps and the header
  grows to hold it.
- The grid: an [Icon View](/components/icon-view) with 128 pt cells, multiple selection,
  the arrow keys and type-select. An open picture is an [Image View](/components/image-view),
  and a source with nothing in it an [Empty State](/components/empty-state).

| --- | --- | --- |

| Zoom | the slider | Cells of 64 to 256 pt; 128 pt, as captured, in the middle. |
| Search | the search field | Lists the pictures whose title, keywords or month and year start with every word typed. |
| Showing | the header | All Items, Favorites, Edited, Photos or Videos. |

| Info, Share | the toolbar | Info describes the selection, or else what the pane lists. Share reports the chosen service through `onShare`. |
| Show as Memory, Slideshow | the header | Shows the album under Memories; opens the pictures one after another, three seconds each. |

## Source walkthrough

The demo itself is six files in `demos/photos/`; the files of the Ferrite desktop's library
(`photos-desktop.tsx`, `photos-editor.tsx`, `photos-served-data.ts`, `photos-library.ts`,
`photos-drive.ts`, `photos-edit.ts` and `photos-exif.ts`) are described
under [On the Ferrite desktop](#on-the-ferrite-desktop).

**`photos-data.ts`** is the library. A `Photo` has a title, a date, a pixel size, a kind and
the albums it is in; a `PhotosSource` is a row of the sidebar and says what the pane shows
for it. `photosOf(source, photos, memory)` returns the pictures of a source, `matches` and
`passes` are the search and the Showing menu, `dateRange` and `countText` write the header's
line, and `fitted` gives a thumbnail its size in a square cell:

```ts
fitted(photo, 128); // 4:3 -> 128 x 96, 16:10 -> 128 x 80, 16:9 -> 128 x 72, 1:1 -> 128 x 128
```

**`photos-artwork.tsx`** holds every drawing: the placeholder pictures, the glyphs of the
sidebar and of the toolbar, and the heart on a favorite. All are original and drawn with
`currentColor` or neutral greys.

**`photos.tsx`** is the window. Its state is the selected source, the expanded folders, the
zoom, the search text, the choice of the Showing menu, the selection, the open picture, and
the favorites and quarter turns by picture. What the pane lists is derived from it:

```tsx
const collection = useMemo(() => photosOf(sourceId, photos, memory), [sourceId, photos, memory]);
const listed = useMemo(
  () =>
    collection?.filter(
      (photo) =>
        matches(photo, query) &&
        passes(photo, filter, {
          favorite: favorites.has(photo.id),
          edited: (turns[photo.id] ?? 0) % 4 !== 0,
        }),
    ) ?? [],
  [collection, query, filter, favorites, turns],
);
// What Favorite, Rotate and Info act on: the open picture, and else the selected ones.
const current = picture ? [picture] : listed.filter((photo) => selected.has(photo.id));
```

The chrome is the title bar with its toolbar. `variant="title-hidden"` puts the window
controls and the toolbar on one row and centres the title over it; the title names where Back
goes, which for an album is its folder:

```tsx
<TitleBar variant="title-hidden" title={title} controls={<WindowControls />}>
  <Toolbar aria-label="Toolbar" style={{ "--mac-toolbar-gap": "10px" }}>
    <ToolbarButton aria-label="Back" disabled={!back} onClick={back}>
      <SystemImage name="go-back" />
    </ToolbarButton>
    <div className="mac-photos-zoom">
      <ZoomGlyph />
      <Slider aria-label="Zoom" size="small" step={5} value={zoom} onValueChange={setZoom} />
      <ZoomGlyph large />
    </div>
    <ToolbarSpace />
    {/* Info in a Popover, then the ShareMenu */}
    <ToolbarButton
      aria-label="Favorite"
      aria-pressed={allFavorite}
      disabled={current.length === 0}
      onClick={toggleFavorite}
    >
      <HeartGlyph filled={allFavorite} />
    </ToolbarButton>
    {/* Rotate */}
    <ToolbarSearchField value={query} onValueChange={setQuery} />
  </Toolbar>
</TitleBar>
```

The sidebar is controlled: its value is the source, and the folders' `expanded` state is the
window's, so that opening an album from its folder can expand the folder too.

```tsx
<Sidebar aria-label="Library" material="opaque" value={sourceId} onValueChange={choose}>
  <SidebarSection title="Albums">
    <SidebarItem
      value="my-albums"
      label="My Albums"
      icon={<SourceGlyph name="stack" />}
      expanded={expanded["my-albums"]}
      onExpandedChange={(next) => setExpanded((old) => ({ ...old, "my-albums": next }))}
    >
      <SidebarItem value="wallpapers" label="Wallpapers" icon={<PhotoArtwork photo={key} cropped />} />
    </SidebarItem>
  </SidebarSection>
</Sidebar>
```

The grid is the icon view. Its icon box is the square cell, the thumbnail is fitted into it,
and the selection ring and the badges are drawn on the thumbnail by `renderIcon`:

```tsx
<IconView
  aria-label={heading}
  count={listed.length}
  renderIcon={renderThumbnail}
  getItemLabel={(index) => listed[index].title}
  getItemId={(index) => listed[index].id}
  iconSize={size}
  cellWidth={size + 8.25} // cells of 128 pt are 144.25 pt apart in the captures
  rowHeight={size + 16}
  value={selection}
  onValueChange={setSelection}
  onItemAction={(index) => open(listed[index].id)}
/>
```

Photos shows no titles under its thumbnails, and the icon view has no cell without a label.
The demo gives the label's line no height (`font-size: 0` on the view, in `style`); the title
stays in the document as the name of its option, so assistive technology and type-select
still have it.

**`photos.css`** places the toolbar's controls where the capture has them and sets the
header's type, the thumbnail with its ring and badges, and the open picture.
**`photos.tokens.css`** holds the colours measured in the captures that no shared token has
(the pane's fill, the divider, the header's rule, the title's black and white, and the grey of
the zoom glyphs) and every length and font size of `photos.css` (see
[Styling hooks](#styling-hooks)).

**`app.ts`** is the descriptor the [Desktop](/demos/desktop) reads.

## Hosting the window

`Photos` renders the library's `Window` as its root and passes every `Window` prop to it, so
it does not assume it fills the page. Place it with `style`; a window manager gives it
`state`, the size, and eight more props that the component hands to the window controls and
the title bar. These are the props a [Managed Window](/components/managed-window)'s `render`
form supplies, so the component can be spread with them as they come.

<Example name="hosted" />

| Prop | Type | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `photos` | `Photo[]` | The library; the demo's 24 placeholders by default. |
| `defaultSource` | a source's id, such as `"photos"` or `"wallpapers"` | The sidebar row selected at first; `"wallpapers"`, as captured. |
| `defaultZoom` | `number` | The zoom slider's value at first, 0 to 100; `50`, which is the captured 128 pt. |
| `onShare` | `(service, photos) => void` | Called with the service chosen in the Share menu and the pictures to share. |
| `onClose`, `onMinimize`, `onZoom` | functions | Passed to the `WindowControls`. |
| `zoomRole`, `minimizeDisabled`, `zoomDisabled` | as on `WindowControls` | Passed to the `WindowControls`. |
| `onDragRegionPointerDown`, `onDragRegionDoubleClick` | functions | Passed to the `TitleBar`: a press or a double click on the bar that is not on a control. |
| `width`, `height` | `number \| string` | The frame; 1200 by 720 by default, as captured. |
| the rest | `WindowProps` | `state`, `fullScreen`, `style`, `className`, `ref`, pointer events and so on. |

`app.ts` exports the descriptor a desktop page reads, as `photosApp` and as `app`: `id`,
`name`, `component`, `defaultSize` (1200 by 720), `minSize` (760 by 360), `resizable` and
`menus`, the menu titles after the Apple menu.

## Accessibility

- The window is a `region` named by its title.
- The toolbar is one tab stop; the arrow keys move between its buttons and the search field.
  The buttons show a glyph only and are named "Back", "Info", "Share", "Favorite" and
  "Rotate"; one that has nothing to do is marked unavailable (`aria-disabled`) and stays
  reachable. Favorite reports with `aria-pressed` whether the selected pictures are all
  favorites.
- The zoom slider is a `slider` named "Zoom" and a tab stop of its own: the arrow keys change
  it by a step, Page Up and Page Down by more, Home and End go to its ends.
- The search field is a `searchbox` named "Search"; its clear button is named "Clear".
- The sidebar is a `tree` named "Library" with a `group` for each section and `aria-expanded`
  on the two folders. The arrow keys move the selection, Right and Left Arrow expand and
  collapse, and typing selects by the start of a label.
- The album's name is the pane's `h1`. "Showing" is a button named with its choice ("Showing:
  All Items") that opens a `menu` of `menuitemradio`s.
- The grid is a `listbox` named after the album, with `aria-multiselectable`; each picture is
  an `option` named by its title, and its drawing is hidden. Whether a picture is a favorite
  is told by the Favorite button once the picture is selected, not by its option.
- An open picture is an `img` named by its title. Opening one moves the keyboard to Back,
  because the grid that had it is gone; Back returns it to the grid. A picture that closes
  for another reason, a source chosen in the sidebar or a search, leaves the keyboard where
  it is.
- "No Results" and the other texts of an empty list are a `status`, so they are announced.
- The divider is a `separator` named "Resize the sidebar" that the arrow keys move.

### Placeholders

None of these is Apple's artwork, and none is fitted to what the captures show:

- **The 24 pictures**, 17 of them in the album: original generated photographs, none drawn
  after a captured picture. Without its art a picture is a flat grey with one of six shapes
  (a disc, two bands, a triangle, a ring, a diagonal, two squares).
- **The album's thumbnail** in the sidebar, and the album's and the project's tile in their
  folder: the first picture of each, cropped square.
- **The glyphs of the sidebar**: Photos, Memories, People, Places, Recents, Imports, Activity
  and the stack of My Albums and My Projects.
- **The glyphs of the toolbar**: the two picture frames at the ends of the zoom slider, the
  heart of Favorite and Rotate; and the three service glyphs of the Share menu, the heart
  on a favorite's thumbnail and the length on a video's.

The chevron of Back, Info's mark (the [System Image](/components/system-image)
`info-outline`), the Share button's glyph, the search field's magnifier, the disclosure
triangles and the chevron of the Showing menu are the library's own drawings.

### Masks

Masks are in points from the window's top-left corner; the three crops use the same
rectangles from their own corner.

- **Pictures, in every window comparison: 15 rectangles.** Two for each of the seven columns
  of the grid, 130 pt wide from x 193, 337, 481, 626, 770, 914 and 1058, and one of 20 by
  20 pt over the album's thumbnail in the sidebar at x 33, y 330 (it is in `sidebar-*` too).
  Each grid rectangle is the union of where the capture has a picture and where the demo has
  its placeholder: the first row y 121 to 233 in the first column and y 129 to 225 in the
  others; below it y 237 to 509 in the first column, y 237 to 537 in the second and third,
  y 233 to 369 in the fourth and y 237 to 365 in the last three. The two differ in height
  because of the rows (below), so these masks also hide up to 8 pt of the pane's fill above
  and below a picture. They are 31% of the window.
- **Placeholder glyphs, in every window comparison: 14.** The two zoom glyphs and the glyphs
  of Info, Favorite and Rotate in the toolbar, and the icon of nine rows of the sidebar
  (x 17 to 39). Info's mask stays from the time its glyph was a stand-in; the library's
  `info-outline` is compared with the Reminders captures on the System Image page. The
  whole-window comparisons mask only these; the `-layout` ones mask them
  inside the larger rectangles below.
- **Text and stand-in glyphs of the toolbar, in `window-*-layout` and `toolbar-*`: 9.** The

### Known differences

- **The rows of the grid.** In Photos a row is as tall as its tallest thumbnail and the rows
  are 16 pt apart, so the captured rows start at y 121, 233 and 329. The
  [Icon View](/components/icon-view) lays every row out at one height, the square cell and
  16 pt: the demo's rows of cells start at y 121, 265 and 409, each thumbnail centred in its
  cell. The first row's pictures are 16 pt lower than the captured ones, the second row's
  56 pt and the third row's 80 pt. The columns are the captured ones. The library has no
  thumbnail grid with rows of their own height, and the demo does not make one privately.
- **No titles under the pictures.** As captured; but the icon view always has a label, so its
  line is given no height here (see the walkthrough). A view with titles shown, which Photos
  offers under View, Metadata, is not built.
- **The sidebar's icon inset.** Photos keeps its icons 18 pt from the edge although it has
  outline rows; the library's sidebar moves the icons of an outline to 24 pt, as Disk Utility
  has them. The demo's drawings hang 6 pt to the left of their icon box and the label gap is
  set to follow, which puts icons and labels where the capture has them; the box itself, and
  with it the room between a triangle and its icon, is the library's.
- **The sidebar's material** is the library's opaque one, a level darker than the capture in
  Aqua (`#e7e8e8` where Photos has `#e8e9e9`) and a level of blue darker in Dark Aqua
  (`#2c2d2f` for `#2c2d30`); the selection over it follows.
- **The toolbar's fill** is the library's title bar, two levels lighter than the capture at
  its lower edge in Aqua and about six levels lighter in Dark Aqua, which is the mean Δ of
  `toolbar-dark`.
- **The chevron of the Showing menu** is the library's for a pull-down button without a
  bezel, which is smaller and lighter than the captured one, 9 pt wide.
- **Text.** Set in the fallback font off Apple devices, which is nearly all of the
  `window-light` and `window-dark` scores. The 28 pt bold title is 5% wider in it.
- **Glyphs.** Stand-in drawings, listed above.
- **Not in a screenshot (provisional):** the ring of a selected picture and the heart of a
  favorite, the open picture, a folder's tiles, the Info popover, the Share menu's services,
  the choices of the Showing menu, the search, the pictures of every source but the album,
  the range of the zoom slider, the slideshow and the memory, the minimum size, and the
  window when it is not key.
- **Not built:** Years, Months and Days, editing, people and places, shared albums, dragging
  to select or to an album, and the header scrolling away with the pictures: here it stays
  above the grid.
