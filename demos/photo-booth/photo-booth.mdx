<Example name="basic" />

Page through the effects with the dots or the arrows and choose a preview: the window turns to
the camera view with that effect. Take Photo counts down from three and adds a picture to the
strip; Effects returns to the browser. As a page of its own (here, and on the static desktop) no
camera is used and nothing is recorded: the picture is an original generated photograph of an
empty, lamp-lit living room, and an effect is a CSS filter or transform applied to it. On the
desktop served by the `macalicious-desktop` Ferrite application (`ferrite-apps/desktop`), the
previews and the camera view show the browser's camera instead, once the person allows it, and
every picture taken is saved, with its effect drawn in, to the person's Photos library: the
original goes to Pictures/Photo Booth on the person's drive, and Photos lists it under Photo
Booth. The strip shows those pictures; a click on one shows it large from its original, until
Back to Camera or Escape. Delete or Backspace on one moves it to Photos' Recently
Deleted. Edit > Undo and Redo take back and bring back a delete, a flip, a change of effect, a
Cut and a Paste, as long as the window stays open. Copy puts the chosen pictures on the desktop's
clipboard, and the first still also on the browser's as a PNG, for other programs; Paste brings
in either.

## What it shows

- A [Window](/components/window) of 720 by 562 pt at its smallest, as captured, with a plain 22 pt
  [Title Bar](/components/title-bar) and the [Window Controls](/components/window-controls).
- The stage, 480 pt high in the measured dark grey that both appearances share. In the
  Effects browser it holds nine [Image Buttons](/components/image-button), 236 by 156 pt and
  3 pt apart, each with a preview of the placeholder under its effect and the effect's name
  over it. The button's clear space is taken away through `--mac-image-button-padding`; the
  name's 18 pt white text is the demo's own.
- In the camera view the stage holds the picture in an [Image View](/components/image-view),
  the countdown's figure over it, and every picture taken as a scrolling list of small image
  views.
- The bar, 60 pt in the measured colour of each appearance: a
  [Page Control](/components/page-control) in its `pager` variant on the window's centre, or
  the shutter in the camera view, and the Effects [Push Button](/components/push-button) at
  the right.

The countdown is one timer for each count: an effect that depends on the count sets the next
one, and at the last it takes the picture through a ref, so that the picture is taken with
the pictures and the effect of that moment. Leaving the count clears the timer, which is all
Cancel and Escape have to do.

## Hosting the window

`PhotoBooth` renders the library's `Window` as its root and passes every prop it does not use
to it, so a window manager can place and activate it.

| Prop | Type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `defaultView` | `"effects" \| "camera"` | What the window shows when it opens. Default: the Effects browser, as captured. |
| `defaultPage` | `number` | The browser's page at first, from 0. Default: 1, the captured page. |
| `defaultEffect` | `string` | The id of the effect chosen at first. Default: `"normal"`. |
| `countdownStepDuration` | `number` | Milliseconds each of the three counts lasts. Default: 1000. |
| `onEffectChange` | `(effect: string) => void` | Called with the id of an effect when one is chosen. |
| `onPictureTaken` | `(picture) => void` | Called with `{ number, effect }` when a picture is taken. |
| `pictures` | `PhotoBoothPicture[]` | The strip's pictures, when the owner keeps them (each may carry an `id` and an image `src`). Without it the window keeps its own. |
| `source` | `(effect) => ReactNode` | What a preview and the camera view show under an effect, such as a camera's video. Default: the placeholder picture. |
| `takeDisabled` | `boolean` | Disables the shutter, as when there is no camera. |
| `notice` | `ReactNode` | A line over the stage, such as why no picture can be taken. |
| `onDeletePictures` | `(pictures) => void` | Makes each picture of the strip a button; Delete or Backspace on it calls this, with the selection when it is in one. |
| `onPicturePointerDown` | `(event, picture) => void` | Called when a picture of the strip is pressed, which may start a drag of it. |
| `shareMenu` | `ReactNode` | The Share button, in the bar left of Effects. |
| `readPicture` | `(picture) => Promise<Blob>` | Reads a picture at full size, to show it large when it is clicked, and again when its `rev` changes. Without it a click shows the strip's image. |
| `onClose`, `onMinimize`, `onZoom` | functions | Passed to the `WindowControls`. |
| `zoomRole`, `minimizeDisabled`, `zoomDisabled` | as on `WindowControls` | Passed to the `WindowControls`. |
| `onDragRegionPointerDown`, `onDragRegionDoubleClick` | functions | Passed to the `TitleBar`: a press or a double click on the bar that is not on a control. |
| `width`, `height` | `number \| string` | The frame; 720 by 562 by default, as captured. The previews share the stage, so they grow with it. |
| the rest | `WindowProps` | `state`, `fullScreen`, `style`, `className`, `ref`, pointer events and so on. |

`app.ts` exports the descriptor a desktop page reads, as `photoBoothApp` and as `app`: `id`,
`name`, `component`, `defaultSize` and `minSize` (both 720 by 562), `resizable` (true, for full screen) and
`menus`, the menu titles after the Apple menu. The [desktop demo](/demos/desktop) finds it
there and lists Photo Booth among its applications.

## Accessibility

- The window is a `region` named by its title.
- The browser is a `group` named with its page, such as “Effects, page 2 of 3”. Each preview
  is a button named by its effect; the chosen one has `aria-current`. The picture in a
  preview is decoration. Choosing one moves the keyboard focus to the shutter.
- The page control is a `group` named “Effects pages”: the dots are one tab stop, the arrow
  keys, Home and End move between them, and the current dot has `aria-current="page"`.
- The camera's picture is an image named as a placeholder with its effect. A picture in the
  strip is an image named with its number and effect, in a list named “Photos”.
- The countdown's figure is decoration: each count, the picture that is taken, a cancelled
  countdown and a chosen effect are announced in a `status` region. While it counts, the
  shutter is a Cancel button, Escape cancels too, and Effects is disabled.

## Fidelity

`photo-booth.visual.json` compares the window's chrome with the two Photo Booth screenshots.

**Not compared: the nine previews**, 236 by 156 pt each at x 3, 242 and 481 and y 25, 184
and 343, with the names over them. In the captures they show the camera through nine filters;
the demo shows its own picture of an empty room in their place, which takes
nothing from those pictures, so there is nothing to compare. They are four fifths of the
window, more than a comparison may mask, so instead of the whole window with the previews
masked the strips of chrome around them are compared, with nothing masked: the top 25 pt (the
title bar and the stage's top edge) and the bottom 63 pt (the stage's bottom edge and the
bar). `stage-geometry-*` then holds the stage to its 720 by 480 pt and to the stage's colour
in the eight gaps around and between the previews. No budget was raised for the artwork.

Measured in the screenshots and reproduced: the 720 by 562 pt frame; the stage's colour and
its 480 pt; the nine 236 by 156 pt cells with their 3 pt gaps; the bar's colour in both
appearances; the page control on the window's centre, 20 pt under the bar's top, with its
triangles, its three dots at a 36 pt pitch and the blue of the current one (the Page
Control's own measurements, compared with three pages as the captures have them, where the
app has a fourth, of backdrops); and the Effects button, 95 pt wide and 19.5 pt from the right
edge.

What differs, and why:

- **Artwork.** The previews are the camera's picture; an abstract placeholder stands in its
  place and is not compared.
- **The title bar.** Photo Booth's bar meets the stage with no line under it and is a little
  darker than the standard bar (`#dfdfdf` to `#c9c9ca`). The library's title bar is used as
  it is, with its shadow row and separator and its own gradient, which is nearly all of the
  two title bar scores.
- **The effects.** Photo Booth filters the camera with Core Image. Here, on the placeholder, an
  effect is a CSS filter or transform, which only suggests it. On the Ferrite-served desktop the
  camera's frames of the first two pages are computed on their pixels; the numbers were chosen by eye,
  as no capture shows those effects on a known picture, and the distortions are centred on the first
  face found, or on the frame without one. The names of the first and third pages are not in a capture,
  and the third page's art and warps are made here, not taken from Apple's.
- **Faces.** The detector's short-range model finds faces near a camera, within about two metres,
  and takes the whole frame, so a small face in the distance gets no art.
- **The shutter.** Photo Booth draws a large round red shutter; the library has no such
  control, and the camera view uses a push button titled Take Photo.
- **Text.** Set in the fallback font off Apple devices.
- **Not in a screenshot (provisional):** the camera view with its countdown and strip, the
  other two pages of effects, and the window when it is not key.
- **Not built:** the camera itself (except on the Ferrite-served desktop, where the browser's
  camera is shown and the effect is drawn into the saved picture; a blur there is a box blur, an
  approximation of the CSS one), the backdrops and the effects that follow a face.
