<Example name="basic" />

Choose an entry of the table of contents, or a topic under its triangle; the page follows, and
Back and Forward walk the pages you opened. Type in the search field for the topics that
mention a word, and choose one to read it. Nothing is fetched: the guide's text is the demo's
own, short and generic, and the pictures the real guide shows (a round photograph, an illustration
of a laptop and the Finder's icon before each search result) are replaced by pictures of the
served desktop's own windows and its Finder icon. A guide's page and its first topic show a picture
of the application. A reload brings the first screenshot's state back.

## What it shows

- A [Window](/components/window) of the `panel` variant, which casts the smaller shadow of a
  floating panel, with a [Title Bar](/components/title-bar) in the `utility` layout: a 16 pt
  title strip with the small [Window Controls](/components/window-controls) and, on the same
  surface, a [Toolbar](/components/toolbar), 49 pt together. The bar is the window's drag
  region. The frame's corners are rounded by 3 pt, as measured, through the window's radius
  token set on this window.
- The toolbar: Back and Forward as a
  [Toolbar Segmented Control](/components/toolbar-segmented-control) of the `navigation`
  variant with momentary segments, a [Toolbar Button](/components/toolbar-button) for the table
  of contents, a [Share Menu](/components/share-menu) on a second one, and a
  [Toolbar Search Field](/components/toolbar-search-field) that a flexible space keeps at the
  trailing edge. Every glyph is a [System Image](/components/system-image): `go-back`,
  `go-forward`, `sidebar`, `share` and, on the pages, `chevron-down`.
- The body: a [Split View](/components/split-view) of the table of contents, 203 pt wide, and
  the page. The first pane is `collapsible` and controlled: hidden, it has a size of 0 and
  leaves its divider at the window's edge, which is what the Dark Aqua capture shows. The
  divider takes its measured colour through the split view's `--mac-split-view-divider-color`.
- The table of contents: an [Outline View](/components/outline-view) with 26 pt rows. An entry
  is an item whose children are its topics; the outline is controlled, so the demo keeps the
  expanded entries and selects the row of the page that is shown.
- The page: a [Scroll View](/components/scroll-view) around one of three pages. The welcome
  page carries `data-appearance="light"`, because the real one has no dark style and is white
  in Dark Aqua too. Its rule is a [Separator](/components/separator) and its link a
  [Label](/components/label) rendered as a button, in the colours measured on that page, which
  the demo sets as the two shared tokens on the page's element.
- The results: a list of links, each with a title and a description as
  [Labels](/components/label), the count as a small label, "Show all" as a label rendered as a
  button, a [Separator](/components/separator), and an [Empty State](/components/empty-state)
  when nothing is found. A result is highlighted under the pointer and with keyboard focus.
- The data is in `help-viewer-data.ts`: the entries with their topics, each topic's summary
  and text, and `searchHelp`, which lists the topics whose title or summary contains the text,
  the ranked ones first. The history is a list of pages and searches in the component's state;
  served by the desktop, it and the open rows of the contents are kept in
  `help-viewer-kept.ts`, a Book pop-up in the toolbar goes to a server's book, and a pop-up over
  the results limits them to one book. None of these appears on the unserved page.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Back, Forward | Go one step back or forward in the history of pages and searches. Each is unavailable at its end of the history. |
| The sidebar button | Hides the table of contents, or shows it again. Unavailable while search results are shown. |
| Share | Opens a menu of four services. Choosing one closes the menu: nothing is sent anywhere. Unavailable while search results are shown. |
| Search | Shows "All Help" with the number of topics that mention the text and the first six of them. More text narrows the list; clearing the field, with Escape or its clear button too, returns to the page the search started from. |
| An entry of the table of contents | Shows the entry's own page, which lists its topics as links. Its triangle, or Right Arrow, shows its topics in the outline. |
| A topic | Shows the topic's page. |
| The divider | Drag it to resize the table of contents between 140 and 320 pt. A double click, or Return, hides the list and shows it again. |
| "Table of Contents" on the welcome page | Shows or hides the table of contents, like the sidebar button. |
| A search result | Opens its topic as a new step of the history; Back returns to the results. |
| Show all | Lists every result. |

The component takes three props for the state it starts in, so that an example can show each
capture: `defaultSidebarOpen`, `defaultQuery` and `searchRanking`, the ids of the topics a
search lists first. The two search captures list different topics for "File";
`lightRanking` and `darkRanking` in the data file hold the two orders.

## Hosting the window

<Example name="hosted" />

`HelpViewer` renders the library's `Window` as its root and passes every `Window` prop to it,
so it does not assume it fills the page. Place it with `style`; a window manager gives it
`state`, the size, and eight more props that the component hands to the window controls and to
the title bar, which with its toolbar is this window's drag region. These are the props a
[Managed Window](/components/managed-window)'s `render` form supplies, so the component can be
spread with them as they come.

```tsx
import { ManagedWindow } from "macalicious/managed-window";
import { app } from "./app";

<ManagedWindow
  id={app.id}
  title={app.name}
  resizable={app.resizable}
  minWidth={app.minSize.width}
  minHeight={app.minSize.height}
  defaultFrame={{ x: 80, y: 60, ...app.defaultSize }}
  render={(props) => <app.component {...props} />}
/>;
```

| Prop | Type | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `defaultSidebarOpen` | `boolean` | Whether the table of contents is shown at first; `true` by default. |
| `defaultQuery` | `string` | Text the search field holds at first: the viewer opens on its results, one step after the welcome page. |
| `searchRanking` | `readonly string[]` | The ids of the topics a search lists first; the Aqua capture's order by default. |
| `onClose`, `onMinimize`, `onZoom` | functions | Passed to the `WindowControls`. |
| `zoomRole`, `minimizeDisabled`, `zoomDisabled` | as on `WindowControls` | Passed to the `WindowControls`. |
| `onDragRegionPointerDown`, `onDragRegionDoubleClick` | functions | Passed to the `TitleBar`: a press with the primary button, or a double click, on the bar that is not on a control. |
| `width`, `height` | `number \| string` | The frame; 877 by 722 by default, as captured. |
| `state` | `"key" \| "main" \| "inactive"` | The window's state. |
| the rest | `WindowProps` | `fullScreen`, `style`, `className`, `ref`, pointer and focus events and so on. |

`app.ts` exports the descriptor a desktop page reads, as `helpViewerApp` and as `app`: `id`,
`name`, `component`, `defaultSize` (877 by 722), `minSize` (540 by 300, at which the toolbar's
items fit and the page keeps its smallest width beside the table of contents as it opens),
`resizable` and `menus`, the menu titles after the Apple menu.

At other sizes the table of contents keeps its width and the page takes the rest; the welcome
page's margins grow with the pane, and the search field gives up width before the buttons do.

On the desktop, the Edit menu holds the rows from Undo to Select All: they act on the search or find
field that has the keyboard, and where no field has it, Copy takes the text selected on the page and
Select All selects the page. What cannot act is dimmed and says why.

## Accessibility

- The window is a `region` named by its title, "macOS User Guide". The toolbar is one tab
  stop; the arrow keys move between Back, Forward, the two buttons and the search field.
- The toolbar's buttons show only a glyph and are named with `aria-label`. Back and Forward
  are in a group named "History". The sidebar button's name says what it will do: "Hide Table
  of Contents" or "Show Table of Contents". An item that is unavailable stays reachable in the
  toolbar and says so with `aria-disabled`.
- The table of contents is a `treegrid` named "Table of Contents" and one tab stop. An entry
  with topics has `aria-expanded`; Right Arrow expands it, Left Arrow collapses it or goes to
  the entry, and typing selects by title. Moving the selection opens the page of the row.
- The divider is a focusable separator named "Resize the table of contents"; the arrow keys
  resize the list and Return hides it or shows it again.
- Each page is an `article`, or for the results a `region`, named by its heading, the page's
  only `h1`. The count of results is a `status`, so a change of it is announced while typing.
- A result is a link named by its title and its description. It is highlighted under the
  pointer and with keyboard focus, where it also has the focus ring. "Show all" and the
  welcome page's "Table of Contents" are buttons; the latter has `aria-expanded`.
- The round picture and the icons are decorative and hidden from assistive technology; a page's
  picture and the welcome page's say what they show in their `alt` text.
- The welcome page's link is `#007aff` on white as captured, a contrast of 4.0 to 1, which is
  under the 4.5 to 1 that text of its size asks for.
- A title of the table of contents that does not fit its row is cut with an ellipsis and has
  no tooltip; its page's heading has it in full.

## Fidelity

`help-viewer.visual.json` compares the demo with the four Help screenshots, the whole capture
with the panel's shadow each time: the welcome page with the table of contents in Aqua
(`welcome-light`), the welcome page without it in Dark Aqua (`welcome-dark`), and the results
of a search for "File" in both appearances (`search-light`, `search-dark`). The comparisons use
real input for what the captures show: a click on the selected row gives the table of contents
its keyboard focus, a click in the search field gives it its focus ring, and in Aqua the
pointer is put over the first result, which highlights its row.

**Every comparison masks the artwork, whole.** The round picture above the guide's title, the
illustration under the rule and the icon before each of the six results are not reproduced:
the demo has a flat disc, a flat panel and a flat square there, each with the same generic
mark, and nothing in them follows what the captures show. Scoring them would measure a
difference that is intended, so their boxes are left out of every score.

Each capture is compared twice: once with only the artwork masked, and once more
(`*-layout-*`, at the stricter threshold of 0.05) with the text and the glyphs masked too: the
window's title, the four toolbar glyphs, the search field's magnifier and text, the titles of
the table of contents, and every line of text on the page. What those score is the frame and
its shadow, the bar and the bezels of its toolbar, the focused search field, the selected row,
the disclosure triangles, the divider, the page's fills, the highlighted result and the rules.

Scores from `npm run visual -- help-viewer/` (Chromium on Linux, fallback font), share of
pixels that differ and mean colour distance out of 255:

| Comparison | 1x | 2x |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `welcome-light` | 3.58%, 4.94 | 2.95%, 5.16 |
| `welcome-layout-light` | 0.30%, 0.39 | 0.07%, 0.46 |
| `welcome-dark` | 1.97%, 3.10 | 1.61%, 3.26 |
| `welcome-layout-dark` | 0.12%, 0.47 | 0.00%, 0.53 |
| `search-light` | 4.98%, 6.19 | 4.23%, 6.40 |
| `search-layout-light` | 0.27%, 0.22 | 0.05%, 0.25 |
| `search-dark` | 4.55%, 5.38 | 3.82%, 5.42 |

The budgets in `help-viewer.visual.json` are set just above these. The whole-window scores are
nearly all text; with it masked the four windows differ in 0.00 to 0.07% of their pixels at 2x.

Taken from the spec's measurements of the screenshots and laid out to them: the 877 by 722 pt
frame, its 3 pt corners and its 49 pt bar with 11 pt window controls; Back and Forward 25 pt
each from 8 pt, the two 37 pt buttons from 67 and 112 pt and the 323 pt search field 8 pt from
the trailing edge; the 203 pt table of contents with 26 pt rows from the bar down, triangles
8 pt and titles 20 pt from the edge; the 286 pt title block with its gradient from white to
`#f2f2f2`, the title's capitals from 189 pt and the subtitle's from 234 pt under the bar; the
rule 328 pt under the bar and 20 pt from the pane's edges (34 pt without the table of
contents); the illustration's box, 387 by 222 pt, from 365 pt; "All Help" 21 pt from the edge,
result rows of 56 pt at a pitch of 60 pt from 55 pt under the bar, 2 pt from the window's
edges, with the title 46 pt from the edge and the description 22.5 pt under it; the rule 20 pt
from the edges. The colours that are the app's own are in `help-viewer.tokens.css`, each with
the place it was measured.

What differs, and why:

- **The pictures.** Placeholders, as said above, and masked.
- **Two-line titles in the table of contents.** The capture wraps three titles ("Use Apple
  devices together", "Music, books, news, and more", "Resources for your Apple devices") onto
  two lines in rows of 42 pt. The library's outline view has one row height, so the demo
  keeps them on one 26 pt row, cut with an ellipsis where they do not fit, and the rows after
  them stand 16, 32 and 48 pt higher than in the capture. The layout comparison scores the
  three triangles that are out of place because of it.
- **Disclosure triangles.** In the capture's 26 pt rows the triangles are centred in the row;
  the library's outline view centres them in the highlight, which is the row less its last
  point, so they stand 1 pt higher.
- **Toolbar in Dark Aqua.** The library's bar and bezels are a few levels more neutral than
  this capture's: the bar `#39393a` where it has `#3a3c3d`, a bezel `#646465` where it has
  `#67696a`.
- **Focus ring of the search field.** In the Aqua capture the bezel's edge shows through the
  ring as a darker line (`#6e98dd` along the top, `#5b84ca` along the bottom); the library's
  ring is one colour, `#7aa3e9`.
- **Shadow.** The Aqua capture has a darker pixel directly outside the frame (`#d9d9d9` where
  the library's panel shadow gives `#e1e1e1`).
- **Glyphs.** The library's sidebar image is 15 by 11 pt where the capture's is 15 by 12, and
  its share image stands three quarters of a point lower. The glyph before "Table of Contents"
  on the welcome page is the sidebar image too, where the capture has a list in a frame. The
  arrow after "See more results on the web" is an original drawing.
- **Text.** Set in the fallback font off Apple devices, so lines end a few points from where
  the screenshots end them. The second result's description wraps as captured.
- **The count.** 558 is the number both captures give for "File". The demo reaches it with a
  real index: the guide's own 27 topics, of which 8 mention the word, and 550 invented topics
  of other applications' guides, one per application and command. "Show all" lists them.
- **"See more results on the web"** is text in the link colour, not a link: the demo opens no
  web page.
- **Not in a screenshot (provisional):** the table of contents in Dark Aqua and with an entry
  expanded; the divider of a hidden table of contents in Aqua; every page other than the
  welcome page and the results (a topic's and an entry's page, whose layout and dark fill
  follow the results page); the highlight of a result in Dark Aqua; the full list after "Show
  all" and a search that finds nothing; the welcome page's margins at widths other than the two
  captured, which are interpolated; the Share menu and its services; the window when it is not
  key and every size other than the captured one.
- **Not built:** floating above other windows. The served desktop has a guide for each
  application but About This Mac, Force Quit and Help Viewer, and books for macOS Help, What’s New
  and Tips; an application's Help row opens its guide, or else a search.
