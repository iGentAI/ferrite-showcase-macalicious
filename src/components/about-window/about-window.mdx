About This Mac is System Information's window: an `NSWindow` whose title bar holds a segmented
control in place of a title, over panes for Overview, Displays, Storage, Support and Service.
`AboutWindow` assembles the library's [Window](/components/window),
[Title Bar](/components/title-bar) and [Window Controls](/components/window-controls) that way,
and its parts lay out the Overview pane. The tabs are built on Base UI's
[Tabs](https://base-ui.com/react/components/tabs) and keep its anatomy: `AboutWindow` is the
root, `AboutWindowTabs` the list, `AboutWindowTab` a tab and `AboutWindowPanel` a panel.

## Usage

```tsx
import {
  AboutWindow,
  AboutWindowActions,
  AboutWindowDetail,
  AboutWindowDetails,
  AboutWindowFooter,
  AboutWindowLink,
  AboutWindowOverview,
  AboutWindowPanel,
  AboutWindowTab,
  AboutWindowTabs,
  AboutWindowTitle,
  AboutWindowVersion,
} from "macalicious/about-window";
import { PushButton } from "macalicious/push-button";
import "macalicious/styles.css";

<AboutWindow
  title="About This Mac"
  onClose={close}
  tabs={
    <AboutWindowTabs aria-label="Sections">
      <AboutWindowTab value="overview">Overview</AboutWindowTab>
      <AboutWindowTab value="storage">Storage</AboutWindowTab>
    </AboutWindowTabs>
  }
>
  <AboutWindowPanel value="overview">
    <AboutWindowOverview image={<img src={badge} alt="" width={153} height={153} />}>
      <AboutWindowTitle emphasized="macOS">Catalina</AboutWindowTitle>
      <AboutWindowVersion label="Version">10.15</AboutWindowVersion>
      <AboutWindowDetails>
        <AboutWindowDetail label="MacBook Pro (15-inch, 2019)" />
        <AboutWindowDetail label="Memory">16 GB 2400 MHz DDR4</AboutWindowDetail>
      </AboutWindowDetails>
      <AboutWindowActions>
        <PushButton size="small">System Report…</PushButton>
      </AboutWindowActions>
    </AboutWindowOverview>
    <AboutWindowFooter>
      © 2019 Example Inc. <AboutWindowLink href="/license">License Agreement</AboutWindowLink>
    </AboutWindowFooter>
  </AboutWindowPanel>
  <AboutWindowPanel value="storage">…</AboutWindowPanel>
</AboutWindow>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add about-window`.

- `tabs` takes an `AboutWindowTabs` element, which the window centres in its title bar. The
  first tab that is not disabled is selected unless `defaultValue` or `value` says otherwise;
  pass `value` and `onValueChange` to control the pane. The window reads the tabs from the
  element, fragments included, so that a server-rendered window arrives with its pane. A
  component of yours that renders the tabs itself cannot be read that way: give such a window
  a `defaultValue`.
- With tabs the bar shows no title, and `title` only names the window for assistive
  technology. Without `tabs` the window has the plain title bar and shows the title.
- `AboutWindowOverview` puts its `image` at the left and its children in the text column:
  `AboutWindowTitle` (26 pt, light, with an optional bold beginning), `AboutWindowVersion`,
  `AboutWindowDetails` with one `AboutWindowDetail` per row, and `AboutWindowActions` for small
  [Push Buttons](/components/push-button). `AboutWindowFooter` is the legal text under it, and
  `AboutWindowLink` a link in that text.
- The library ships no artwork. Supply the picture yourself: an
  [Image View](/components/image-view), an `<img>` or an inline SVG, up to 153 pt.
- The window does not open, move or close itself: a window manager gives it `state` and a
  position through `style`, moves it from `onDragRegionPointerDown` (a press on the title bar,
  not on a window control or a tab), and unmounts it on `onClose`.

## Guidelines

From the Human Interface Guidelines for macOS,
[Menu Bar Menus](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503145009/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/menus/menu-bar-menus/)
and
[Window Anatomy](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503145009/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/windows-and-views/window-anatomy/):

- Open the About window from the first item of the app menu, "About" followed by the app's
  name, separated from the items after it. Use the same short app name there as in the menu's
  title.
- Put the version and the copyright in the About window. A version number does not belong in
  the app's menus or in its name.
- A window may leave out its title where the context makes one unnecessary, and a window
  control may be disabled individually: About This Mac shows tabs in place of a title and
  disables the zoom button.
- When controls share the bar that moves the window, leave enough of it free to drag the
  window without activating them.

## Sizes

An About window is not resizable. `width` is 586 pt unless given, the width of About This Mac;
the height follows the content, or `height`. With six rows of details the window comes to the
353 pt of the capture. Measured from the window's top-left corner: title bar 38 pt; tab control
18 pt high (19 pt with its border), its tabs 10 pt wider than their titles on either side;
picture 153 pt at 51, 109; text column at 255; baselines of the title at 102, of the version at
118 and of the details at 150 and every 19 pt after; button bezels 17 pt high, 11 pt apart;
baseline of the legal text 8 pt above the bottom edge. The tab titles, the version and the
details are 11 pt, the title 26 pt and the legal text 10 pt.

## Accessibility

- The window is a `region`. With tabs it is named by `title` through `aria-label`, since the
  bar shows no title: always pass one, or an `aria-label` of your own. Without tabs it is
  named by the visible title.
- The tab control is a `tablist` of `tab` buttons, each linked to its `tabpanel`. Give the
  control an `aria-label`. It is one tab stop: the left and right arrow keys move between the
  tabs, wrapping round, and select as they move; Home and End go to the first and last tab.
  With `activateOnFocus={false}` the arrow keys only move the focus and Space or Return
  selects.
- Control-Tab and Control-Shift-Tab select the next and the previous tab while the focus is
  in the tab control, as in AppKit. A browser keeps those keys for its own tabs, so the page
  only receives them in an installed web app or an embedded web view.
- The pane is in the tab order after the control, as Base UI makes it, and shows a focus ring
  inside its edge when it is reached with the keyboard.
- `AboutWindowTitle` is an `<h1>`: the heading of the window. The details are a list; each
  row reads as its label followed by its value. The picture is decoration next to that text:
  give it an empty `alt`, or `aria-hidden` for an inline SVG.
- `AboutWindowLink` has the colour of the legal text around it, as in Catalina, so it is told
  apart only by its wording and by the underline under the pointer: word it as an action or a
  document ("License Agreement").
- Command-W is a window manager's key; the window only reports its close button through
  `onClose`.

## Visual comparison

`about-window.visual.json` compares the whole window with the About This Mac capture in both
appearances. The capture is a full screen, so the window is cropped at its frame: the shadow is
not compared, and the four corners, where the desktop picture shows, are masked. The macOS
Catalina badge is Apple's artwork: a plain ring stands in for it and every comparison masks
it, with the serial number, which only the dark capture shows. The `-layout` twins also mask
the tab titles, the title, the version, the details, the button titles and the legal text,
which are set in the fallback font off Apple platforms, and score the frame, the title bar,
the tab control and the buttons at the tighter threshold (0.05).

Scores from `npm run visual -- about-window` in Chromium on Linux with the fallback font: the
share of pixels that differ by more than the comparison's threshold, and the mean colour
difference out of 255.

| Comparison | Reference (crop 1094, 386, 1172 x 706) | Mismatch 1x | Mean Δ 1x | Mismatch 2x | Mean Δ 2x |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `about-this-mac-light` | Light-About-This-Mac | 6.00% | 6.73 | 4.86% | 6.37 |
| `about-this-mac-light-layout` (masked) | Light-About-This-Mac | 1.16% | 0.51 | 0.02% | 0.11 |
| `about-this-mac-dark` | Dark-About-This-Mac | 5.97% | 6.28 | 4.88% | 5.92 |
| `about-this-mac-dark-layout` (masked) | Dark-About-This-Mac | 0.73% | 0.74 | 0.01% | 0.34 |

The unmasked scores are text: the title is San Francisco's display cut at 26 pt, which the
fallback font sets about a tenth wider. At 1x the masked score is the hairlines of the tab
control and the buttons, which a 1x screen cannot draw at half a point. The dark capture's
window background is tinted by the desktop picture (`#303032`); the example sets that colour,
and its title bar is up to three levels lighter than the library's for the same reason.

State checks with real input, since no screenshot shows these: `tab-click-*` clicks another
tab and reads the selected and unselected fills; `tab-arrow-key-*` moves with the right arrow
key and reads the selection and the focus ring; `inactive-*` reads the control in an inactive
window.

Measured: the title bar and the place of the window controls; the tab control's size, fills,
borders, dividers and title colours in both appearances; the picture's place and size; the
text column, the sizes and weights of its text and every baseline; the buttons' place and
spacing; the legal text's size, colour and baseline. Provisional: a pressed tab, a disabled
tab, the focus rings, the control in a window that is not key, the underline of a link under
the pointer, and the forced-colours look. Not reproduced: the panes other than Overview, which
the reference does not show, and the standard About panel of an application. In the capture the
legal text sits 5.5 pt to the left of the window's centre; the component centres it, and the
comparison's example adds the padding that moves it.
