AppKit's `NSComboBox` placed in a toolbar or a format bar: the textured rounded bezel of a
[toolbar button](/components/toolbar-button) holding a text field and, after a hairline, a
button that opens a list of suggested values. It is the [combo box](/components/combo-box) in
another bezel: built on Base UI's
[Autocomplete](https://base-ui.com/react/components/autocomplete) in the same way, with the
same props except `size`, the same items and the same list. The value is the text in the
field: an item is
a suggestion, and text that is not in the list is as valid as text that is. Unlike the combo
box of a window's content, the button has no accent colour.

## Usage

```tsx
import { ComboBoxItem, ComboBoxSeparator } from "macalicious/combo-box";
import { ToolbarComboBox } from "macalicious/toolbar-combo-box";
import "macalicious/styles.css";

<ToolbarComboBox size="small" aria-label="Font size" value={size} onValueChange={setSize}>
  <ComboBoxItem>9</ComboBoxItem>
  <ComboBoxItem>12</ComboBoxItem>
  <ComboBoxSeparator />
  <ComboBoxItem value="288">Poster</ComboBoxItem>
</ToolbarComboBox>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add toolbar-combo-box`.

- The children are `ComboBoxItem` and `ComboBoxSeparator` elements from
  `macalicious/combo-box`. Choosing an item puts its `value` in the field; without `value`
  its children are used, when they are a string or a number.
- `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange` hold the text; `open`, `defaultOpen` and
  `onOpenChange` the list. `className`, `style` and `ref` go to the root element, which draws
  the bezel; `inputRef` reaches the `<input>`; every other prop, `id`, `aria-label` and
  `placeholder` included, goes to the `<input>`.
- `mode` says what typing and the arrow keys do, as on the combo box. The default,
  `"inline"`, is AppKit's: the list always shows every item, opens only with the button or
  Down Arrow, and moving through it puts the highlighted item in the field.
- The control has a width of its own, 60 pt, or 43 pt at the small size; set another with
  `style`. Its box is the bezel's fill, as a toolbar button's: Aqua's hairline border is drawn
  outside it, so TextEdit's control measures 44 x 16 pt over its border and is 43 x 15 pt here.
- Inside a [`Toolbar`](/components/toolbar) the field is one of the toolbar's items: the
  arrow keys reach it, and inside the text they move the insertion point.

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines for
[combo boxes](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503142537/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/fields-and-labels/combo-boxes/)
and
[toolbars](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503121036/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/windows-and-views/toolbars/):

- Show a meaningful default value from the list in the field, not an empty field.
- Keep the list to relevant choices, and no item wider than the field: a longer one is cut
  off in the field.
- A typed value is not added to the list. Add it yourself if people expect to find it there
  again.
- A toolbar holds the commands people use most; every one of them should also be in a menu,
  because a toolbar can be hidden or customised.

## Variants and states

Focus and the pressed button are transient, so this gallery pins them. In an application they
appear on their own.

<Example name="states" both hideCode />

| State | How it is triggered | Look | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | – | The toolbar bezel under the field and the button, a 1 pt divider (black at 10%), a chevron in black at 65% (white at 85% in Dark Aqua), text in the control text colour | measured (TextEdit, small size) |
| Main window, not key | an ancestor has `data-window-state="main"`, as a window that shows a sheet | As normal: bezel, divider, chevron and text unchanged. No focus ring | the look measured (TextEdit under its Print sheet, small size); that the ring is absent provisional |
| Focused | the field has the keyboard | Focus ring around the whole control | provisional |
| Button pressed | `:active` on the button | The button's part of the bezel takes the toolbar button's pressed fill | provisional |
| List open | button, Down Arrow, `open` | Button as pressed; the list is the combo box's, 3 pt under the control | provisional |
| Disabled | `disabled` | Text and chevron in the toolbar button's disabled ink; the bezel stays | provisional |
| Read-only | `readOnly` | As enabled; the text can be selected, not changed | provisional |
| Inactive window | an ancestor has `data-window-state="inactive"` | The toolbar button's inactive bezel and ink; no focus ring | provisional |

The window state is not a prop: see [Building a window](/building-a-window). The list is
rendered in a portal and counts as a key window of its own.

## Sizes

<Example name="sizes" both />

| `size` | Fill | Button | Text | List text | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `regular` | 22 pt high, 60 pt wide | 18 pt | 13 pt | 13 pt | provisional |
| `small` | 15 pt high, 43 pt wide | 18 pt | 9 pt | 12 pt | measured, but for the list |

The sizes are a toolbar's, not the three control sizes: `regular` matches the other toolbar
controls, which are 22 pt high, and `small` is the control of TextEdit's format bar. There is
no `"mini"`, so this is the one prop of the [combo box](/components/combo-box) that does not
carry over unchanged: a combo box with `size="mini"` has no toolbar counterpart. No
screenshot shows the regular one.

`.mac-menu`.

## Accessibility

- The field is a native `<input>` with the `combobox` role, `aria-expanded`,
  `aria-controls` and `aria-autocomplete` (the value of `mode`); the list is a `listbox` of
  `option` elements. The highlighted item is announced through `aria-activedescendant`, and
  the keyboard focus stays in the field.
- Keys: typing edits the field; Down Arrow opens the list; the arrows move through it; Return
  puts the highlighted item in the field; Escape closes the list and brings the typed text
  back. In a toolbar, Left and Right Arrow move to the neighbouring item once the insertion
  point is at that end of the text.
- The control has no visible label. Name the input with `aria-label`, or with a label
  element and `id`.
- The button is not in the tab order, as in AppKit: the field's Down Arrow does what it does.
  It is a real `<button>` for the pointer and for assistive technology, named by
  `buttonLabel`; translate that string with the rest of the interface.
- `disabled` uses the real attribute on the input and on the button, so the control leaves the
  tab order.
- The focus ring shows whenever the field has the keyboard, for pointer focus too, and only
  in the key window.
- In forced-colours mode the bezel's edge, the divider and the chevron use `ButtonText`, the
  text `FieldText`, a disabled control `GrayText` and the ring `Highlight`.

## Visual comparison

`npm run visual -- toolbar-combo-box` compares the small size with the font size control of
TextEdit's format bar in both appearances, in the two states the screenshots show: in the key
window (reference crop `544,126,98,40` of the two TextEdit screenshots, 2x pixels) and in a
window that is main and not key, under its Print sheet (crop `869,158,98,40` of the Aqua Print
screenshot and `783,128,98,40` of the Dark Aqua one). Share of pixels that differ, in Chromium
on Linux with the fallback font:

| Comparison | Aqua, 2x | Aqua, 1x | Dark Aqua, 2x | Dark Aqua, 1x |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `textedit`, whole crop | 1.76% | 13.37% | 1.79% | 8.88% |
| `textedit-drawing`, text masked | 0.81% | 13.88% | 0.88% | 13.25% |
| `print-sheet`, whole crop | 1.79% | 13.47% | 1.84% | 8.98% |
| `print-sheet-drawing`, text masked | 0.91% | 13.75% | 0.88% | 13.50% |

The masked comparisons use a threshold of 0.05 and gate the drawing; each has a budget
0.05 percentage points above its score. What the whole crop adds is the text, set in the
fallback font instead of San Francisco. What remains with the text masked is the ends of the
chevron's arms, which Catalina cuts a little differently, and single pixels on the corners.
There is no 1x reference, so the 2x screenshot is averaged down, and a half-point hairline
that the browser must draw as a full pixel cannot match its averaged counterpart
([Fidelity limits](/fidelity)).

Measured from those crops: the 43 x 15 pt fill and Aqua's border around it, the fill's
gradient in both appearances (in Dark Aqua a little lighter than the toolbar button's, which
is measured on Finder), the 24 pt field, the 1 pt divider and its colour, the 18 pt button,
the chevron's shape, place and colour, and the 9 pt text with its colour and baseline. Under
the Print sheet the fill, the border, the divider, the chevron and the text read the same
values as in the key window, in both appearances: the control at rest does not change when
its window becomes main and stops being key. An inactive window is another state, which no
screenshot shows. The two Dictation screenshots show the control a third time, at rest in a
key window with the same values; they are not compared.

The regular size and the states no screenshot shows are checked pixel by pixel against the
spec's values (the `expect` comparisons of `toolbar-combo-box.visual.json`):

| State | What is checked | Source of the values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular size, at rest | The box of three controls; the fill at mid-height, the divider 18 pt before the trailing edge, the chevron's ink | Fill, divider and chevron measured on the small size; that the regular size keeps them inferred |
| Button pressed | The toolbar button's pressed fill on the button, the resting fill on the field | Inferred |
| Disabled | The chevron in the toolbar button's disabled ink | Inferred |

These checks hold the component to the spec; where the spec infers a value, they do not show
that Catalina looks that way.

### What no screenshot shows

Six screenshots show this control, all at the small size and all with the value 12: the two of
the TextEdit window, the two of its Print sheet and the two of Dictation. The record of the
review of all 294 screenshots lists no other combo box in a toolbar bezel: the others it lists
(Go to Folder, Connect to Server, Date & Time, Font Book) are the combo box of a window's
content. So two states are
measured, at rest in the key window and at rest in a main window, and everything else on this
page is provisional because no screenshot shows it:

- the regular size, in every state;
- the focus ring, and that it is absent in a window that is not key;
- the pressed button;
- the open list, which is the combo box's and provisional there too;
- the disabled control, and a read-only one;
- the control in an inactive window;
- placeholder text, and an empty field.

The corner radius is the toolbar button's and was not fitted on this control. The small size
sets its text in the 9 pt system font, which is what the digits in the screenshots measure;
the list of the small size uses 12 pt text.
