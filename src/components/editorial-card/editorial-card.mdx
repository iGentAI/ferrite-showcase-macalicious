<Example name="basic" both />

The App Store's Discover page opens with a hero, a photograph as wide as the page with a
headline over it, and continues with editorial cards: tinted panels with an eyebrow, a title,
a sentence or two and a round picture. TV opens its tv+ section with a hero too, raised from
the page like its other cards. All are drawn by the apps (collection view items; no AppKit
class and no system card style), and Base UI has no primitive for them: `EditorialCard` is a
styled element whose `render` prop, through Base UI's `useRender`, makes it a link or a button.
The smaller cards under a hero belong to [Card Grid](/components/card-grid).

## Usage

```tsx
import { EditorialCard } from "macalicious/editorial-card";
import "macalicious/editorial-card.css";

<EditorialCard
  render={<a href="/story/act-fast" />}
  eyebrow="Collection"
  title="Act Fast!"
  headingLevel={3}
  artwork={<img src={story.art} alt="" style={{ width: "100%", height: "100%", objectFit: "cover" }} />}
>
  These games push your reaction time to the limit.
</EditorialCard>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add editorial-card`. See
[Installation](/installation).

A card is as wide as its container. The App Store sets two panels side by side with a 20 pt
gap, which a [Card Grid](/components/card-grid) with `columns={2}` does. The library ships no
artwork: the examples on this page draw gradients; supply your own images.

## Guidelines

The Human Interface Guidelines have no page for these cards. What the two apps do:

- One hero at the top of a page, and nothing else beside it.
- Keep the eyebrow to a word or two that says what kind of story this is, and the title to
  two lines.
- Put the text of a hero where its picture is calm: it lies over the picture's leading top
  corner and has no backdrop of its own.
- A card is one target: make the whole card the link or the button.

## Variants and states

<Example name="hero" both />

| Variant | From | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `panel` | App Store, editorial cards | Fill `#f7f7f8`, Dark Aqua `#343538`; corners of 8 pt; no border, no shadow; text 32.5 pt in; a 170 pt disc of artwork 15 pt from the trailing edge |
| `hero` | App Store, Discover | The artwork fills the card; corners of 6 pt; no border, no shadow; text 27.5 pt in, in colours that do not change with the appearance, because it lies on the picture |
| `hero` with `raised` | TV, tv+ | Corners of 12px (see Visual comparison), a hairline on every side and the drop shadow of TV's cards; 1214 : 455 |

The states apply when the card is a link or a button. Pressed and keyboard-focused are
transient, so this gallery pins them.

<Example name="states" both hideCode />

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | – | As above |
| Hover | – | No change |
| Pressed | `:active` on a link or button card | The card dimmed by a tenth |
| Keyboard focus | `:focus-visible` | Focus ring around the card |
| Disabled | the card is a disabled button, or has `aria-disabled="true"` | Half opacity. The look only: with `aria-disabled` the card still receives its events, so leave out its handler or its `href` as well |
| Window not key | an ancestor has `data-window-state="main"` or `"inactive"` | No focus ring; nothing else changes |

Measured, in both appearances: the panel's size, fill, corners, the positions, sizes and
colours of its three texts and its disc; the App Store hero's width, bottom corners and the
positions, sizes and colours of its eyebrow and title; TV's hero with its corner, hairline and
shadow. Provisional, because no screenshot shows them: the pressed and disabled looks and the

## Sizes

One size, set by the container.

| Variant | Width | Height |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `panel` | its container's; 462 pt in the App Store | at least 230 pt |
| `hero` | its container's; 945 pt in the App Store | from `--mac-editorial-card-aspect`, 945 : 340 by default; or give it a `height` |
| `hero` with `raised` | its container's; 1214 pt in TV | 1214 : 455 by default |

In a panel the eyebrow is 11 pt Semibold capitals with its cap top 28 pt below the card's
top, the title 24 pt with its cap top at 45.5 pt, and the body 13 pt on a 19 pt pitch from
83.5 pt. A hero's text is placed from its bottom edge, the only edge the capture shows: the
eyebrow's cap top is 289 pt above it, and the title is 24 pt on a 28 pt pitch from 258 pt
above it, in a column of at most 290 pt. In a hero of 340 pt, the part the capture shows,
that is 51 and 82 pt below the top; a taller hero has the difference above its text, and a
shorter one keeps the text 51 pt below its top and cuts it off at the bottom.

## API

<PropsTable name="EditorialCardProps" />

The remaining props are those of a `<div>`, or of the element given to Base UI's `render`
prop, and go to that element with `ref`.

| Attribute | On | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `data-variant` | card | `panel`, `hero` | Reflects `variant`. |
| `data-raised` | card | present or absent | Reflects `raised` on a hero. |
| `data-force` | card | `pressed`, `focus` | Documentation and visual tests only: pins a transient state. |

Classes: `.mac-editorial-card`, `.mac-editorial-card-text` (with `-eyebrow`, `-title` and
`-body` inside) and `.mac-editorial-card-artwork`.

| CSS variable | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--mac-editorial-card-aspect` | `945 / 340`; raised `1214 / 455` | Proportions of a hero. |
| `--mac-editorial-card-radius` | `8px`; hero `6px`; raised `12px` | Corner radius. |
| `--mac-editorial-card-fill` | `#f7f7f8`, `#343538` in Dark Aqua | Fill of a panel. |
| `--mac-editorial-card-title`, `--mac-editorial-card-secondary` | `#262626` and `#7c7c7c`; `#e0e0e1` and `#a3a4a5` in Dark Aqua | Title, and eyebrow and body, of a panel. |
| `--mac-editorial-card-hero-title`, `--mac-editorial-card-hero-secondary` | `#303033` and `#878785` | The same of a hero. Set them light for a dark picture. |
| `--mac-editorial-card-raised-edge`, `--mac-editorial-card-raised-shadow` | a grey at 20%; `0 4px 7px` black at 25% | Hairline and shadow of a raised hero. |
| `--mac-editorial-card-placeholder` | `#e9e9eb`, `#48494d` in Dark Aqua | Behind artwork that is missing or transparent. |

## Accessibility

- A card is static unless you render it as a link or a button with `render`. Then it is one
  tab stop, Return (and Space, for a button) activates it, and it is named by its text:
  eyebrow, title and body, in that order. A card without text needs an `aria-label`.
- The title is plain text unless `headingLevel` is given, which makes it a heading of that
  level (`role="heading"` with `aria-level`), so that a page of stories can be read by its
  headings. It is a `<span>` and not an `<h3>` so that the card stays valid as a link or a
  button. Leave `headingLevel` out of a card that is a button: a button's content is read as
  its name, not as structure.
- Leave the `alt` of the artwork empty when the text already says what the story is.
- The text of a hero lies on the picture with no backdrop. Check its contrast against your
  picture and set the two hero text tokens accordingly; the measured greys suit a light sky.
- The focus ring is drawn only in a key window. In forced-colours mode the card has a
  border, one point wide, in the system's text colour.

## Visual comparison

`npm run visual -- editorial-card` compares four examples with the App Store and TV captures
in both appearances. Apple's artwork is masked or replaced by flat colour. Share of pixels
that differ, in Chromium on Linux with the fallback font:

| Comparison | Aqua 2x | Aqua 1x | Dark Aqua 2x | Dark Aqua 1x |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Panel, "Act Fast!" (App Store `634,796,924,460`), the disc's square masked | 2.48% | 2.96% | 2.53% | 2.90% |
| The same, text masked too | 0.00% | 0.00% | 0.00% | 0.00% |
| Hero, bottom-left corner (App Store `622,732,40,36`) | 2.99% | 2.50% | 4.44% | 1.94% |
| Hero, eyebrow and title (App Store `660,160,480,180`), the cloud behind them replaced by a gradient | 14.58% | 15.93% | 12.33% | 13.92% |
| The same, text masked | 3.86% | 3.75% | 1.45% | 1.00% |
| Raised hero, bottom-left corner (TV `180,1596,48,48`), nothing masked | 1.43% | 2.78% | 0.65% | 1.22% |

The first row scores the three texts in the fallback font; with them masked the panel's fill,
edges and corners match the capture. Its fixture breaks the body's line where the capture
does, because the fallback font sets the line shorter and would fit another word. The App Store hero's corner is compared over water in the photograph, which the fixture
replaces by its average colour, so that row scores the photograph's texture as well as the
corner. The hero's eyebrow and title lie on cloud, which the fixture draws as a gradient
through the cloud's colours: with the text masked that row scores the stand-in, and without
it the text, whose lines stand where the capture's do and which the fallback font sets wider
at 24 pt. TV's corner is a continuous curve, which `border-radius` cannot draw: 12px matches the
pixels best (11px, the spec's circular fit, scores 2.00% and 1.35% at 2x), and what is left
lies along the curve. The raised hero has a hairline along its bottom edge, which TV's
smaller cards do not.

No screenshot shows a pressed or focused card or a window that is not key. Those states are
checked in a real browser (the `expect` comparisons): a panel that is a button keeps the
panel's layout, its disc round and its title at the top; pressed, the fill is at 90%
brightness; Tab puts the focus ring around it in a key window and none in another.

Not built: the round play button TV shows over a card under the pointer. No screenshot shows
it and the spec infers only that it exists, with no size, position or colours to build from.
The hero's paging is a component of its own, [Shelf Pager](/components/shelf-pager).
