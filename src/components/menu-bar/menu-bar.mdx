<Example name="basic" both />

AppKit's main menu (`NSApplication.mainMenu`) in the system menu bar. The titles are a
[Base UI Menubar](https://base-ui.com/react/components/menubar), which supplies the roles,
the arrow-key navigation between titles and the switching of menus while one is open. Each
menu is a [`Menu`](/components/menu) whose popup is attached under the bar; the trailing end
holds the [menu bar extras](/components/menu-bar-extra).

The example shows the bar in both appearances. A menu opened from it renders in a portal and
takes the appearance of the page, not of the bar it hangs from.

## Usage

```tsx
import { MenuBar, MenuBarMenu } from "macalicious/menu-bar";
import { MenuItem, MenuSeparator } from "macalicious/menu-item";
import "macalicious/menu.css";
import "macalicious/menu-item.css";
import "macalicious/menu-bar.css";

<MenuBar>
  <MenuBarMenu title={<AppMark />} aria-label="System">
    <MenuItem>About This Computer</MenuItem>
  </MenuBarMenu>
  <MenuBarMenu title="Notes" application>
    <MenuItem keyEquivalent="command+,">Preferences…</MenuItem>
    <MenuSeparator />
    <MenuItem keyEquivalent="command+q" onClick={quit}>Quit Notes</MenuItem>
  </MenuBarMenu>
  <MenuBarMenu title="File">
    <MenuItem keyEquivalent="command+n" onClick={newNote}>New Note</MenuItem>
  </MenuBarMenu>
</MenuBar>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add menu-bar menu-item`.

`MenuBarMenu` assembles one menu: a `Menu`, its title cell and a `MenuPopup attached`. The
first menu after the system menu is the application menu; mark it with `application`, which
sets its title in bold. For a title that needs more than `MenuBarMenu` offers, build the menu
from the parts: a `Menu` with a `MenuBarTitle` and a `MenuPopup attached`.

The bar does not position itself. On a desktop page, fix it to the top
(`style={{ position: "fixed", inset: "0 0 auto", zIndex: 1 }}`); the material then shows a
blurred copy of what is behind it. The mark of the Apple menu is Apple's and is not included:
pass an image of your own as the title, with an `aria-label`.

A menu's items do not exist while it is closed, so key equivalents are not handled by the bar:
listen for them with `onMenuKeyEquivalents` ([Menu Item](/components/menu-item)).

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines,
[Menu Bar Menus](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503110758/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/menus/menu-bar-menus/):

- Keep the standard menus in their standard order: the application menu, File, Edit, Format,
  View, application-specific menus, Window, Help.
- Use one-word titles where possible, in title case.
- Keep a menu's title enabled even when all of its items are unavailable, so that people can
  still open it and see what it holds.
- Every command of the application should be in the menu bar; toolbars and contextual menus
  repeat commands, they do not replace the bar.

## Variants and states

| Part or state | Look | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bar | 22 pt strip of the menu bar material, with a 1 pt line under it (black at 16%; 36% in Dark Aqua) | Measured |
| Title cell | The text and 10 pt on each side; cells abut; the first starts 10 pt from the edge | Measured |
| Titles | 14 pt, the application's name bold; black at 85% in Aqua, white in Dark Aqua | Measured, computed |
| Menu open | The cell is filled with the accent's highlight from top to bottom, square, and its title is white | Measured, per accent |
| Keyboard focus | The same highlight | Provisional |
| Menu disabled | The title in the disabled menu item colour | Provisional |
| Window not key | Unchanged: the bar belongs to the screen, not to a window | Catalog |

The highlight is a table of its own (`--mac-menu-bar-highlight`), fitted for each of the eight
accents to the open application title of the sixteen accent screenshots.

Auto-hiding the bar in full screen and dimming it while another display has the focus are not
reproduced.

## Sizes

The menu bar has one size: 22 pt high, with the 14 pt menu bar font.

## Accessibility

- The titles are a `menubar` of `menuitem`s with `aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded`.
  One title is in the tab order; Left and Right Arrow move between titles and wrap, and Down
  Arrow, Return or Space opens the menu. In an open menu, Left and Right Arrow go to the
  neighbouring menu, and Escape closes it and returns focus to its title.
- While a menu is open, moving the pointer to another title switches to its menu, as in
  macOS. Hovering does not open a menu while none is open.
- A title that is an image needs an `aria-label`. Give the bar an `aria-label` when the page
  has more than one.
- An open menu is modal, as in AppKit: the page behind it does not scroll or take clicks.
  `modal={false}` switches that off.
- macOS moves focus to the menu bar with Control-F2. The bar adds no global shortcut; bind one
  yourself if your page needs it.
- With `prefers-reduced-transparency` the material is opaque; under `forced-colors` the bar
  has a solid lower border and the open title uses the system highlight colours.

## Visual comparison

`npm run visual -- menu-bar/` compares the left end of Finder's menu bar with the Desktop
screenshots, the bar with the Apple menu open with the Apple-Menu screenshots, and the open
application title with the sixteen accent screenshots (`menu-bar.visual.json`). Scores at 2x,
in Chromium on Linux with the fallback font:

| Crop | Aqua, whole crop | Aqua, drawing only | Dark Aqua, whole crop | Dark Aqua, drawing only |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bar, no menu open: pixels that differ | 15.69% | 1.68% | 16.74% | 1.70% |
| Bar, no menu open: mean Δ of 255 | 24.60 | 5.75 | 26.94 | 3.93 |
| Apple menu open: pixels that differ | 15.68% | 5.85% | 16.74% | 1.48% |
| Apple menu open: mean Δ of 255 | 24.04 | 5.79 | 26.69 | 4.04 |
| Open title, eight accents: pixels that differ | | 4.45% | | 0.00% |
| Open title, eight accents: mean Δ of 255 | | 2.05 to 4.32 | | 1.59 to 2.41 |

"Drawing only" masks the mark and the titles and counts a pixel as differing at a colour
distance of 0.05, half the default. The bar is a thin crop that is mostly text, so the
whole-crop scores are dominated by the titles, set in the fallback font instead of San
Francisco, and by the stand-in for the Apple mark.

The switching of menus is checked with real input as well: with the Finder menu open the
pointer moves to the File title, and chosen pixels must show File highlighted and Finder not.

What is not exact:

- **The desktop picture.** The bar is compared over the colour of the sky just under it, a
  stand-in for Apple's picture, which is not shipped.
- **The material.** Over that sky the shared menu bar material comes out about 4 levels off
  the capture in each channel ([Fidelity limits](/fidelity)).
- **Vibrant titles.** Over the sky Catalina draws the titles navy; here they are black at 85%.
- **The line under an open menu's title in Aqua.** At 2x the attached menu's first half point
  differs from the capture under the open title, which is the 4.45% of the accent comparisons.

Provisional: keyboard focus, the disabled title, and a custom accent, which uses the shared
menu selection colour.
