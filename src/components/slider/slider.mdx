AppKit calls this control a *slider* (`NSSlider`, linear type). It is built on
[Base UI's Slider](https://base-ui.com/react/components/slider) and assembled from its parts
(Root, Control, Track, Indicator, Thumb), which supply the `<input type="range">` semantics,
keyboard handling, dragging, form submission and right-to-left support. `Slider` is that
assembly as one component, because a Catalina slider is always put together the same way; it
holds one value.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Slider } from "macalicious/slider";
import "macalicious/slider.css";

<Slider aria-label="Volume" value={volume} onValueChange={setVolume} />
<Slider aria-label="Tracking speed" tickMarks={10} tickMarkValuesOnly max={9} defaultValue={3} />
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add slider`. See
[Installation](/installation).

A slider is as long as the space it is given, like a block element; set a `width` on it or on
its container. Its height is fixed: 15 pt, or 20 pt with tick marks.

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines for sliders, as the measured spec summarises them.
Not verified: this text was not checked against Apple's page.

- Without tick marks the thumb is round and the track is filled with colour from the minimum
  to the thumb. With tick marks the thumb points at them and the track is not tinted.
- Give a slider an introductory label, in title-style capitalisation and ending in a colon,
  and consider icons or labels at the minimum and the maximum. Labelling only those two tick
  marks is usually enough.
- Use tick marks when people choose among distinct stops, and let the value snap to them
  (`tickMarkValuesOnly`) when nothing in between makes sense.
- Never reverse the direction: the minimum is on the leading side, or at the bottom.
- Give live feedback while the thumb is dragged where that is cheap (`onValueChange`), and
  act on release where it is not (`onValueCommitted`).

## Variants and states

Pressed and keyboard-focused are transient, so this gallery pins them. In an application they
appear on their own.

<Example name="states" both hideCode />

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | – | Round white knob (light grey in Dark Aqua); track filled with the accent colour up to it |
| Tick marks | `tickMarks` | Pointed thumb, marks beyond the track, no fill |
| Pressed | while the thumb is held or dragged | The thumb darkens (lightens in Dark Aqua) |
| Keyboard focus | `:focus-visible` on the thumb's input | Focus ring in the accent colour, following the thumb's shape |
| Disabled | `disabled` | The whole control at half opacity; the filled part drawn as unfilled track |
| Window not key | an ancestor has `data-window-state="main"` or `"inactive"` | No accent: the filled part is drawn as unfilled track; no focus ring |

The window state is not a prop: see [Building a window](/building-a-window).

### Tick marks

`tickMarks` is the number of marks, spread evenly so that the first and the last are under the
thumb's centre at the minimum and the maximum. `tickMarkPosition="above"` puts them on the
other side and turns the thumb round.

<Example name="tick-marks" both />

### Vertical

`orientation="vertical"` turns the slider on its end, the minimum at the bottom. It takes the
height of its container, or its own. Tick marks are on the trailing side unless
`tickMarkPosition="leading"`.

| `mini` | 9 pt | 9 x 10 pt | 1 x 3 pt, 3.5 pt from the track | 9 pt, 12 pt | provisional |

The track is 3 pt thick in every size. The element's box runs from the top of the thumb to
the bottom of the tick marks and is as long as the track; the thumb's hairline and shadow, and
the focus ring, are drawn outside it, so leave 3 pt around a slider.

## Accent colour

The filled part of the track follows the accent colour: set `data-accent` on any ancestor. See
[Theming](/theming). It is the colour the checkboxes of System Preferences › General are
filled with, which is what the Displays pane shows for blue. No screenshot shows a slider in
another accent, so the other seven are inferred from the checkboxes.

## API

<PropsTable name="SliderProps" />

Props not listed go to the root element. `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby`, `aria-describedby`,
`aria-valuetext`, `getAriaValueText` and `inputRef` are handed to the thumb, whose input is the
element with the `slider` role; `aria-labelledby` is also given to the root. The range variant of
Base UI's slider (several thumbs) is not offered: AppKit has none.

| Attribute | On | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `data-size` | root | `regular`, `small`, `mini` | Reflects `size`. |
| `data-tick-marks` | root | `below`, `above`, `trailing`, `leading`, or absent | Reflects `tickMarkPosition` when there are tick marks. |
| `data-orientation` | every part | `horizontal`, `vertical` | Set by Base UI. |
| `data-disabled` | every part | present or absent | Set by Base UI. |
| `data-dragging` | every part | present or absent | Set by Base UI while the thumb is dragged. |
| `data-force` | root | `pressed`, `focus` | Documentation and visual tests only: pins a transient state. |

The parts carry the classes `mac-slider-control`, `mac-slider-track`, `mac-slider-fill` (Base
UI's Indicator), `mac-slider-thumb`, `mac-slider-ticks` and `mac-slider-tick`. The slider has no
CSS variables meant for consumers.

How it is drawn, for anyone who changes the stylesheet:

- The control is padded by half a thumb at each end, and the thumb's centre moves along the
  track inside that padding, so the thumb stops flush with the ends of the visible track,
  which a pseudo-element draws half a thumb longer on each side.
- Base UI centres the thumb with a translation; the component replaces it with margins, so
  that the browser lays the thumb out on device pixels, as AppKit draws it.
- The pointed thumb is two masked layers: its fill, and behind it the same outline 0.5 pt
  further out for the hairline (3 pt further out, in the focus colour, when focused). The
  shapes are SVG mask images rather than clip-paths because a mask is laid out on device
  pixels with its box. In points, the regular thumb has top corners of radius 2, straight
  sides, an arc of radius 4.5 into a taper of 43 degrees that would leave the side 8.85 pt
  down, and a sharp tip 17 pt down; the small one has corners of 1.5, an arc of 3 from
  5.9 pt and its tip at 12.05 pt. They are original drawings fitted to the screenshots.
- Tick marks on the other side, and a vertical slider's pointed thumb, are the same drawing
  mirrored or turned, so their hairline and shadow are lit from the wrong side. No screenshot
  shows either.

## Accessibility

- The thumb holds a native `<input type="range">`, visually hidden, which is what assistive
  technology and the keyboard reach. Arrow keys change the value by `step`; Page Up, Page Down
  and Shift with an arrow by `largeStep`; Home and End go to the ends.
- A slider needs an accessible name: `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`. Every example on this
  page has one. Use `getAriaValueText` when the number alone does not say enough.
- Pressing the track moves the thumb there. A drag is followed by Base UI's own listeners,
  added only for the duration of the drag.
- A disabled slider's input is disabled and leaves the tab order.
- Tick marks are decoration (`aria-hidden`); label the stops with text if they mean something.
- In forced-colours mode the track, the fill, the tick marks and the thumb are drawn with
  system colours.
