AppKit's push button in the textured rounded bezel style, which is reserved for toolbars. It is
Base UI's [Button](https://base-ui.com/react/components/button), or its
[Toggle](https://base-ui.com/react/components/toggle) when `pressed` or `defaultPressed` is
given. Inside a [Toolbar](/components/toolbar) it joins the toolbar's arrow-key navigation; on
its own it is an ordinary button.

## Usage

```tsx
import { ToolbarButton } from "macalicious/toolbar-button";
import "macalicious/toolbar-button.css";

<ToolbarButton aria-label="Share" onClick={share}>
  {/* a 16 pt glyph drawn with currentColor */}
</ToolbarButton>
<ToolbarButton onClick={done}>Done</ToolbarButton>
```

The button is as wide as its content plus padding; set `style={{ width: 38 }}` for the fixed
widths the screenshots show (25 pt Back and Forward, 36 to 38 pt single glyphs).

## Guidelines

Not verified: this section paraphrases Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and was not checked
against Apple's text.

- A toolbar button acts immediately and does not stay highlighted. Make it a toggle only for a
  real on and off state, such as Safari's Reader button.
- Prefer a template glyph to text. Draw it with `currentColor` so it follows the state.
- Use this style only in a toolbar; in the window body use a [Push Button](/components/push-button).
- A Back and Forward pair is a [Toolbar Segmented Control](/components/toolbar-segmented-control)
  with `variant="navigation"`.

## Variants and states

<Example name="states" both hideCode />

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | – | Aqua: white-to-light-grey gradient in a half-point border, darker at the bottom, with a faint shadow row. Dark Aqua: flat grey with a lighter top row, no border |
| Pressed | `:active` | Darker in Aqua, lighter in Dark Aqua, with a darker glyph in Aqua; the border is the resting one. Measured on Safari's Share button while its menu is open |
| On | `pressed`, or a toggle that is on | Dark bezel with a white glyph in Aqua; light bezel with a dark glyph in Dark Aqua |
| On, tinted | a toggle that is on, with `pressedStyle="tint"` | The plain bezel with a blue glyph; a mark inside the glyph is white in Aqua and a hole in Dark Aqua. Grey like any glyph in an inactive window |
| Disabled | `disabled` | Dimmed glyph; the bezel stays |
| Keyboard focus | `:focus-visible` | Focus ring, in the key window only (provisional) |
| Main window | `data-window-state="main"` on an ancestor | As in the key window, without the focus ring |
| Inactive window | `data-window-state="inactive"` on an ancestor | Flat outlined bezel on the flat bar, grey glyph |

<Example name="toggle" />

### A toggle that tints its glyph

Console's Now and Info buttons keep the plain bezel while they are on and show their state in
the glyph: `pressedStyle="tint"`. A glyph that is a solid shape with a mark in it (the letter
of Info) cuts the mark out and draws it again filled with
`var(--mac-toolbar-button-glyph-mark)`, which the button sets: transparent while it is off,
and the mark's colour while it is on and tinted.

<Example name="tint" both />

### Laying buttons out by their bezels

The button's box is the bezel's fill, so a gap between two buttons is the distance between
their fills, and Aqua's hairline border is drawn in the gap. Keychain Access counts the border
as part of the button instead: its two buttons stand 8 pt apart from border to border in Aqua
and from fill to fill in Dark Aqua, which has no border. `frame="bezel"` gives a button that
box: a margin of one hairline on each side in Aqua and none in Dark Aqua, from the token
`--mac-toolbar-button-border-room`. With it the same toolbar inset and gap put the buttons
where the captures have them in both appearances. Console and Finder do not lay their buttons
out this way, which is why it is not the default.

## Sizes

One size: the button is 22 pt high, as wide as its content plus 8 pt of padding on each side,
and at least 25 pt wide. Set `style={{ width }}` for an exact width, with `minWidth` too for
one under 25 pt (Keychain Access's are 24 pt).

## API

<PropsTable name="ToolbarButtonProps" />

| Attribute or variable | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-pressed` | present or absent | Set by Base UI on a toggle that is on. |
| `data-pressed-style` | `tint` or absent | Reflects `pressedStyle="tint"`. |
| `data-frame` | `bezel` or absent | Reflects `frame="bezel"`. |
| `data-disabled` | present or absent | Set by Base UI while the button is disabled. |
| `data-force` | `pressed`, `focus` | Documentation and visual tests only. |
| `--mac-toolbar-button-glyph-mark` | a colour, set by the button | For a mark inside a solid glyph: transparent, and the mark's colour while a tinted toggle is on. |

## Accessibility

- A native `<button>`. Space and Return activate it.
- A button that shows only a glyph needs an `aria-label`, or a
  [Toolbar Labelled Item](/components/toolbar-labelled-item) around it, whose label names it.
- A toggle exposes `aria-pressed`. A tinted toggle shows its state by colour alone to the eye;
  in forced colours the glyph takes the system highlight colour.
- Inside a toolbar the button is reached with the arrow keys and the toolbar is one tab stop;
  a disabled button stays reachable there, as Base UI's toolbar keeps disabled items focusable.
  On its own, a disabled button leaves the tab order.
- The focus ring shows only in the key window.

The two `-whole` rows score the inactive crop with nothing masked, at the default threshold, so
they include the stand-in sidebar glyph; the masked rows use the stricter threshold 0.05.

The two `pressed` rows compare the upper 18 pt of the toolbar, the bar and the upper half of
the bezel: the capture is of a button whose menu is open, and the menu's shadow darkens the
capture below that. The fill's lower half is the capture's with that shadow taken out; the
[Share Menu](/components/share-menu) page compares the whole bezel with its menu open.

Measured: the 22 pt fill, the gradient stops, the three border colours and the shadow row, the
corner, the enabled, disabled, on and inactive glyph colours, the Back chevron, and the pressed
fill and glyph. Provisional: the focus ring, which no screenshot shows, and a pressed button
in a window that is not key, which keeps the pressed fill here. The dark fill differs by a few
levels between apps on a Mac (wallpaper tinting, and lighter where light content scrolls under
the bar); the Finder values are used.

The tint is read on the disc of Console's Info button (Light-/Dark-Console, x=741 to 770,
y=133 to 162: 553 pixels of `#417eee` and of `#5190f0`) and on the disc of Now. The letter in
the disc is white in Aqua; in Dark Aqua it has the bezel's colour (`#6e6f70`), a hole. The
captures are taken with the blue accent, and the tint is not the accent's blue, so the token
does not follow the accent colour; whether Console's does is not known.

The bezel frame is read on row 114 of the Keychain Access captures: the two 24 pt fills start
at x=269 and x=335 behind a border pixel at x=268 and x=334 in Aqua, and at x=268 and x=332 in
Dark Aqua; the window's edge is x=112, so the buttons start 78 pt from it and are 8 pt apart
once Aqua's border counts as part of the button. In Console the six buttons are 44 pt from
border to border in Aqua (fills from x=129 to 214) and 44 pt of fill in Dark Aqua (x=128 to
215), in the same place; neither box of this component is that one, and the Console comparison
above leaves the button's ends out.

The bezel's corner is built at 4 pt on the border's outer edge: the shared control radius plus
the hairline border. The spec's fit of the light Finder corner gives 4.25 pt. Rendered at 3.75,
4, 4.25 and 4.5 pt against the same corners, 4 and 4.25 pt tie in Aqua (rms 1.93 and 1.98
levels on the Back button's top-left corner) and 4 pt is closer in Dark Aqua (5.27 against
5.36), with 3.75 and 4.5 pt clearly worse in both, so the radius stays on the shared token. This
is the adopted value as built, not a new measurement.
