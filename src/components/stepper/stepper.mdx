AppKit calls this control a *stepper* (`NSStepper`). It shows no value of its own: it changes
a number, a date or a time that a field next to it displays.

It is not built on a Base UI primitive. The closest one, Base UI's Number Field, keeps the
keyboard handling, the focus and the `spinbutton` semantics in its text input, and its two
buttons are out of the tab order because that input is always there; a stepper has no input.
Hiding the input would leave an invisible text field that takes typed digits, and Number Field
has no wrapping (its buttons disable at the bounds) and no Page Up and Page Down. So the
stepper is written here after the WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices
[spinbutton pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/spinbutton/): one focusable
element with `role="spinbutton"` and two buttons inside it. Its prop names are Number Field's
where the concept is the same (`value`, `defaultValue`, `onValueChange`, `min`, `max`, `step`,
`largeStep`, `disabled`, `name`) and AppKit's where it is the stepper's own (`valueWraps`,
`autorepeat`).

## Usage

```tsx
import { Stepper } from "macalicious/stepper";
import "macalicious/stepper.css";

<Stepper aria-label="Copies" defaultValue={1} min={1} max={99} />
<Stepper aria-labelledby="hours-label" value={hours} onValueChange={setHours} min={0} max={23} valueWraps />
```

The stepper needs an accessible name, and it needs something that shows its value: put it
4 pt to the right of the field it changes, centred on it, and keep the two in step yourself
(`value` and `onValueChange`). Or copy the source into your project:
`npx macalicious add stepper`. See [Installation](/installation).

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines for
[steppers](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503153700/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/selectors/steppers/):

- Use a stepper to let people raise or lower a numeric, date or time value in increments.
- Place it next to a field that displays the current value, because the stepper itself
  displays none.

The page says no more than that. From AppKit's `NSStepper`: holding a half repeats the step
(`autorepeat`), and a stepper may wrap from its maximum to its minimum (`valueWraps`), which
suits cyclic values such as minutes and not quantities.

## Variants and states

A pressed half and keyboard focus are transient, so this gallery pins them. In an application
they appear on their own.

<Example name="states" both hideCode />

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | – | White bezel (translucent white in Dark Aqua), two chevrons, no divider |
| Half pressed | `:active` on the upper or the lower half | That half takes the accent gradient and a white chevron; the other half is unchanged |
| Keyboard focus | `:focus-visible` | Focus ring in the accent colour around the bezel |
| Disabled | `disabled` | Bezel at half opacity, grey chevrons |
| Window not key | an ancestor has `data-window-state="main"` or `"inactive"` | No accent: a pressed half darkens instead; no focus ring |

The pressed half follows the accent colour: set `data-accent` on any ancestor. See
[Theming](/theming). The window state is not a prop; a window component sets
`data-window-state` on itself.

## Sizes

<Example name="sizes" />

| Size | Box | Bezel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `regular` | 13 x 22 pt | 12 x 21 pt (measured) |
| `small` | 11 x 19 pt | 10 x 18 pt (provisional) |
| `mini` | 10 x 16 pt | 9 x 15 pt (provisional) |

The element's box is AppKit's alignment rectangle; the visible bezel is one hairline smaller on
every side, like a push button's. Beside the field it changes the stepper is centred with
`align-items: center`: the regular bezel is 1 pt shorter than Catalina's 23 pt date field at
the top and at the bottom.

## API

<PropsTable name="StepperProps" />

| Attribute | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-size` | `regular`, `small`, `mini` | Reflects `size`. |
| `data-disabled` | present or absent | Present while the stepper is disabled. |
| `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax` | numbers | The value and the bounds that are set. |
| `data-force` | `pressed` (the upper half), `pressed-increment`, `pressed-decrement`, `focus` | Documentation and visual tests only: pins a transient state. |

| Class | Element |
| --- | --- |
| `mac-stepper` | The root, with `role="spinbutton"`. |
| `mac-stepper-increment`, `mac-stepper-decrement` | The upper and the lower half, each a `<button>`. |
| `mac-stepper-glyph` | The chevron in a half, an inline SVG in `currentColor`. |

The stepper has no CSS variables for a consumer to set.

## Accessibility

- The root has `role="spinbutton"` with `aria-valuenow`, and `aria-valuemin` and
  `aria-valuemax` for the bounds that are set. It has no visible text, so it requires a name:
  `aria-label`, or `aria-labelledby` pointing at the label of the field it changes. Give a
  value that is not a plain number a spoken form with `aria-valuetext`, and name the field it
  changes with `aria-controls`.
- Tab reaches the stepper. Up and Down step by `step`, Page Up and Page Down by `largeStep`,
  Home and End go to `min` and `max` when they are set. Keys held with Command, Control or
  Option are left to the page.
- The halves are buttons named "Increment" and "Decrement" (`incrementLabel`,
  `decrementLabel`). They are out of the tab order, because the keyboard works on the stepper
  itself, but assistive technology that walks the page, such as a touch screen reader, finds
  them and can activate them.
- Clicking a half does not move keyboard focus, so a field that is being edited keeps it.
- Holding a half repeats the step after 400 ms, every 60 ms, until the button is released,
  the pointer leaves the half or the value reaches a bound. The timer belongs to the instance
  and is cleared when the stepper is disabled or unmounted; there are no global listeners.
- A disabled stepper has `aria-disabled`, leaves the tab order, and its halves are disabled
  buttons.
- With `name`, a hidden input submits the value with a form.
- In forced-colours mode the bezel is drawn with system colours.

## Visual comparison

`npm run visual -- stepper` compares two examples with the reference screenshots in both
appearances (`stepper.visual.json`): the stepper beside the From time field of the
Notifications pane, and the disabled one beside the date field of Date & Time. The bezel is
the shared control bezel, whose fill, hairlines, halo and shadow read the same on the stepper
as on a push button; the bezel's size, the chevrons and their colours are measured on those
screenshots. Share of pixels that differ at a colour distance of 0.05, half the default, and
the mean difference per channel out of 255, in Chromium on Linux:

| Crop | Aqua, 2x | Dark Aqua, 2x | Aqua, 1x | Dark Aqua, 1x |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Notifications, enabled (`@ 950,540,38,58`) | 0.82%, Δ 0.61 | 0.86%, Δ 0.67 | 19.06%, Δ 7.13 | 11.07%, Δ 4.98 |
| Date & Time, disabled (`@ 634,388,38,58`) | 0.00%, Δ 0.52 | 0.00%, Δ 0.43 | 8.35%, Δ 3.52 | 5.44%, Δ 2.29 |

The pixels that still differ at 2x are on the edge of the chevrons and on the bezel's four
corners. The corner is not a quarter circle in Catalina (it is flat for 2 pt at the top of a
12 pt bezel and straight from 5.75 pt down the side); a 5px circle is the closest CSS corner.
The chevron is a filled outline fitted to the screenshot's pixels; it measures 5.9 x 3.6 pt
with a 1.2 pt arm, where the spec's reading is a 6 x 3.5 pt stroke of 1.4 pt. At 1x the
scores are high because the crop is 19 x 29 pt and nearly all edge: there is no 1x reference,
so the 2x screenshot is averaged down, and the bezel's outline, which sits on half points,
cannot match its averaged counterpart ([Fidelity limits](/fidelity)).

No screenshot shows a pressed half, keyboard focus on a stepper, the small and mini sizes or a
stepper in a window that is not key. Those are checked in a real browser against values from
the spec instead (the `expect` comparisons): the example is pressed with the mouse or focused
with Tab, and chosen pixels must have the expected colour.

| State | What is checked | Source of the values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upper half pressed | Accent gradient in the half, white chevron, the other half unchanged; blue, purple and green, each in both appearances | Gradient measured on the default button; that a pressed half takes it is inferred |

| Main and inactive window | No focus ring; both appearances | Documented by AppKit |

The lower half pressed, the half pressed in an inactive window, the missing ring in a main or
inactive window and the three sizes are checked in Dark Aqua as well as in Aqua. Over the
example's `#2f3032` the dark fill is `#636465` (white at 25%) and the pressed fill of an
inactive window `#7a7b7c`.

Provisional, and checked nowhere against Catalina: the chevrons of the small and mini sizes
(the regular drawing scaled down), the corner radii of those sizes, whether the accent
gradient of a pressed half spans the half (as drawn here) or the whole bezel, and the
repeat timing (400 ms, then 60 ms, Base UI's values; AppKit's are not documented).
