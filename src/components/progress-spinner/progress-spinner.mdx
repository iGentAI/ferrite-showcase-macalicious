title: Progress Spinner
description: Twelve fading spokes that step round while work of unknown length is under way, for places too small for a progress bar.

<Example name="basic" both />

AppKit's `NSProgressIndicator` with the spinning style. It is built on
[Base UI's Progress](https://base-ui.com/react/components/progress), which supplies the
`progressbar` role and the value attributes. When there is room, and when the length of the
task is known, use a [progress bar](/components/progress-bar).

## Usage

```tsx
import { ProgressSpinner } from "macalicious/progress-spinner";
import "macalicious/progress-spinner.css";

<ProgressSpinner size="small" aria-label="Loading" />
<ProgressSpinner size="small" animating={busy} aria-label="Loading" />
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add progress-spinner`.

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines for progress indicators:

- Use a spinner where space is tight: beside a control, in a list row, in a corner of a pane.
- Show it only while the work is under way and hide it when the work finishes. With
  `animating={false}` the spinner disappears but keeps its place, so nothing moves when it
  comes back.
- Do not switch between a spinner and a bar for the same task.
- Add a few words when it is not obvious what is in progress.

## Variants and states

<Example name="states" both hideCode />

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spinning | default | Twelve spokes; the strongest is the head and the eleven behind it fade. The pattern steps one spoke clockwise twelve times a second |
| Stopped | `animating={false}` | Hidden, keeping its place |
| Stopped, displayed | `animating={false} displayedWhenStopped` | A still frame with the head at 12 o'clock |
| Determinate | `value` | A ring with a sector that grows clockwise from 12 o'clock, in the secondary label colour |

The spokes are black in Aqua and white in Dark Aqua, at measured opacities; they do not follow
the accent colour or the window state. In Aqua the spokes are stronger on a plain window
surface (the head is black at 98%) than in a sidebar (68%), as the Bluetooth pane and Notes'
sidebar show; a spinner inside a [sidebar](/components/sidebar), or under any other element
that has `data-material`, takes the fainter values by itself.

Measured: the geometry of the small size (diameter, spoke length and width) and the opacity of
each of the twelve spokes, in both appearances. Provisional, because no screenshot shows them:
the rate of one turn per second (a still image cannot show it; the direction follows from the
fade), the regular and mini sizes, which are the small artwork scaled, and the determinate
form.

### Animation cost

- One `transform` animation per spinner, in twelve steps a second, on the compositor. The
  spokes are a mask over a conic gradient, so the gradient is rasterised once and only
  turned. Measured in Chromium: twelve frames a second while it is on screen.
- While the spinner is outside the viewport its animation is paused. One
  `IntersectionObserver`, shared by every indicator on the page
  (`macalicious/lib/offscreen`), sets `data-offscreen` on the spinner; nothing re-renders.
  Measured in Chromium over two seconds: an off-screen spinner produces no frames at all, on
  the compositor or on the main thread.
- A stopped spinner has no animation and no observer entry.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the spokes stand still and the spinner fades in and
  out instead.

## API

<PropsTable name="ProgressSpinnerProps" />

It also takes the props of Base UI's `Progress.Root`: `min`, `max`, `format`, `locale`,
`getAriaValueText` and `aria-valuetext`.

| Attribute | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-indeterminate`, `data-progressing`, `data-complete` | present or absent | The status, set by Base UI. |
| `data-size` | `regular`, `small`, `mini` | Reflects `size`. |
| `data-animating` | present or absent | Reflects `animating`. |
| `data-displayed-when-stopped` | present or absent | Reflects `displayedWhenStopped`. |
| `data-offscreen` | present or absent | Set while a spinning spinner is outside the viewport; pauses it. |

## Accessibility

- The spinner has `role="progressbar"` without a value while it spins, and with
  `aria-valuenow` in its determinate form. It is not focusable.
- It needs a name that says what is in progress: `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- A stopped, hidden spinner is removed from the accessibility tree.
- For a region that is loading, also set `aria-busy="true"` on the region.
- In forced-colours mode the spokes are drawn in the system text colour.

shows a spinner in a sidebar; Dark Aqua uses one set of values, which the Startup Disk and
Bluetooth captures both match.

The comparisons cover still frames of the small size. They say nothing about the animation.
