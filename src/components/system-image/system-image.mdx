<Example name="basic" both />

AppKit ships a set of named images (`NSImage.Name`) that toolbars, gradient buttons and
tables use everywhere: a plus, a minus, the action gear, the share arrow, the four view-mode
glyphs. Most are template images, drawn in the text colour of the control they are in.
`SystemImage` is that set as inline SVG. Base UI has no image primitive; this is an `<svg>`
element and takes every `<svg>` prop.

The drawings are original, made on the boxes measured in the reference screenshots. None is
Apple's artwork.

## Usage

```tsx
import { SystemImage } from "macalicious/system-image";
import "macalicious/system-image.css";

<ToolbarButton aria-label="Share">
  <SystemImage name="share" />
</ToolbarButton>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add system-image`.

<Example name="in-controls" both />

## Guidelines

- Use a system image only for the meaning AppKit gives it: the plus adds a row, the gear opens
  an action menu, the share arrow opens the share menu. People know these from every Mac app.
- Let the image take the control's colour. Do not colour a template image yourself; it then
  no longer follows the disabled state, the appearance or a window that is not key.
- Keep the image at its own size in a regular control. Use `scale` for a larger rendering
  outside a control, not to fill a button.
- A button that shows only an image needs a name: put `aria-label` on the button, not on the
  image.

| `user-group` | `NSUserGroup` | 15 x 11 | Users tables |
| `everyone` | `NSEveryone` | 15 x 11 | Users tables |

An image has no states of its own. It is drawn in `currentColor`, so it is as dim as the
title of a disabled control and changes with the appearance, the accent of a selected row and
the window state exactly as the text beside it does. `user-group` and `everyone` draw the
figures at the back at half the colour's strength, as the Users table does.

`tag` and `rotate-left` are the only images that change with the appearance: in Aqua the tag
and the page are outlines, in Dark Aqua they are filled, as the Finder and Quick Look captures
show them. The tag's hole is a dot in Aqua and is cut out of the filled tag in Dark Aqua. Two
tokens of the image's own, `--mac-system-image-body` and `--mac-system-image-dot`, carry
this; the outline, the arrowhead and the arc are drawn the same way in both.

## Sizes

Every image has one size, the one in the table. `scale` multiplies it; `width`, `height` or a
CSS size override it.

## API

<PropsTable name="SystemImageProps" />

It also accepts every prop of an `<svg>` element. `SYSTEM_IMAGE_NAMES` is the list of names,
and `SystemImageName` their type.

| Attribute | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-name` | a name from the table | Which image is drawn. |

| Custom property | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `--mac-system-image-body` | Fill of the tag of `tag` and the page of `rotate-left` (`.mac-system-image-body`): transparent in Aqua, the image's colour in Dark Aqua. |
| `--mac-system-image-dot` | Fill of the hole of `tag` (`.mac-system-image-dot`): the image's colour in Aqua, transparent in Dark Aqua. |

## Accessibility

- Without a name the image is decorative: `aria-hidden="true"`, and never focusable. The
  control around it carries the name.
- With `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` it is exposed as an image (`role="img"`) with that
  name. Use this only for an image that stands alone and means something, such as the group
  glyph in a table cell without text.
- In forced-colours mode the image takes the system text colour of its control, because it
  has no colour of its own.

The list-button, action-bar, Back and Forward, Safari and view-switcher comparisons place the
images in the library's own gradient button, toolbar button and toolbar segmented control, at
the crops those components are compared with. The Console, Preview and Sharing comparisons
are 17 to 19 pt crops close around the glyph alone, so every pixel of a drawing's edge counts
for much more than in a crop of a whole control: the scores say that the stand-ins have the
measured size, position and weight, not that they are Apple's shapes.

Console draws `info` in the accent colour, and the comparison gives the image that colour;
no capture shows it as a grey template. Its letter is measured there: a 2 pt dot, a 2 pt stem
with a 1 pt flag at its top left and a 4 pt foot. The letter is cut out of the disc and drawn
again over the hole with the fill a [Toolbar Button](/components/toolbar-button) gives a mark
(`--mac-toolbar-button-glyph-mark`), so in a tinted toggle that is on it shows white in Aqua,
as Console's Info button does; outside a toolbar button the hole stays empty.

`info-outline` is the same letter in a 16 pt ring with a 1 pt stroke, measured on the info
mark at the end of a Reminders row: the ring at x 1580..1611 and y 964..995 of the Aqua
capture, a 2 pt dot centred 4.5 pt down, a 2 pt stem from 6.5 to 12.5 pt down with a 1 pt
flag at its top left and a 4 pt foot. Reminders draws it in the system blue
(`--mac-color-system-blue`), and the comparison gives it that colour: the captures store it
as #2f7cf6 and #3686f7, which their Display P3 profile turns into #027aff and #0584fd in
sRGB. Keychain Access draws it in its toolbar button's grey.

`tag` is measured on Finder's Edit Tags button, disabled in both Home captures: 17 x 10 pt of
ink at x 1017..1050, y 138..157 of the Aqua capture, a 1 pt outline with a 45 degree point
and a 2 pt hole centred 4 pt from the point. The Dark Aqua capture has the same tag filled,
1 pt further left, so its crop starts 1 pt further left. The comparisons are 21 x 14 pt crops
on the button's fill, in the grey of a disabled toolbar glyph (#acacac and #8f9192).

`rotate-left` is measured on Quick Look's Rotate Left button: a 9 x 7 pt page with a 1 pt
outline at the bottom left of the 16 pt box, an arrowhead 4.5 pt deep and 5 pt across at the
top, pointing left, and a 1 pt arc from it round to the right edge and down to 11.5 pt. The
gallery's Preview pane in the Gallery View captures shows the same glyph. The comparisons are
20 x 17 pt crops on the button's fill, white in Aqua and #667080 in Dark Aqua, where the
panel's material shows through; the Dark Aqua capture has the glyph 0.5 pt further left in
its button, and its crop follows it.
