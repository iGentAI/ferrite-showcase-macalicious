AppKit calls this window the *font panel* (`NSFontPanel`); its title is "Fonts", and every
Catalina app with styled text opens it from Format > Font > Show Fonts (Command-T). It sets the
font of the selected text: a row of text effects sits over four columns, and a search field
under them filters the families.

| Part | What it does | Built from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text underline, Text strikethrough | None or Single | [`PopUpButton`](/components/pop-up-button), small |
| Text color | The colour of the text | [`ColorWell`](/components/color-well), toolbar bezel |
| Collection | Filters the families: All Fonts, Fixed Width, Fun, Modern, Traditional, Web | [`BorderedList`](/components/bordered-list) |
| Family | Sets the family | `BorderedList` |
| Typeface | Sets bold and italic, by the family's names for them (Helvetica's are Oblique) | `BorderedList` |
| Size | A field for any size, over AppKit's list of sizes | [`TextField`](/components/text-field) and `BorderedList` |
| Search | Filters the families by name | [`SearchField`](/components/search-field) |

**No reference screenshot shows the Fonts panel.** Its arrangement follows AppKit's panel;
every measurement of its own is an estimate. See [Visual comparison](#visual-comparison).

## Usage

```tsx
import { FontPanel } from "macalicious/font-panel";
import "macalicious/font-panel.css";
import { applyRichTextFormat, FONT_FAMILIES, readRichTextFormat } from "macalicious/lib/rich-text";

<FontPanel
  families={FONT_FAMILIES}
  value={format}
  onFontChange={(change) => {
    keeper.restore();
    applyRichTextFormat(editor, change);
    setFormat(readRichTextFormat(editor));
  }}
  onClose={() => setShown(false)}
/>
```

The panel holds no font of its own. `families` are the families it lists: `FONT_FAMILIES`,
the families that come with Catalina (with fallbacks for other systems), or a list of your own.
`value` is the format of the selection, which it shows;
every change a control makes is reported to `onFontChange` as the part that changed
(`{ family }`, `{ bold, italic }`, `{ size }`, `{ underline }`, `{ strikethrough }` or
`{ color }`). Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add font-panel`. See
[Installation](/installation).

### With a rich text view

The helper `macalicious/lib/rich-text` applies such a change to the selection of a
[`RichTextView`](/components/text-view) and reads the format back where the insertion point is.
A click in the panel takes the focus from the text, so give the text its selection back before
the change is applied: `keepRichTextSelection(editor).restore()` does that. The helper also
knows the key equivalents of the Format menu (`richTextKeyCommand`), such as Command-T for
Show Fonts and Command-B for Bold.

## Guidelines

From Apple's documentation of
[NSFontPanel](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appkit/nsfontpanel) and of the
[Font menu](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appkit/nsfontmanager), not verified
against a screenshot:

- The Fonts panel is the system's one place to choose a font. Open it from Format > Font > Show
  Fonts (Command-T) rather than building a chooser of your own, and keep the format bar or the
  Format menu for the common styles.
- It acts on the selection of the key window and follows it: when the insertion point moves
  into other text, the panel shows that text's font.
- A change applies at once; there is no OK button. Close the panel with its close button or
  Command-T again.

## Variants and states

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The selection's font | `value` | Its family, typeface and size are the selected rows; the size is in the field |
| A family the panel does not list | `value.family` names one | No family row is selected; its typefaces are not listed |
| Filtered families | a collection, or text in the search field | Only those families are listed |
| Window not key | `state="main"` or `"inactive"`, or such an ancestor | As any window, and the lists' selections are grey |

The controls' own states (focus, a pressed pop-up button) are on their pages.

## Sizes

The panel is 444 pt wide by default (`width`) and as tall as its content; the four lists are
150 pt high. Its controls are small ones, as in AppKit's panel.

## Accessibility

- The panel is a region named by its title, "Fonts".
- The effects are a group named "Text effects": two pop-up buttons named "Text underline" and
  "Text strikethrough", and a colour well named "Text color".
- Each column is a `listbox` named after its heading ("Collection", "Family", "Typeface",
  "Size") and is one tab stop; the arrow keys, Home, End and typing a name move its selection,
  which applies at once.
- The size field is named "Font size"; Return or leaving the field applies what was typed,
  Escape puts back the size of the text.
- The search field is named "Search fonts".

## Visual comparison

No screenshot in the reference set shows the Fonts panel, and no spec measures it, so there is
no crop to compare with and `npm run visual -- font-panel` reports no score. Its checks hold
chosen pixels of the panel, in both appearances, to the measured colours of the window and of
the lists it is built from, and to the format it is given (`font-panel.visual.json`).

Everything about the panel's own geometry is provisional: its width, the 10 pt margins, the
columns' widths and the lists' height. Not implemented: the action menu (Add to Favorites,
Show Preview, Characters), the document colour and the text shadow controls, double underline
and coloured underlines, the size slider, and the families a visitor's system has beyond the
list the panel is given.
