The month under Calendar's list of calendars: the month and year between two chevrons, the
initials of the weekdays, and six weeks of days with today in a red disc and the days of the
neighbouring months dimmed. Choosing a day selects it, and Calendar then shows that day in its
main view. This is Calendar's own drawing, not AppKit's graphical date picker (`NSDatePicker`),
which has a different header, pitch and selection; Base UI has no primitive for either, so the
component is written here as a grid of buttons.

## Usage

```tsx
import { CalendarMiniMonth } from "macalicious/calendar-mini-month";
import "macalicious/styles.css";

<CalendarMiniMonth today={new Date()} onValueChange={(day) => console.log(day)} />
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add calendar-mini-month`.

- It is as wide as its container and has no background: put it on the surface it belongs to.
  In Calendar that is the foot of the 165 pt [Sidebar](/components/sidebar).
- `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange` hold the selected day; `month`, `defaultMonth`
  and `onMonthChange` the month shown. Either pair can be controlled or left to the component.
  Choosing a day of a neighbouring month also shows that month.
- `today` puts a day in the red disc. Nothing is marked without it: the component reads no
  clock while it renders, so a page rendered on a server matches the browser's. Only when
  neither a month, a value nor `today` is given does it open on the current month.
- `weekStartsOn` is the first day of the week, Sunday by default, as Calendar's “Start week
  on” preference sets it.
- `navigation={false}` leaves the chevrons out, for a month that another control steps: the
  twelve months of Calendar's year view, say.

## Guidelines

The Human Interface Guidelines for macOS describe no month view of this kind; these notes
describe what Calendar on macOS 10.15 does, as the reference screenshots show it.

- Use it beside a larger view of the same dates, to jump to a day. For entering a date in a
  form, use a date picker.
- Mark today whenever today is in the month shown.

## Sizes

One size. With its rule it is 164 pt tall: a 23 pt header 10 pt under the top edge, 21.5 pt
of weekday initials and six weeks of 17 pt. The seven columns share the width less 9 pt, and
the days sit 1.75 pt nearer the trailing edge than the leading one, as in Calendar, where the
trailing edge is the sidebar's divider.

## API

`CalendarMiniMonth` takes the props of a `<div>`, which go to its root with `ref`, `className`
and `style`, and these:

<PropsTable name="CalendarMiniMonthProps" />

| Attribute | On | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `data-date` | day | `year-month-day`, such as `2019-10-3` | The day, in local time. |
| `data-outside` | day | present or absent | A day of the month before or after. |
| `data-weekend` | day, weekday initial | present or absent | Saturday and Sunday. |
| `data-today`, `aria-current="date"` | day | present or absent | Today. |
| `data-selected` | day | present or absent | The selected day; its cell has `aria-selected`. |

Classes: `.mac-calendar-mini-month` (the root), `-header`, `-title`, `-step` (a chevron),
`-grid`, `-row`, `-weekday` and `-day`, each prefixed with `.mac-calendar-mini-month`. There are
no CSS variables to set.

## Accessibility

- The weeks are a `grid` named by the title, with a row of column headers that carry the full
  weekday names and a `gridcell` per day; the selected day's cell has `aria-selected`.
- Every day is a `<button>` named with its full date (“Thursday, October 3, 2019”). One day is
  in the tab order: the day the focus was on last, else the selected day, else today, else the
  first of the month.
- From a day, the arrow keys move by a day or a week, also into the days of the neighbouring
  months and, past the weeks shown, into the month beside. Home and End go to the ends of the
  week, Page Up and Page Down to the same day of the month before and after (or its last day).
  Space and Return choose the day.
- The two chevrons are buttons named “Previous month” and “Next month”; translate them with
  `previousLabel` and `nextLabel`. The title is a polite live region, so a change of month is
  announced.
- Today carries `aria-current="date"`. In forced colours today and the selected day are
  outlined.

## Visual comparison

`calendar-mini-month.visual.json` compares the whole mini month with the foot of Calendar's
sidebar in each appearance: October 2019 with the 3rd as today in Aqua and the 4th in Dark
Aqua. Nearly all of the crop is text, so each comparison has a twin with the text masked that
scores the rule, the chevrons, the fill and today's disc with its number.

Scores from `npm run visual -- calendar-mini-month` in Chromium on Linux, with the text in the
fallback font: the share of pixels that differ by more than the threshold (0.1; 0.05 for the
masked twins), and the mean colour difference out of 255.

| Comparison | Reference (crop x, y, width, height) | Mismatch 1x | Mean Δ 1x | Mismatch 2x | Mean Δ 2x |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `calendar-light` | Light-Calendar (114, 996, 326, 326) | 6.27% | 4.92 | 5.27% | 4.73 |
| `calendar-light-drawing` | the same, text masked | 1.55% | 0.54 | 1.03% | 0.40 |
| `calendar-dark` | Dark-Calendar (114, 996, 326, 326) | 6.29% | 4.97 | 5.17% | 4.80 |
| `calendar-dark-drawing` | the same, text masked | 1.44% | 1.17 | 1.31% | 1.19 |

What is left in the masked twins is the number in today's disc and the chevrons, which are
original drawings.

`selected-light` chooses a day with a click and checks its disc, and `focus-ring-dark` reaches
today with Tab and checks the ring; no screenshot shows either. Both pass at 1x and 2x.

Measured: the rule, the text colours, today's disc, and the positions of the title, the
chevrons, the initials and the days. Provisional: the selected day, the pressed looks, the
focus ring and the look in a window that is not key. The sidebar behind the month is vibrant in
Catalina; the colours were read over the capture's sidebar, `#e8e9e9` and `#2c2d2e`.
