<Example name="basic" both />

AppKit's `QLPreviewPanel`. One translucent surface frames the preview: a 38 pt bar with a
close and a full-screen button, the item's name and a few action buttons, and a thin frame at
the sides and the bottom. The material shows what is behind the panel, blurred and tinted, so
the bar takes the colour of the page under it. The shadow, the hairline and the corners come
from the library's [Window Shadow](/components/window-shadow); the buttons are
[Base UI Buttons](https://base-ui.com/react/components/button).

## Usage

```tsx
import { QuickLookPanel, QuickLookPanelButton } from "macalicious/quick-look-panel";
import "macalicious/styles.css";

<QuickLookPanel
  title="Wave.jpg"
  width={892}
  onClose={close}
  onFullScreen={enterFullScreen}
  actions={
    <>
      <QuickLookPanelButton aria-label="Rotate Left"><RotateGlyph /></QuickLookPanelButton>
      <QuickLookPanelButton onClick={open}>Open with Preview</QuickLookPanelButton>
      <QuickLookPanelButton aria-label="Share"><ShareGlyph /></QuickLookPanelButton>
    </>
  }
>
  <img src={url} alt="Wave" />
</QuickLookPanel>
```

- The preview is the children: an image, a video, or any element. It is centred, and an image
  larger than the panel is scaled down to fit; the material fills the bands beside it.
- The title is centred on the panel while there is room beside the actions. A longer name
  moves aside and is cut with an ellipsis; it never lies under the discs or the buttons.
  Tools wider than the space between the close button and the actions scroll sideways.
- Without a `height` the panel is as tall as the bar and the preview, which is how Finder
  sizes it to the item's shape.
- The panel draws itself and reports Close, Full Screen and Escape. Opening it on Space,
  moving between items with the arrow keys, and placing it over the window (`style`) are the
  application's.
- Glyphs are yours to supply, about 15 pt, drawn with `currentColor`. Quick Look's own are
  Apple artwork and are not part of the library.

### Markup mode

<Example name="markup" both />

Give the panel `tools`, normally a [Markup Toolbar](/components/markup-toolbar), and they take
the place of the title; the full-screen button is hidden, as in Catalina. Swap the `actions`
for Done at the same time.

## Guidelines

From the Human Interface Guidelines for macOS,
[Quick Look](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503144246/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/system-capabilities/quick-look/):

- Quick Look shows full-size previews of documents, images and other files, which helps people
  find the one they are looking for.
- Offer a Quick Look viewer if your application lets people attach or otherwise work with
  files, especially files it cannot open itself, so that they can preview them without
  leaving the application.

Not from that page, but what the screenshots show: the bar holds only the few actions that
suit a preview (rotate, mark up, open in the default application, share).

## Variants and states

| State | Look | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Key panel | Coloured material, full-strength buttons | Measured (Finder Quick Look) |
| Tools in the bar | Tools replace the title; no full-screen button | Measured (Quick Look Markup) |
| Button pressed | Darker fill | Provisional: no screenshot |
| Button disabled | Half opacity | Provisional: no screenshot |
| Keyboard focus | Focus ring on the button or disc | Provisional: no screenshot |
| Not key (`state` `main` or `inactive`) | Discs, title and buttons at half opacity, no focus ring | Provisional: no screenshot |
| Reduced transparency | Opaque bar, `#e6e6e6` (dark `#2d2d2d`) | Provisional: neutral equivalents of the tint |

## Sizes

One size. The bar is 38 pt high; the discs are 12 pt, 12 pt from the leading edge; buttons are
19 pt high, at least 39 pt wide, 10.5 pt apart and 8.5 pt from the trailing edge; the frame is
3 pt at the sides and the bottom.

## Accessibility

- The panel is a `dialog` named by its title (or by `aria-label`). It is not modal: the window
  behind it stays available, as in Finder, so it does not set `aria-modal` or contain focus.
  Move focus into the panel when you open it and back to the item when you close it.
- Escape pressed inside the panel calls `onClose`.
- The two discs are buttons named "Close" and "Enter Full Screen". A button that shows only a
  glyph needs an `aria-label`; give the preview a text alternative (`alt`).
- Every button is in the tab order and is activated with Space or Return.
- With `prefers-reduced-transparency` the material is opaque; in forced-colours mode the bar
  and the buttons use system colours and a border.

## Visual comparison

`quick-look-panel.visual.json` compares the two ends of the bar with the reference screenshots
in both appearances: the leading end with the two discs and the panel's corner, and the
trailing end with the four buttons. In the captures the panel floats over the desktop picture,
and the bar's colour is that picture, blurred and tinted. Each example therefore paints,
behind the panel, a colour that stands for the picture at that spot and gives the bar colour
measured there; the comparison scores the material's tint and the drawing on it, not the blur.
The buttons' glyphs are Apple artwork and are masked, as is the text button's title and, at
the trailing corner, a stretch of bar through which another window shows in the capture.

Scores from `npm run visual -- quick-look-panel` in Chromium on Linux with the fallback font:
the share of pixels that differ by more than the comparison's threshold, and the mean colour
difference out of 255.

| Comparison | Reference (crop x, y, width, height) | Mismatch 1x | Mean Δ 1x | Mismatch 2x | Mean Δ 2x |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `controls-light` | Light-Finder-Quick-Look (1480, 90, 130, 76) | 4.21% | 3.74 | 0.40% | 2.81 |
| `actions-light` (masked) | Light-Finder-Quick-Look (2650, 90, 614, 76) | 1.90% | 2.27 | 2.25% | 2.89 |
| `controls-dark` | Dark-Finder-Quick-Look (964, 118, 130, 76) | 4.70% | 5.22 | 0.97% | 3.94 |
| `actions-dark` (masked) | Dark-Finder-Quick-Look (2556, 118, 614, 76) | 4.69% | 6.14 | 4.70% | 6.48 |

The captured bar is not flat: the picture's grain shows through, which is most of the mean
difference in Dark Aqua. Pixel checks without a reference: `pressed-*` hold a button down and
read its fill and its neighbour's; `long-title-*` give the panel a name too long for its bar
and read the gaps on both sides of the title, which must show the bar; `wide-tools-*` do the
same for tools twice as wide as the panel.

Measured: the bar's height, the discs' size and position, the buttons' size, spacing, fill and
edges, the frame's widths, the tints. Provisional: the blur radius and saturation, the neutral
label colour of the discs and the title (the real one is vibrant and takes the picture's hue),
the pressed, disabled, focused and not-key looks. Not shown in any screenshot and not built:
full-screen presentation, the previous and next buttons and the index sheet of a multiple
selection.
