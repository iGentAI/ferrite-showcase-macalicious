A scope bar appears beneath the toolbar while people search. Its scopes are a single-selection
group built on [Base UI's Radio Group](https://base-ui.com/react/components/radio); its action
buttons are Base UI's [Button](https://base-ui.com/react/components/button).

## Usage

```tsx
import {
  ScopeBar,
  ScopeBarAction,
  ScopeBarActions,
  ScopeBarButton,
  ScopeBarGroup,
  ScopeBarLabel,
} from "macalicious/scope-bar";
import "macalicious/scope-bar.css";

<ScopeBar>
  <ScopeBarLabel id="scope">Search:</ScopeBarLabel>
  <ScopeBarGroup aria-labelledby="scope" defaultValue="folder" onValueChange={setScope}>
    <ScopeBarButton value="mac">This Mac</ScopeBarButton>
    <ScopeBarButton value="folder">“Macintosh SSD”</ScopeBarButton>
  </ScopeBarGroup>
  <ScopeBarActions>
    <ScopeBarAction onClick={save}>Save</ScopeBarAction>
    <ScopeBarAction glyph="plus" aria-label="Add criterion" onClick={add} />
  </ScopeBarActions>
</ScopeBar>
```

Place it under the [Title Bar](/components/title-bar) or the
[Window Tab Bar](/components/window-tab-bar), and give the bar above it `divider="bar"`.

## Guidelines

Not verified: this section paraphrases Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and was not checked
against Apple's text.

- A scope button (AppKit's recessed style) selects one search scope, and the others are
  deselected; a round-rect button starts an action or adds search criteria. Use both only in
  a scope bar.
- Show the bar while a search is in progress, not permanently.
- Lead with a short label that says what the scopes narrow ("Search:").

## Variants and states

<Example name="find-bar" both />

| Surface | Height | Seen in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `plain` (default) | 32 pt, flat | Finder search |
| `toolbar` | 31 pt, on the toolbar gradient, with wider scopes and 18 pt actions | Preview's find bar |
| `toolbar` with `size="small"` | 23 pt, with 11 pt medium scopes, darker scope titles and lighter bottom rows | Console, Dictionary |

<Example name="compact" both />

`ScopeBarActionGroup` joins the actions inside it into one segmented round-rect, like the
previous and next buttons of the find bar.

<Example name="states" both hideCode />

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope off | – | Bold text |
| Scope on | its value is the group's value | Filled pill, 16 pt high |
| Scope under the pointer | `:hover` | Outline pill (provisional) |
| Pressed, keyboard focus | `:active`, `:focus-visible` | provisional; the focus ring shows in the key window only |
| Disabled | `disabled` | Dimmed (measured on the find bar's arrows) |
| Window not key | `data-window-state="main"` or `"inactive"` on an ancestor | The pill of the scope that is on turns the unemphasised grey (provisional) |
| Inactive window | `data-window-state="inactive"` on an ancestor | The toolbar surface flattens (provisional) |

## Sizes

`surface` and `size` set the bar's height. There is no mini bar.

| Size | Height | Scopes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `regular` (default) | 32 pt for `plain`, 31 pt for `toolbar` | 13 pt bold, 16 pt pill; 8 pt or 13.5 pt beside the title |
| `small` | 23 pt: 22 pt of fill over the bottom point | 11 pt medium, 16 pt pill centred on the fill; 6.5 pt beside the title |

The small bar is measured on the toolbar surface only; on the plain surface it keeps the plain
bar's colours with the small height and type (provisional). Dictionary's bar is the same bar
25 pt high: set `style={{ height: 25 }}`. The action buttons are 16 pt high on the plain
surface and 18 pt on the toolbar surface.

## Accessibility

- The scopes are a `radiogroup` of `radio`s: one tab stop, arrow keys move the selection. Name
  the group with `aria-labelledby` pointing at the `ScopeBarLabel`, or with `aria-label`.
- Actions are native buttons. One that shows only a glyph needs an `aria-label`.
- Name a `ScopeBarActionGroup` with `aria-label`.
- A scope that is on is told apart by its pill and, for assistive technology, by its checked
  state; in Dark Aqua the pill is light with dark text.
