An iPad app that runs on the Mac keeps UIKit's segmented control: a grey track 24.5 pt tall
with titles of equal width, the selected one on a raised pill. Find My's sidebar switches
between People and Devices with it. It is a radio group, built on Base UI's RadioGroup and
Radio. AppKit's own control, with its bezel and its accent-coloured selection, is the
[Segmented Control](/components/segmented-control); the map types that Find My floats over its
map are the `MapTypeControl` of [Map Controls](/components/map-controls), the same control at
22.25 pt over a map.

## Usage

```tsx
import { CatalystSegment, CatalystSegmentedControl } from "macalicious/catalyst-segmented-control";
import "macalicious/catalyst-segmented-control.css";

<CatalystSegmentedControl
  aria-label="List"
  value={list}
  onValueChange={(value) => setList(value as string)}
>
  <CatalystSegment value="people">People</CatalystSegment>
  <CatalystSegment value="devices">Devices</CatalystSegment>
</CatalystSegmentedControl>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add catalyst-segmented-control`. See
[Installation](/installation).

The control is as wide as its titles need, or as wide as you make it; the segments share the
width equally and a title that does not fit ends in an ellipsis. `value`, `defaultValue`,
`onValueChange`, `disabled` and `name` are Base UI's.

## Guidelines

The macOS Human Interface Guidelines describe AppKit's segmented control, not this one (not
verified against a source for Catalyst). What the captured app does:

- Use it in the content of a Catalyst app, for a choice between a few views of the same
  place. In a toolbar, a Catalyst app uses the toolbar's own segmented control (Home).
- Keep titles short, one or two words, and of similar length: every segment is as wide as the
  others.
- One segment is always selected.

One variant. Keyboard focus is transient, so this gallery pins it.

| Normal | – | Track: grey at 12% (`#e8e7e5` on Find My's sidebar), Dark Aqua grey at 24% (`#343438`) | measured |

| Keyboard focus | `:focus-visible` | Focus ring inside the segment, since the track clips | provisional |
| Window not key | an ancestor has `data-window-state="main"` or `"inactive"` | No focus ring; nothing else changes | measured: the Dark Aqua capture's window is not key |

## Sizes

One size: 24.5 pt tall. The width is the layout's (246.5 pt in Find My's 261 pt sidebar).

## Accessibility

- The control is a `radiogroup` and each segment a `radio`. Give the control an `aria-label`
  (or `aria-labelledby`): its titles alone do not say what is being chosen.
- It is one tab stop. The arrow keys move the selection and the focus together, and pass over
  a disabled segment.
- The focus ring is drawn only in a key window. In forced-colours mode the track has a border
  and the selected segment the system's highlight colours.

## Visual comparison

`npm run visual -- catalyst-segmented-control` compares the People and Devices switch with the
two Find My captures: 260 x 40 pt of the sidebar with the track, the pill and its shadow
(`114,136,520,80`; Dark Aqua `70,112,520,80`, a window that is not key). Share of pixels that
differ, in Chromium on Linux with the fallback font:

| Comparison | Aqua 2x | Aqua 1x | Dark Aqua 2x | Dark Aqua 1x |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Titles masked, strict (a pixel differs at a colour distance of 0.05) | 0.11% | 0.33% | 0.17% | 1.70% |
| Nothing masked | 3.11% | 3.36% | 2.78% | 4.19% |

The budgets in `catalyst-segmented-control.visual.json` are these scores, measured in a
visual run, rounded up.

Measured: the track's and the pill's size, place and colours, the titles' size, colour and
place. Fitted: the pill's shadow, to the three sides of the Aqua capture where the track shows
it. Inferred: the 7 pt and 5.5 pt corner radii (UIKit's, scaled as Catalyst scales an iPad
app); the comparison agrees with them. The second row of the table is the titles in the
fallback font instead of San Francisco. At 1x the track's and the pill's edges lie on half
pixels.

No screenshot shows another segment selected, a third segment, a disabled segment or the focus
ring. Those are checked in a real browser (the `expect` comparisons): a click moves the pill,
and Tab puts the focus ring inside the selected segment in a key window and none in another.

Not built: the pill sliding from one segment to the next, which UIKit animates; here the
selection changes at once.
