<Example name="basic" both />

Find My lists people by a disc of 29 pt: the person's picture, or a white silhouette on a grey
disc. `Avatar` draws that disc. It is built on Base UI's Avatar, which shows the picture once
it has loaded and the fallback until then, and when the picture cannot be loaded. The
silhouette is the library's own drawing, a circle and an ellipse placed and sized where the
capture has the head and the shoulders; it is not Apple's artwork. For any other picture,
cropped or not, use the [Image View](/components/image-view), whose monogram is the flat grey
disc of System Preferences.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Avatar } from "macalicious/avatar";
import "macalicious/avatar.css";

<Avatar alt={person.name} src={person.picture}>
  {person.initials}
</Avatar>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add avatar`. See
[Installation](/installation).

What the disc shows: the picture at `src`, cropped to the disc; without a loaded picture, its
children (initials); without children, the silhouette. `alt` names it in all three cases.

<Example name="variants" both />

The initials stand in while a picture loads, and stay when it cannot be loaded. A new `src`
shows them again until the new picture has arrived.

## Guidelines

The Human Interface Guidelines have no page for avatars (not verified). What Find My does:

- An avatar stands before a person's name in a list; it does not replace the name.
- A person without a picture gets the silhouette, on the same grey disc in both appearances.

## Variants and states

| Shows | When | Look | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Silhouette | no `src`, no children | A white head and shoulders on a disc that runs from `#a5aab6` at the top to `#868a93` at the bottom, the same in Aqua and Dark Aqua; in Dark Aqua a black hairline just outside the disc | The disc, its rim and the silhouette's place and size are measured; the silhouette's drawing is the library's own |
| Initials | children, no loaded picture | White, Medium, two fifths of the disc high | provisional: no capture shows initials on this disc |
| Picture | `src` has loaded | The picture, cropped to the disc | provisional: no capture shows a picture |

The avatar has no states of its own: it is not interactive, and it is the same in a window
that is not key (the Dark Aqua capture is of one).

## Sizes

`size` is the diameter in points: 29 by default, as in Find My's list. The silhouette and
initials scale with it.

## API

<PropsTable name="AvatarProps" />

The remaining props are those of Base UI's `Avatar.Root` and go to the root `<span>` with
`ref`.

Classes: `.mac-avatar` (the disc), `.mac-avatar-image` (the picture),
`.mac-avatar-fallback` (initials or the silhouette), `.mac-avatar-glyph` (the silhouette).

| CSS variable | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--mac-avatar-size` | `29px` | The diameter; set by `size`. |
| `--mac-avatar-fill` | `#a5aab6` to `#868a93`, top to bottom | The disc behind initials and the silhouette. |
| `--mac-avatar-glyph` | `#ffffff` | The silhouette and initials. |
| `--mac-avatar-rim` | none, black in Dark Aqua | The hairline just outside the disc. |

## Accessibility

- With `alt`, the avatar is an image named by it (`role="img"`), whether it shows the
  picture, initials or the silhouette; initials are not read separately.
- Without `alt` it is decoration and hidden from assistive technology. Leave `alt` out when
  the person's name stands beside the avatar, as in a list row.
- In forced-colours mode the disc has a border and the system's text colour.

## Visual comparison

`npm run visual -- avatar` compares the disc of "Me" in Find My's People list with both
captures: 36 x 36 pt of the sidebar around the disc (`128,240,72,72`; Dark Aqua
`84,216,72,72`), with nothing masked. Share of pixels that differ, in Chromium on Linux:

| Comparison | Aqua 2x | Aqua 1x | Dark Aqua 2x | Dark Aqua 1x |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The disc and the silhouette on the sidebar | 3.30% | 5.48% | 4.51% | 5.56% |

The budgets in `avatar.visual.json` are these scores, measured in a visual run, rounded up.
What differs is the edges: the capture's disc, rim and silhouette are softer than the
browser draws them, by about a pixel at 2x. Inside the edges the colours agree within a few
levels.

Measured: the disc's diameter, its place in the row, its gradient, its Dark Aqua rim, and the
place and size of the head and the shoulders. The `expect` comparisons check in a real
browser that the silhouette is white at the middle of the head and of the shoulders, that the
disc shows between them, at its rim and near its top and bottom in the gradient's colours,
and that a 48 pt avatar scales all of it. `avatar.browser.test.tsx` loads real pictures in
each installed engine: one that loads replaces the initials, one that fails leaves them, and a
new address shows them again until its picture arrives.

Not built: a badge on the avatar, a ring around it and groups of avatars; no capture of Find
My shows them.
