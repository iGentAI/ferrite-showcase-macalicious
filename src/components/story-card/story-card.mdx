<Example name="basic" both />

The Today page of News opens with top stories: two lead stories, each a picture 399 pt
wide and 300 tall with the publisher's logo, a bold headline and the story's age under it,
and a row of smaller stories with the same three lines beside a square thumbnail. News is an
iPad app in a Mac window ([Catalyst Chrome](/components/catalyst-chrome)) and draws the
stories itself, in iPad metrics scaled to 77%, which is why the sizes are fractional. Base UI
has no primitive for them: `StoryCard` is a styled element whose `render` prop, through
Base UI's `useRender`, makes it a link or a button.

## Usage

```tsx
import { StoryCard } from "macalicious/story-card";
import "macalicious/story-card.css";

<StoryCard
  render={<a href={story.url} />}
  headingLevel={3}
  publisher={<img src={story.logo} alt={story.publisher} />}
  title={story.headline}
  time="1h ago"
  artwork={<img src={story.picture} alt="" />}
/>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add story-card`. See
[Installation](/installation).

A card is as wide as its container: 399 pt for a lead story in News and 258 pt for a
secondary one, 22 pt apart. The library ships no artwork and no logos: the examples on this
page draw gradients and write the publisher's name, which the card sets in small bold
capitals.

## Guidelines

The Human Interface Guidelines have no page for the stories of News. What News does:

- A headline takes at most three lines; a longer one ends in an ellipsis.
- The age stands on the same line in every card of a row, however long the headlines are.
- The publisher is shown by its logo, at the logo's own height.
- A card is one target: make the whole card the link or the button.

## Variants and states

| Variant | From | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lead` | News, Top Stories | A picture 399 : 300 with corners of 3 pt; under it the publisher, a headline of 22 pt Bold on a 24 pt pitch with room for three lines, and the age in 9 pt Bold |
| `secondary` | News, Top Stories, second row | The same three lines in a column beside an 88 pt square thumbnail, 8 pt apart; headline of 15.5 pt Bold on a 16 pt pitch; the age level with the thumbnail's foot |
| `video` | News, the second lead story | A 44 pt disc, black at 60%, with a white triangle, over the middle of the picture |

The states apply when the card is a link or a button. Pressed and keyboard-focused are
transient, so this gallery pins them.

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | – | As above |
| Hover | – | No change |
| Pressed | `:active` on a link or button card | The card dimmed by a tenth |
| Keyboard focus | `:focus-visible` | Focus ring around the card |
| Disabled | the card is a disabled button, or has `aria-disabled="true"` | Half opacity. The look only: with `aria-disabled` the card still receives its events, so leave out its handler or its `href` as well |
| Window not key | an ancestor has `data-window-state="main"` or `"inactive"` | No focus ring; nothing else changes |

Measured, in both appearances: the two pictures' sizes and corners, the positions, sizes and
colours of the headline and the age in both variants, the width of the secondary story's text
and its distance from the thumbnail, and the play badge's size and triangle. Computed: the
badge's black, from one place where it lies over a dark part of a photograph. Provisional,
because no screenshot shows them: the pressed and disabled looks, the focus ring, the fill
behind a missing picture, and the publisher's name as text (News always shows a logo).

## Sizes

One size, set by the container.

| Variant | Width | Height |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lead` | its container's; 399 pt in News | the picture, 399 : 300 (`--mac-story-card-aspect`), and 132.75 pt of text |
| `secondary` | its container's; 258 pt in News | 90.5 pt, a little more than the 88 pt thumbnail |

Under a lead picture the publisher's line starts 13 pt down and is 16 pt tall, the headline's
cap top is 43 pt down and the age's 124 pt, wherever the headline ends. News fits the
publisher's line to the logo, and the text under it moves with it: set
`--mac-story-card-publisher-height` to your logo's height (the Dark Aqua capture's first logo
is 14 pt tall, and its headline stands 2 pt higher). In a secondary story the publisher's line
is 10 pt tall, the headline's cap top is 19.5 pt down and the age's 82.5 pt.

## API

<PropsTable name="StoryCardProps" />

The remaining props are those of a `<div>`, or of the element given to Base UI's `render`
prop, and go to that element with `ref`.

| Attribute | On | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `data-variant` | card | `lead`, `secondary` | Reflects `variant`. |
| `data-video` | card | present or absent | Reflects `video`. |
| `data-force` | card | `pressed`, `focus` | Documentation and visual tests only: pins a transient state. |

Classes: `.mac-story-card`, `.mac-story-card-text` (with `-publisher`, `-title` and `-time`
inside) and `.mac-story-card-artwork`.

| CSS variable | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--mac-story-card-aspect` | `399 / 300` | Proportions of a lead story's picture. |
| `--mac-story-card-publisher-height` | `16px`; secondary `10px` | Height of the publisher's line. |
| `--mac-story-card-title` | `#242527`, `#dadcdd` in Dark Aqua | The headline, and the publisher's name as text. |
| `--mac-story-card-time` | `#767472`, `#92989f` in Dark Aqua | The story's age. |
| `--mac-story-card-badge`, `--mac-story-card-badge-glyph` | black at 60%; `#ffffff` | The play badge's disc and triangle. |
| `--mac-story-card-placeholder` | `#e9e9eb`, `#48494d` in Dark Aqua | Behind a picture that is missing or transparent. |

## Accessibility

- A card is static unless you render it as a link or a button with `render`. Then it is one
  tab stop, Return (and Space, for a button) activates it, and it is named by its text:
  publisher, headline and age, in that order. A card without text needs an `aria-label`.
- The headline is plain text unless `headingLevel` is given, which makes it a heading of that
  level (`role="heading"` with `aria-level`), so that a page of stories can be read by its
  headings. It is a `<span>` and not an `<h3>` so that the card stays valid as a link or a
  button. Leave `headingLevel` out of a card that is a button.
- Give a publisher's logo its name as `alt`, and leave the picture's `alt` empty when the
  headline says what the story is.
- The play badge is a drawing and is not announced. Say that a story is a video in its
  headline or in an `aria-label`.
- The age's grey has a contrast of about 4 : 1 on the page of News in Aqua, as in the app:
  just under the 4.5 : 1 that text of this size needs. Darken `--mac-story-card-time` where
  the age must be read.
- The focus ring is drawn only in a key window. In forced-colours mode the picture has a
  border, one point wide, in the system's text colour.

## Visual comparison

`npm run visual -- story-card` compares the text of a lead story, a secondary story and a
picture's corner with the News captures in both appearances. The photographs and the
publishers' logos are News's content: they are masked or replaced by one colour, and the
headlines are stand-ins with as many lines as the capture's. Share of pixels that differ, in
Chromium on Linux with the fallback font:

| Comparison | Aqua 2x | Aqua 1x | Dark Aqua 2x | Dark Aqua 1x |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead story's text, 399 x 140 pt (`744,1102,798,280`; Dark Aqua `785,1102,798,280`), logo masked | 11.75% | 13.17% | 17.16% | 19.01% |
| The same, headline and age masked too | 0.01% | 0.03% | 0.03% | 0.00% |
| Secondary story, 279 x 112 pt (`744,1415,558,224`; Dark Aqua `785,1410,558,224`), logo and thumbnail masked | 12.19% | 13.85% | 11.47% | 13.21% |
| The same, headline and age masked too | 0.00% | 0.00% | 0.00% | 0.00% |
| Picture's corner, 20 x 20 pt (thumbnail `1075,1406,40,40`; Dark Aqua lead picture `775,492,40,40`) | 4.00% | 4.25% | 0.06% | 0.00% |

The first and third rows score other words than the capture's in another font, so they say
only that the lines stand where the capture's do: each line of the fixture starts within a
point of the capture's line. With the text masked the page around it matches. The corner is
compared where a photograph is one colour: on a thumbnail in Aqua, whose edges lie on
fractions of a pixel in the capture, and on the lead picture in Dark Aqua. The Dark Aqua
fixture sets the publisher's line to 14 pt, the height of that capture's logo.

No screenshot shows the play badge on a flat picture, a pressed or focused card or a window
that is not key. Those are checked in a real browser (the `expect` comparisons): the lead
picture fills its box, the badge's triangle and disc are at its middle, the secondary story's
thumbnail is 88 pt square at the trailing edge with 8 pt before it; pressed, the picture is at
90% brightness; Tab puts the focus ring around the card in a key window and none in another.

Not built: the rest of the Today page (its masthead, the "Top Stories" title and the "More"
link), which are plain text in News's own type, and the Stocks app's story list, whose rows
have other proportions and belong to that demo.
