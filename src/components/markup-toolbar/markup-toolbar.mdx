The tools of the Markup extension: sketch, draw, shapes, text, highlight, sign, then shape
style, border colour, fill colour and text style, then rotate and crop. A tool is a borderless
glyph; the active drawing tool is tinted with the accent colour; a tool that opens choices
carries a small chevron. The toolbar is built on
[Base UI's Toolbar](https://base-ui.com/react/components/toolbar) and has no background: it
sits on the bar of a [Quick Look Panel](/components/quick-look-panel) or on any bar.

## Usage

```tsx
import {
  MarkupToolbar,
  MarkupToolbarSeparator,
  MarkupToolbarSwatch,
  MarkupToolbarTool,
} from "macalicious/markup-toolbar";
import "macalicious/styles.css";

<MarkupToolbar aria-label="Markup">
  <MarkupToolbarTool aria-label="Sketch" selected={tool === "sketch"} onClick={() => setTool("sketch")}>
    <SketchGlyph />
  </MarkupToolbarTool>
  <MarkupToolbarTool aria-label="Shapes" menu>
    <ShapesGlyph />
  </MarkupToolbarTool>
  <MarkupToolbarSeparator />
  <MarkupToolbarTool aria-label="Fill Colour" menu>
    <MarkupToolbarSwatch color={fill} />
  </MarkupToolbarTool>
</MarkupToolbar>
```

- Glyphs are yours to supply, about 16 pt, drawn with `currentColor` so that they take the
  tool's colour. Markup's own glyphs are Apple artwork and are not part of the library; the
  examples use stand-in drawings.
- `selected` marks the drawing tool in use. Which tool that is, is your state: set `selected`
  to `true` or `false` on the tools that are modes and leave it out on the others.
- `menu` only draws the chevron and sets `aria-haspopup`. To open a menu or a popover, give
  the tool to the trigger of the library's menu or popover with `render`.
- `MarkupToolbarSwatch` stands for the glyph in the colour tools; without a `color` it shows
  "none".
- In a Quick Look panel, pass the toolbar as the panel's `tools`.

## Guidelines

Apple publishes no guideline for the Markup toolbar itself. From what the screenshots show
(not verified against a source):

- Keep the order: drawing tools, then style tools, then tools that change the whole image,
  with a separator between the groups.
- Exactly one drawing tool is active at a time.
- A tool that needs a selection is disabled, not hidden, while nothing is selected.

## Variants and states

<Example name="states" both />

| State | Look | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | Glyph in the bar's label colour | Measured (Quick Look Markup), stored as a neutral equivalent |
| Selected | Accent colour | Measured (Sketch) |
| With a menu | Chevron 4 pt after the glyph | Measured by inspection |
| Pressed | Darker by a fifth | Provisional: no screenshot |
| Disabled | 35% opacity | Provisional: no screenshot |
| Keyboard focus | Focus ring inside the tool | Provisional: no screenshot |
| Window not key | The selected tool loses its accent colour | Provisional: no screenshot |

## Sizes

One size: tools are 22 pt high and as wide as their glyph plus 8 pt on each side; a separator
is 1 pt wide and 20 pt high with 8 pt on each side. `--mac-markup-toolbar-gap` adds space
between tools.

## Accessibility

- The bar is a horizontal `toolbar`; give it an `aria-label`. It is one tab stop, and Left and
  Right Arrow move between its tools.
- A tool is a `button` and needs an `aria-label`, because it shows only a glyph. A tool with
  `selected` set announces whether it is pressed; a tool with `menu` announces that it opens a
  menu.
- The swatch is decorative; say the colour in the tool's label if it matters
  ("Fill Colour: none").
- In forced-colours mode glyphs use the button text colour, the selected tool the highlight
  colour, and the swatch keeps its own colour.

## Visual comparison

`markup-toolbar.visual.json` compares the leading 600 pt of Quick Look's bar in Markup mode
with the reference screenshots in both appearances. Every glyph is Apple artwork, left out of
the example and masked, so what is scored is the bar, the close disc and the two separators,
with the tools sized so that the separators stand where the capture has them. As for the Quick
Look panel, the example paints a flat colour behind the panel in place of the desktop picture.

Scores from `npm run visual -- markup-toolbar` in Chromium on Linux with the fallback font: the
share of pixels that differ by more than the comparison's threshold, and the mean colour
difference out of 255.

| Comparison | Reference (crop x, y, width, height) | Mismatch 1x | Mean Δ 1x | Mismatch 2x | Mean Δ 2x |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `quick-look-markup-light` (masked) | Light-Finder-Quick-Look-Markup (1480, 90, 1200, 76) | 1.07% | 4.40 | 0.88% | 4.43 |
| `quick-look-markup-dark` (masked) | Dark-Finder-Quick-Look-Markup (964, 118, 1200, 76) | 5.53% | 6.88 | 5.08% | 7.00 |

Measured: the separators' size, position and colour, the selected tool's colour, the glyph
height and the tools' pitch. Provisional: the neutral glyph colour (the real one is vibrant),
the swatch's drawing, the chevron's exact shape, and the pressed, disabled, focused and
not-key looks. The Preview host, a second toolbar row under the window's toolbar, is not shown
in any screenshot; the same parts serve it.
