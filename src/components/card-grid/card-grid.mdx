The browsing layout of Catalina's store and media apps: a page is a column of sections, each
with a header (a title and an optional `See All`) over a shelf or a grid of cards. In AppKit
these are `NSCollectionView`s with item views the apps draw themselves; there is no system
card style, so each of the four card variants here is measured from one app. Base UI has no
collection primitive: the section and its list are plain elements with list semantics, and
the card and the header's action use Base UI's `useRender`, which keeps the `render` prop
working.

## Usage

```tsx
import { CardGrid, CardGridAction, CardGridCard } from "macalicious/card-grid";
import "macalicious/card-grid.css";

<CardGrid
  title="Recently Added"
  action={<CardGridAction href="/albums">See All</CardGridAction>}
>
  {albums.map((album) => (
    <CardGridCard
      key={album.id}
      variant="square"
      render={<a href={album.url} />}
      artwork={<img src={album.cover} alt="" style={{ width: "100%", height: "100%", objectFit: "cover" }} />}
      title={album.title}
      subtitle={album.artist}
    />
  ))}
</CardGrid>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add card-grid`. See
[Installation](/installation).

Pass the cards as direct children: each child becomes one list item (an array is fine; a
fragment counts as one item). The library ships no artwork. The examples on this page draw
gradients; supply your own images.

## Guidelines

The Human Interface Guidelines have no page for these layouts. What the four apps do:

- Give every section a short title; add `See All` when the section shows only part of a
  collection.
- Use a shelf for a row that continues sideways, and a grid for a collection shown in full.
- Keep one card variant per section, and the 20 pt gap and 40 pt side margins throughout a
  page.
- Divide sections with the 1 pt line (`separator`), not with boxes.
- A card is one target: make the whole card the link or the button, not parts of it. The
  lockup is the exception, because it carries its own button.

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | – | Artwork with its edge and shadow |
| Hover | – | No change |
| Pressed | `:active` on a link or button card | Artwork dimmed by a tenth |

Three values were fitted again from the pixels and differ from the measured spec's first
fits. The shadows are `0 4px 7px` black at 25% (landscape), `0 4px 6px -2.5px` at 18%
(square) and `0 4px 15px` at 8% (episode), each the same in both appearances. The landscape
card's hairline is a grey at 20%, lighter than dark artwork and darker than light artwork, and
is absent along the bottom edge. TV's corner is a continuous curve, which `border-radius`
cannot draw: the spec's circular fit is 11 pt, and 12px matches the pixels best.

## Sizes

Cards have no size of their own: the section sizes them.

| Layout | Without `columns` | With `columns` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `grid` | as many columns of at least `minCardWidth` as fit, sharing the width | exactly that many columns, sharing the width |
| `shelf` | every card `minCardWidth` wide | that many cards fill the width; the rest scroll in |

Measured widths, for `minCardWidth` or `columns`: TV shows four landscape cards of 288.5 pt in
1214 pt (`columns={4}`); Music's album cell is 168 pt, the default; Podcasts' episode card is
252 pt; the App Store has three lockup columns of 301 pt.

The section includes its side margins, 40 pt, so that a shelf scrolls under them, and 32 pt
below its cards. In a container that already has margins, set `--mac-card-grid-margin: 0px`.
A shelf pads itself by at least 14 pt at the sides so that the cards' shadows are not cut off;
with a smaller margin that padding reaches outside the section.

The header is 48 pt with TV's type. The App Store and Podcasts set theirs differently; use
the variables:

| | Title | Action | Colours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TV (default) | 18 pt Bold | 15 pt | title `#0c0c0c` / `#e9e9e9`, line `#f4f4f4` / `#282a2c` |
| App Store | 20.5 pt Semibold, header 44 pt (`11px 0 8px`) | 15.5 pt | title as `--mac-color-label`, line as `--mac-color-grid`, action `#0884ff` in Dark Aqua |
| Podcasts | 18.5 pt Bold | 15.5 pt | title as `--mac-color-label`, line as `--mac-color-grid` |

`CardGrid` renders `<section class="mac-card-grid">` with a header
(`mac-card-grid-header`, `mac-card-grid-title`) and the list (`mac-card-grid-list`), whose
items are `mac-card-grid-item`. A card is `mac-card-grid-card` with the parts
`mac-card-grid-card-artwork`, `-text`, `-caption`, `-title`, `-subtitle`, `-footnote` and
`-accessory`.

| Attribute | On | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `data-separator` | section | present or absent | Reflects `separator`. |
| `data-layout` | list | `grid`, `shelf` | Reflects `layout`. |
| `data-columns` | list | a number, or absent | Reflects `columns`. |
| `data-variant` | card | `landscape`, `square`, `episode`, `lockup` | Reflects `variant`. |
| `data-force` | card, action, list | `pressed`, `focus` | Documentation and visual tests only: pins a transient state. |

Set these on the section or on an ancestor:

| CSS variable | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--mac-card-grid-margin` | `40px` | Side margin of the section. |
| `--mac-card-grid-gap` | `20px` | Distance between cards; set by `gap`. |
| `--mac-card-grid-row-gap` | the gap | Distance between rows of a grid. |
| `--mac-card-grid-min-card-width` | `168px` | Narrowest card; set by `minCardWidth`. |
| `--mac-card-grid-columns` | none | Fixed number of columns; set by `columns`. |
| `--mac-card-grid-header-padding` | `12.5px 0 13.5px` | Padding of the header around the title's line. |
| `--mac-card-grid-title-font` | 18 pt Bold on a 22 pt line | Font of the title. |
| `--mac-card-grid-action-font` | 15 pt on an 18 pt line | Font of the action. Keep its line shorter than the title's. |
| `--mac-card-grid-title` | `#0c0c0c`, `#e9e9e9` in Dark Aqua | Colour of the title. |
| `--mac-card-grid-action` | `#027aff` | Colour of the action. |
| `--mac-card-grid-separator` | `#f4f4f4`, `#282a2c` in Dark Aqua | Colour of the line above the header. |

## Accessibility

- The section is a `<section>` named by its title, which is a heading (`<h2>` unless
  `headingLevel` says otherwise). A section without a title needs an `aria-label`.
- The cards are a list (`role="list"`, each card in a `role="listitem"`), so assistive
  technology announces how many there are.
- A card is static unless you render it as a link or a button with `render`. Then Tab moves
  from card to card and Return (and Space, for a button) activates it. The arrow keys do not
  move between cards, as they do in an AppKit collection view.
- A link or button card is named by its text. A card without text, as in TV, needs an
  `aria-label`. Leave the `alt` of its artwork empty when the text already names it.
- Do not render a lockup that has an accessory as a link or a button: a button inside a link
  is not valid. Make its title a link instead.
- A shelf scrolls sideways. It is a tab stop named after its section and shows the focus ring
  inside its box; while it has the focus the left and right arrow keys scroll it by one card
  and Home and End go to either end, also when its cards are static. When its cards are links
  or buttons, focusing one scrolls it into view as well.
- The focus ring is drawn only in a key window. The shelf has no scroll animation of its own.
- In forced-colours mode the hairline of the artwork is drawn in a system colour.

The two corner comparisons mask nothing, because the artwork of TV's third card is white at
its edges in both captures; they and the rows with the text masked count a pixel as differing
at a colour distance of 0.05, half the default, and each has a 2x budget about 0.05
percentage points above its score. With a corner of 11px or 12.5px instead of 12px the dark
corner comparisons fail. In the album, episode and lockup rows the text is other words than
the capture's, in the fallback font, which is what the unmasked scores show. The lockup
example sets the App Store's header through the variables, and its list is 943 pt wide in the
945 pt column, as measured.

No screenshot shows a pressed or focused card or a window that is not key. Those states are
checked in a real browser against values from the spec (the `expect` comparisons): the
example is pressed with the mouse or focused with Tab, and chosen pixels must have the
expected colour.

| State | What is checked | Source of the values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| At rest | The hairline along the top and the side, none along the bottom, the shadow at 0.5 and 5 pt under the card | Measured |
| Pressed | White artwork at 90% brightness; the other card untouched | Inferred |
| Keyboard focus | Ring colour above the artwork and that it ends 2.5 pt out | Ring measured on a text field; that a card shows it, inferred |
| Inactive and main window | No focus ring | The library's rule; no screenshot |
| Shelf with keyboard focus | Ring inside the shelf's edge; the right arrow key scrolls a shelf of static cards by one card, End to its end | Inferred: no screenshot shows keyboard focus on a shelf |
