A popover (`NSPopover`) shows content about one thing on screen without leaving the window: the
details of a Calendar event, of a reminder, of a place on a map. The parts are built on Base
UI's [Popover](https://base-ui.com/react/components/popover) and keep its anatomy;
`PopoverPopup` combines Base UI's portal, positioner, popup and arrow, because a Catalina
popover is always assembled the same way.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Popover, PopoverDescription, PopoverPopup, PopoverTitle, PopoverTrigger } from "macalicious/popover";
import { PushButton } from "macalicious/push-button";
import "macalicious/styles.css";

<Popover>
  <PopoverTrigger render={<PushButton />}>Show Event</PopoverTrigger>
  <PopoverPopup side="right" style={{ width: 260 }}>
    <PopoverTitle>Make Catalina Screenshots</PopoverTitle>
    <PopoverDescription>Oct 3, 2019 2 PM to 4:45 PM</PopoverDescription>
  </PopoverPopup>
</Popover>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add popover`.

- `PopoverTrigger` renders a `<button>`; pass the real control with `render`. To point at
  something that is not a control, such as an event block or a table row, leave the trigger
  out, control the popover with `open` and `onOpenChange`, and give `PopoverPopup` an `anchor`:
  an element, a ref or a rectangle.
- `side` is NSPopover's preferred edge, the side of the anchor the popover appears on, and
  `align` its alignment along that side. With no room there the popover moves to the opposite
  side, and the arrow follows the anchor.
- The content is yours to lay out. It has 12 pt of padding; set `--mac-popover-padding` to `0`
  for content that reaches the edges, such as full-width separators. `PopoverTitle` and
  `PopoverDescription` name and describe the popover; a popover without a title needs an
  `aria-label`.
- `PopoverSeparator` is a line between groups of content, in the colour lines have on the
  popover: `#dbdbdb` in Aqua and `#4b4b4b` in Dark Aqua, lighter there than a window's
  [Separator](/components/separator) would be. It is as wide as its container; give it a
  margin to inset it, as Reminders does (14 pt).
- The material is translucent, and a page can only blend it with what is behind it in the
  page. The captures record it as `#f2f2f2` and `#282828` over every backdrop measured, so to
  match one, set the material's opaque form on the popup:
  `style={{ "--mac-material-popover": "var(--mac-material-popover-opaque)" }}`.
- It is transient: a click outside, the focus moving elsewhere or Escape closes it. For one
  that stays until the application closes it, cancel those reasons:

```tsx
import { Popover } from "macalicious/popover";

<Popover
  onOpenChange={(open, details) => {
    if (!open && (details.reason === "outside-press" || details.reason === "focus-out")) {
      details.cancel();
    }
  }}
>
  …
</Popover>
```

## Guidelines

From the Human Interface Guidelines for macOS,
[Popovers](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503140859/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/windows-and-views/popovers/):

- Use a popover for a small amount of content or a few controls that belong to one thing on
  screen. It is not an alert: do not use it to warn.
- Point the arrow as directly as possible at the element that opened the popover, and do not
  cover that element.
- Show one popover at a time, and never open a popover from a popover.
- Close a popover when people click outside it or finish what it offers; add a Done or a
  Cancel button only when losing the work in it would matter.
- Keep it small. Content that needs room belongs in a window or a sheet.

## Sizes

A popover is as large as its content; set a width with `style`. The corner radius is 5 pt as
drawn (6 pt nominal), and the arrow is 13 pt long on a 29 pt base. Calendar's event popover is
289 pt wide, Reminders' detail popover 290 pt and Maps' place card 320 pt.

## Accessibility

- The popover is a `dialog` that is not modal, named by its `PopoverTitle` (or `aria-label`)
  and described by its `PopoverDescription`. The trigger carries `aria-expanded` and points to
  it.
- The focus moves into the popover when it opens and returns to the trigger when it closes.
  Escape closes it. Tab leaves it for the rest of the page, which closes a transient popover.
- `modal` on `Popover` keeps the focus inside (`"trap-focus"`) or also blocks the rest of the
  page (`true`); a Catalina popover is neither.
- The arrow is decoration and hidden from assistive technology.
- With reduced motion the popover fades without scaling. In forced colours it has a border and
  the arrow a solid outline.

## Visual comparison

`popover.visual.json` compares two parts of Calendar's event popover in each appearance: its
right edge with both corners, the hairline, the rim, the separators and the shadow, where the
popover lies beyond the Calendar window; and the arrow on its left edge. The event's text and
controls are Calendar's content and are left out of the examples, so no text is in the crops
and there are no masked twins. The capture records the popover's material already blended with
what was behind it, `#282828` in Dark Aqua also over the capture's empty margin, so the dark
example uses the opaque form of the material.

The arrow is the weakest part: its outline is fitted by hand to the capture, and the join of its
rim and shadow with the panel's differs from AppKit's by a pixel in places.

`arrow-up-light` and `arrow-up-dark` open a popover below its anchor and check pixels: the
arrow is filled, turned to point up (read in Dark Aqua, where the page beside the tip differs
from the fill) and joined to the panel. They pass at 1x and 2x.

Measured: the fill, the outer line, the rim, the shadow, the corner radius, the arrow's size and
outline on the left edge. Provisional: the arrow on the other three edges, a popover without an
arrow, the 12 pt default padding, the animation, the reduced-motion and forced-colours looks.
Not built: a detachable popover, which becomes a panel when dragged away.
