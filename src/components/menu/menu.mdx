AppKit's `NSMenu`. The parts are built on [Base UI's Menu](https://base-ui.com/react/components/menu),
which supplies the roles, focus management, arrow-key navigation, type-select and dismissal.
`MenuPopup` combines Base UI's portal, positioner and popup, because a Catalina menu is always
assembled the same way. The rows are [menu items](/components/menu-item); a row that opens
another menu is a [submenu](/components/submenu).

## Usage

```tsx
import { Menu, MenuPopup, MenuTrigger } from "macalicious/menu";
import { MenuItem, MenuSeparator } from "macalicious/menu-item";
import { PushButton } from "macalicious/push-button";
import "macalicious/push-button.css";
import "macalicious/menu.css";
import "macalicious/menu-item.css";

<Menu>
  <MenuTrigger render={<PushButton>Actions</PushButton>} />
  <MenuPopup>
    <MenuItem keyEquivalent="command+n" onClick={newFolder}>New Folder</MenuItem>
    <MenuSeparator />
    <MenuItem keyEquivalent="command+delete" onClick={moveToTrash}>Move to Trash</MenuItem>
  </MenuPopup>
</Menu>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add menu menu-item`.

`MenuTrigger` renders an unstyled `<button>`: a menu has no bezel of its own, so pass the
control that opens it with `render`. The menu is in the document only while it is open, and is
rendered in a portal on `<body>` unless `container` names another element.

`MenuPopup` works under any Base UI menu root, so the same surface serves a menu bar, a
contextual menu and a submenu. Its classes select on attributes only, so the surface can also
be put on a popup that is not a menu: give a Base UI Select or Combobox popup
`className="mac-menu"`, its positioner `className="mac-menu-positioner"`, and its items the
classes of a [menu item](/components/menu-item).

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines,
[Menu Anatomy](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503171533/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/menus/menu-anatomy/):

- A menu stays open until the user chooses an item, moves to another menu or clicks outside it.
- Group related items and separate the groups with separators; put the most used items first.
- Dim an item that is not available instead of removing it, so that the menu keeps its shape.
- Menus are translucent; do not give one an opaque background of your own.
- Keep a menu short enough to fit the screen; one that does not fit scrolls.

## Variants and states

<Example name="attachment" both />

| Prop or state | Look | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| detached (default) | Four corners of 5 pt, a rim all round, 4 pt of padding above and below the rows | Measured (Print and Contacts menus) |
| `attached` | Square top corners, no rim along the top edge; in Aqua the bar's shadow line runs over the first row of pixels | Measured (Apple, Finder and Go menus) |
| Material | The menu tint over a blurred, saturated copy of what is behind it; opaque under `prefers-reduced-transparency` | Measured, computed |
| Rim | White at 17%, 0.5 pt in Aqua; white at 15%, 1 pt in Dark Aqua; it lies over a highlighted row | Measured |
| Hairline and shadow | A 0.5 pt outline and one soft shadow, the same in both appearances | Measured, computed |
| Opening | Appears at once | Provisional |
| Closing | Fades out over 0.25 s after a choice | Provisional |
| Window not key | Unchanged: an open menu is a window of its own and is key | Catalog |

The blink of the chosen item before the menu closes and the scroll arrows of a menu taller
than the screen are not reproduced: a tall menu scrolls with the wheel or the arrow keys.

## Sizes

<Example name="sizes" both />

Without `size` a menu uses the 14 pt menu font with 19 pt rows, as menu bar and contextual
menus do. The menu of a pop-up button takes the font of its control: `size="regular"` is 13 pt
with 18 pt rows and `size="small"` 12 pt with 17 pt rows (both measured), `size="mini"` 11 pt
with 16 pt rows (provisional). The row height is always the font size plus 5.

A menu is as wide as its widest item. Set `--mac-menu-min-width` or a `width` on the popup for
a minimum or fixed width, for example the width of the control that opens it.

## API

`Menu` takes the props of Base UI's Menu.Root (`open`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange`, `modal`,
`disabled`, `loopFocus`, `handle`) and `MenuTrigger` those of Menu.Trigger. `MenuPopup` takes
the props of Menu.Popup and these:

<PropsTable name="MenuPopupProps" />

| Attribute | On | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `data-attached` | popup | present or absent | Reflects `attached`. |
| `data-size` | popup | `regular`, `small`, `mini`, or absent | Reflects `size`. |
| `data-appearance` | positioner | `dark` or absent | Set for the menus of a Dock menu, through `MenuPopupContext`. |
| `data-key-equivalents` | popup | `hidden` or absent | Set for the menus of a contextual or Dock menu, through `MenuPopupContext`. |
| `data-open`, `data-closed` | popup | present or absent | Set by Base UI. |
| `data-starting-style`, `data-ending-style` | popup | present or absent | Set by Base UI while it appears and fades. |
| `data-side`, `data-align` | popup | a side, an alignment | Where the menu was placed. |
| `data-popup-open` | trigger | present or absent | The trigger's menu is open. |

| CSS variable | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `--mac-menu-min-width` | Minimum width of the menu, set on the popup. |
| `--mac-menu-fade` | The transition a menu closes with, `opacity 0.25s`. With `prefers-reduced-motion` it is `none`, for the menu, its rim and the pointer of a Dock menu. |

Classes: `.mac-menu` (the popup) and `.mac-menu-positioner`. The positioner draws the rim over
the rows, so a popup of another primitive that uses the surface needs both.

`MenuPopupContext` carries what a kind of menu decides for every menu opened from it
(`appearance: "dark"`, `keyEquivalents: "hidden"`). A submenu is rendered in a portal of its
own, so it cannot inherit these from its parent's element; `MenuPopup` reads them from the
context instead. [Dock](/components/dock-menu) and [contextual](/components/contextual-menu)
menus provide it; provide it around a menu's items to do the same in a menu of your own. Those
two add their own choice to what a provider around them decided: a contextual menu inside a
dark provider is dark, and so are its submenus.

## Accessibility

- The popup has `role="menu"`; the trigger has `aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded`.
  A trigger without visible text needs an `aria-label`.
- Down Arrow, Return or Space on the trigger opens the menu. Up and Down move the highlight
  and wrap, Home and End go to the first and last item, typing the start of a title selects
  it, Return or Space chooses, and Escape closes the menu and returns focus to the trigger.
- Disabled items and separators are passed over by the arrow keys and by type-select, as in
  Catalina. See [Menu Item](/components/menu-item) for what that means for assistive technology.
- While a menu opened by a click is open it is modal, as in AppKit: the page behind it does
  not scroll or take clicks. `modal={false}` on `Menu` switches that off.
- With `prefers-reduced-transparency` the material is opaque; under `forced-colors` the menu
  has a solid border. The fade is an opacity change only, and with `prefers-reduced-motion` a
  menu closes at once.

## Visual comparison

`npm run visual -- menu/` renders the Apple menu of the Apple-Menu screenshots, attached under
the menu bar's lower edge with its first item highlighted, and compares it with the screenshots
in both appearances (`menu.visual.json`). Scores at 2x, in Chromium on Linux with the fallback
font:

| Crop | Aqua, whole crop | Aqua, drawing only | Dark Aqua, whole crop | Dark Aqua, drawing only |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Apple menu, pixels that differ | 6.79% | 0.22% | 7.03% | 0.90% |
| Apple menu, mean Δ of 255 | 13.12 | 4.91 | 11.47 | 3.38 |

"Drawing only" masks the titles and key equivalents and counts a pixel as differing at a colour
distance of 0.05, half the default. What remains in the whole-crop scores is the text, set in
the fallback font instead of San Francisco.

What is not exact:

- **The desktop picture.** The menu is compared over a gradient of the sky's colours, a
  stand-in for Apple's picture, which is not shipped.
- **The material.** Most of the mean Δ of the drawing is the fill: over the Day sky the shared
  menu material comes out about 10 levels redder and 6 levels less blue than the capture (4
  levels off in Dark Aqua). The recipe is one blur, one saturation and one tint; AppKit's is
  several layers ([Fidelity limits](/fidelity)).
- **Vibrant text.** Over the sky Catalina draws titles navy; here they are black at 85%.

Provisional: the mini size, the fade, and the opaque material under reduced transparency.
