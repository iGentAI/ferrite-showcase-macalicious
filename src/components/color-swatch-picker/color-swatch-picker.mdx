System Preferences › General chooses the accent colour from a row of eight discs and the
appearance from three pictures. Both are single-choice groups, so both are radio groups here:
`ColorSwatchPicker` is built on
[Base UI's Radio Group](https://base-ui.com/react/components/radio), and `ColorSwatch` and
`ThumbnailSwatch` on its Radio. AppKit has no public control for either; the spec measures the
General pane.

## Usage

```tsx
import { ColorSwatch, ColorSwatchPicker } from "macalicious/color-swatch-picker";
import "macalicious/color-swatch-picker.css";

<ColorSwatchPicker aria-label="Accent color" value={accent} onValueChange={setAccent}>
  <ColorSwatch value="blue" aria-label="Blue" />
  <ColorSwatch value="purple" aria-label="Purple" />
  <ColorSwatch value="graphite" aria-label="Graphite" />
</ColorSwatchPicker>
```

A swatch whose `value` is one of Catalina's eight accent names is drawn with the fill and edge
measured for it. Give any other swatch a `color`. Or copy the source into your project:
`npx macalicious add color-swatch-picker`. See [Installation](/installation).

## Guidelines

The Human Interface Guidelines have no page for this control; these follow what they say about
[radio buttons](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503145009/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/buttons/radio-buttons/)
and [colour](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503145009/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/visual-design/color/):

- Use it for a small, fixed set of mutually exclusive choices that are best told apart by
  sight. It is not a colour well: it does not open a colour panel.
- Apply the choice immediately, as preferences do.
- Introduce the row with a label that ends in a colon, right-aligned with the other labels of
  the pane.
- Never rely on hue alone: every swatch has a name, and the chosen one carries a dot or a ring.

## Variants and states

Pressed and keyboard-focused are transient, so this gallery pins them. In an application they
appear on their own.

<Example name="states" both hideCode />

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | – | 16 pt disc with a 1 pt edge, darker than the fill in Aqua and lighter in Dark Aqua |
| Chosen | the group's `value` | 5.5 pt white dot (swatch); 1.5 pt accent ring outside the picture (thumbnail) |
| Pressed | `:active` | The swatch or picture darkens to 88% |
| Keyboard focus | `:focus-visible` | Focus ring, as well as the dot or ring |
| Disabled | `disabled` on the group or a swatch | Half opacity |
| Window not key | an ancestor has `data-window-state="main"` or `"inactive"` | No focus ring; swatches keep their colours and the dot; a thumbnail's ring turns grey |

### Any colour

`color` takes any CSS colour; the edge is derived from it.

### Thumbnails

`ThumbnailSwatch` is a 67 x 44 pt picture with a caption, for a choice such as Light, Dark and
Auto. The picture is its children: an image, an inline SVG or any element. The library ships no
artwork; Catalina's thumbnails are miniature desktops, and this example uses gradients in
their place.

## Sizes

The control has one size: 16 pt swatches 8 pt apart, and 67 x 44 pt thumbnails 19 pt apart.
Both are measured.

## Accessibility

- The group has `role="radiogroup"` and needs a name: `aria-label`, or `aria-labelledby`
  pointing at its introductory label.
- A swatch has no text, so it needs an `aria-label` with the name of its colour, not its value.
  A thumbnail is named by its caption; mark its picture decorative.
- The group is one tab stop. The arrow keys move focus and the choice together and wrap
  around; Space chooses the focused swatch.
- The chosen swatch is marked by a dot and the chosen thumbnail by a ring, not by colour alone,
  and the state is carried by `aria-checked`.
- In forced-colours mode the swatches and a thumbnail's picture keep their colours, which are
  their content, and the rings are drawn with system colours.

## Visual comparison

`npm run visual -- color-swatch-picker` compares the swatch row with System Preferences ›
General and with the General pane of all eight accent screenshots, and the thumbnails with the
Appearance row, in both appearances (`color-swatch-picker.visual.json`). Swatch fills, edges,
the dot and its shadow, the thumbnail ring and hairlines are measured from those screenshots.
Share of pixels that differ at 2x at a colour distance of 0.05, half the default, and the mean
difference per channel out of 255, in Chromium on Linux:

| Crop | Aqua | Dark Aqua |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General, eight swatches, Blue chosen | 0.16%, Δ 0.41 | 0.21%, Δ 0.44 |
| Accent screenshots, each accent chosen (eight crops) | 0.16% to 0.25%, Δ 0.40 to 0.42 | 0.20% to 0.22%, Δ 0.43 |
| Appearance thumbnails, pictures and captions masked | 0.62%, Δ 0.25 | 0.20%, Δ 0.44 |

The thumbnail comparison masks the three pictures, which are Apple's artwork, and the captions,
which are set in the fallback font off Apple platforms: it scores the accent ring, the
hairlines around the other pictures and where they sit. The caption's position is therefore
not scored. At 1x the crops score 4% to 9%: there is no 1x reference, so the 2x screenshot is
averaged down ([Fidelity limits](/fidelity)).

No screenshot shows a pressed, focused or disabled swatch or thumbnail, a swatch in a window
that is not key, or a swatch of another colour. Those are checked in a real browser against
values from the spec and the tokens (the `expect` comparisons): the example is pressed with the
mouse or focused with Tab, and chosen pixels must have the expected colour.

| State | What is checked | Source of the values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pressed | The swatch at 88% of its fill | Inferred |
| Keyboard focus | Ring colour and extent, in blue and purple | Measured on a text field's ring |
| Main and inactive window | No focus ring; fill and dot unchanged; a thumbnail's ring grey | Inferred |
| Disabled, other colour | Half opacity; a custom colour's fill and derived edge | Inferred; edge recipe computed from the sixteen measured edges |
| Thumbnail, purple accent | The ring is the accent colour | Measured accent colour |
| Thumbnail, pressed | The picture at 88%; the chosen thumbnail keeps its picture and ring | Inferred |
| Thumbnail, keyboard focus | Focus ring colour and extent outside the accent ring, which stays | Ring colour measured on a text field; that a thumbnail shows it is inferred |
| Thumbnail, disabled | Picture and accent ring at half opacity, chosen or not | Inferred |

These checks hold the component to the spec; where the spec infers a value, they do not show
that Catalina looks that way.
