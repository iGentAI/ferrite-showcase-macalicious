AppKit calls this control a *radio button* (`NSButton` of the radio type); sibling radio
buttons form a group. Here the group is explicit: `RadioGroup` is built on
[Base UI's Radio Group](https://base-ui.com/react/components/radio) and `RadioButton` on its
Radio, which supply the `radiogroup` and `radio` roles, the single tab stop, the arrow keys and
the hidden form input. The circle, its dot and the dash are drawn in CSS.

## Usage

```tsx
import { RadioButton, RadioGroup } from "macalicious/radio-button";
import "macalicious/radio-button.css";

<RadioGroup defaultValue="automatic" aria-label="Show scroll bars">
  <RadioButton value="automatic">Automatically based on mouse or trackpad</RadioButton>
  <RadioButton value="scrolling">When scrolling</RadioButton>
  <RadioButton value="always">Always</RadioButton>
</RadioGroup>
```

A button's children are its title: clicking it selects the button, and it is the accessible
name. Control the group with `value` and `onValueChange`. Or copy the source into your
project: `npx macalicious add radio-button`. See [Installation](/installation).

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines for
[radio buttons](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503212902/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/buttons/radio-buttons/):

- Use radio buttons for two to five related, mutually exclusive choices. For more, use a pop-up
  button; for a single on or off choice, a [checkbox](/components/checkbox).
- Radio buttons present options; they never start an action. They belong in a view, not in a
  toolbar or a status bar.
- Title each button in sentence case, without ending punctuation.
- An introductory label ends with a colon and shares the baseline of the first button's title.
- In a horizontal group, give every button the same width, that of the longest title, so the
  spacing is constant.

## Variants and states

Pressed and keyboard-focused are transient, so this gallery pins them. In an application they
appear on their own.

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Off | – | White well with an inner shadow (a translucent white gradient in Dark Aqua) |
| On | the group's `value` | Accent fill, white dot (white at 85% in Dark Aqua) |
| Mixed | `mixed` | Accent fill, 8 x 2 pt dash; a look only (see Accessibility) |
| Pressed | `:active` | The well darkens (lightens in Dark Aqua); an accent fill deepens |
| Keyboard focus | `:focus-visible` | Focus ring around the circle |
| Disabled | `disabled` on the group or a button | Circle at half opacity, title at 25% |
| Window not key | an ancestor has `data-window-state="main"` or `"inactive"` | No accent: the selected button is drawn on the off well with a dark (light in Dark Aqua) dot; no focus ring |

### Orientation

A group is a column, 22 pt from one circle to the next. `orientation="horizontal"` lays it out
as a row with 10 pt between a title and the next circle, as in the Siri pane. The arrow keys
work the same in both.

## Accessibility

- The group has `role="radiogroup"` and needs a name: `aria-label`, or `aria-labelledby`
  pointing at its introductory label. Each button has `role="radio"` and `aria-checked`.
- The group is one tab stop: Tab moves focus to the selected button, or to the first one when
  none is selected. The arrow keys move focus and the selection together and wrap around; Space
  selects the focused button.
- A disabled button is skipped by the arrow keys. A disabled group keeps its tab stop, as in
  Base UI, so that it can still be found with the keyboard, but nothing in it can be changed.
- The title is a button's accessible name. A button without a title needs `aria-label` or
  `aria-labelledby`.
- `mixed` changes the look only. ARIA has no mixed radio button, so say what the dash means in
  the title or with `aria-describedby`.
- In forced-colours mode the circle, the dot and the dash are drawn with system colours, so an
  on or mixed button still differs from an off one; a visual check in a real browser covers it.
