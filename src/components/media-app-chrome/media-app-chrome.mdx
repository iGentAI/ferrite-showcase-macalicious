Music and Podcasts, the apps that replaced iTunes in Catalina, have no title bar. The sidebar
runs the height of the window and holds the window controls and a search field; a 55 pt
playback bar sits over the content pane only. This component is that layout and the bar's own
controls. It is custom chrome, so there is no single AppKit class behind it: the bar is built on
[Base UI's Toolbar](https://base-ui.com/react/components/toolbar) and the volume control on
[Base UI's Slider](https://base-ui.com/react/components/slider). It plays nothing; you supply
the state.

## Usage

```tsx
import {
  MediaAppChrome,
  MediaAppChromeBar,
  MediaAppChromeButton,
  MediaAppChromeControls,
  MediaAppChromeDisplay,
  MediaAppChromePane,
  MediaAppChromeSidebar,
  MediaAppChromeTransport,
  MediaAppChromeVolume,
} from "macalicious/media-app-chrome";
import { NowPlayingLcd } from "macalicious/now-playing-lcd";
import { SearchField } from "macalicious/search-field";
import { Sidebar } from "macalicious/sidebar";
import { Window } from "macalicious/window";
import { WindowControls } from "macalicious/window-controls";
import "macalicious/media-app-chrome.css";

<Window width={1220} height={720} aria-label="Music">
  <MediaAppChrome>
    <MediaAppChromeSidebar controls={<WindowControls />} search={<SearchField variant="sidebar" />}>
      <Sidebar aria-label="Library">{/* sections and items */}</Sidebar>
    </MediaAppChromeSidebar>
    <MediaAppChromeBar aria-label="Playback controls">
      <MediaAppChromeTransport>
        <MediaAppChromeButton glyph="previous" aria-label="Previous" />
        <MediaAppChromeButton glyph="play" aria-label="Play" />
        <MediaAppChromeButton glyph="next" aria-label="Next" />
      </MediaAppChromeTransport>
      <MediaAppChromeDisplay>
        <NowPlayingLcd />
      </MediaAppChromeDisplay>
      <MediaAppChromeControls>
        <MediaAppChromeVolume defaultValue={100}>
          <MediaAppChromeButton glyph="output" aria-label="Audio output" />
        </MediaAppChromeVolume>
        <MediaAppChromeButton glyph="lyrics" aria-label="Lyrics" />
        <MediaAppChromeButton glyph="up-next" aria-label="Up Next" />
      </MediaAppChromeControls>
    </MediaAppChromeBar>
    <MediaAppChromePane>{/* content */}</MediaAppChromePane>
  </MediaAppChrome>
</Window>
```

`MediaAppChrome` is the child of a [Window](/components/window), in place of a title bar and a
body. Its three children place themselves: the sidebar column, the bar and the pane. Inside the
bar, `MediaAppChromeTransport`, `MediaAppChromeDisplay` and `MediaAppChromeControls` are the
three columns; the display takes 40% of the bar, as measured in both apps, and holds a
[Now Playing Lcd](/components/now-playing-lcd). The sidebar's list is a
[Sidebar](/components/sidebar); give it and `MediaAppChromeSidebar` the same `material`.
`MediaAppChromeButton` registers with the bar's keyboard navigation, so it is used only inside
a `MediaAppChromeBar`.

The second layout of the media apps, the one of TV and Books, is a standard unified toolbar
with a centred segmented control and a search field. It needs nothing from this component:
build it from [Title Bar](/components/title-bar), [Toolbar](/components/toolbar),
[Toolbar Segmented Control](/components/toolbar-segmented-control) and
[Toolbar Search Field](/components/toolbar-search-field).

## Guidelines

- Use a sidebar for the top-level places of the app, grouped under short titles, and let the
  selected row decide what the pane shows.
- Keep the bar to playback: transport, the display of what is playing, volume and at most two
  trailing buttons. Every one of them must also be a menu command.
- Disable the previous and next buttons, rather than hiding them, when nothing is loaded.
- Swap the play glyph for the pause glyph while something plays, and change the button's name
  with it.

## Sizes

There is no `size` prop. The bar is 55 pt high and its glyphs keep their measured boxes. The
chrome fills the window; the sidebar column is `sidebarWidth` points wide (220 by default), and
the display starts 27.7% into the bar and takes 40% of it, which reproduces both apps at their
captured widths. The volume group floats between the display and the trailing buttons. In a
bar too narrow for that (under about 600 pt with two trailing buttons) the side columns keep
the width of their controls and the display gives way.

## Accessibility

- The bar is a `toolbar`; give it an `aria-label`. Its buttons are one tab stop, and the Left
  and Right arrow keys move between them. As in every Base UI toolbar, a disabled button can
  still be reached, so that a screen reader finds it.
- The transport buttons are a `group` named "Playback".
- Every button shows only a glyph and needs an `aria-label`. When play becomes pause, change
  the label too. For a toggle such as Lyrics, pass `aria-pressed`.
- The volume is a `slider` named "Volume" (`aria-label` changes it). It is a tab stop of its
  own, and the arrow keys, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End change its value.
- A seekable now-playing display in the bar keeps its arrow keys in the same way.
- The chrome does not bind Space to play and pause or Command-F to the search field, as the
  apps do. Bind them where your app handles its menu commands.

## Visual comparison

`media-app-chrome.visual.json` compares the whole playback bar, from the first point of the
pane to the window's right edge, with Music in both appearances, with Podcasts in Aqua (nothing
loaded, skip buttons disabled) and with Podcasts in Dark Aqua (an episode loaded, 15 and 30 in
the skip glyphs). Masked in each: the top point and the top right corner, which are the
window's rim and rounded corner in the capture, and Apple's marks, the show's artwork and the
text in the display. Two more comparisons render the Music bar in an inactive window and check
pixels against the values of the inactive Books window, and two render the public examples at
their own, narrower widths and check that the last trailing button is inside the window. Two
render the states example in an inactive window and check that a toggle that is on has lost
its accent colour, and one checks that a Sidebar row inside the chrome keeps its 18 pt inset.

Scores from `npm run visual -- media-app-chrome` in Chromium on Linux: the share of pixels that
differ at threshold 0.05, and the mean colour difference out of 255.

| `music-bar-dark` | Dark-Music (554, 76, 1998, 110) | 4.71% | 2.10 | 1.13% | 1.13 |

What remains is the glyphs. They are original drawings in the measured boxes, not Apple's
artwork: the audio-output glyph in particular is a different picture from the AirPlay mark, and
the numbers in the skip glyphs are set in the fallback font off Apple platforms.

Measured: the bar's height, gradient and separator, the divider after the sidebar, the
position and width of the display, the boxes, spacing and colours of every glyph, the volume
track and knob. Not compared with a screenshot: the sidebar column, whose rows are the
[Sidebar](/components/sidebar) component's and whose icons are Apple's; its header places the
window controls and the search field at the measured offsets. The sidebar divider is one
colour, black at 20%, which is Music's; Podcasts' is slightly darker in Aqua. Not reproduced:
the vibrancy of the sidebar over a desktop, unless the page has one behind the window.
