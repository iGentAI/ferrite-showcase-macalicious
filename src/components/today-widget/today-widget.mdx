The host of a Today extension (`NCWidgetProviding`): a translucent card with the app's icon
and name in a header band and content the widget draws itself. The same folder has the blocks
macOS puts above the widgets: rows of switches, the date and the summary. They go in the Today
view of a [Notification Center](/components/notification-center).

## Usage

```tsx
import { Switch } from "macalicious/switch";
import {
  TodayWidget,
  TodayWidgetDate,
  TodayWidgetRow,
  TodayWidgetSummary,
} from "macalicious/today-widget";
import "macalicious/switch.css";
import "macalicious/today-widget.css";

<TodayWidget aria-label="Quick settings">
  <TodayWidgetRow label="Do Not Disturb"><Switch /></TodayWidgetRow>
</TodayWidget>
<TodayWidgetDate weekday="Thursday," date="October 3" />
<TodayWidgetSummary><p>Partly Cloudy currently.</p></TodayWidgetSummary>
<TodayWidget title="Stocks" icon={<StocksIcon />}>…</TodayWidget>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add today-widget`. The widget does not
use the switch itself; import its stylesheet only when a row holds one.

The card has no blur of its own: it is an overlay on the material of the panel behind it, so
put it on a Notification Center or on a backdrop of your own.

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines for
[Notification Center Widgets](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503144232/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/extensions/notification-center-widgets/):

- Show a small amount of timely, glanceable information, or one focused function. A widget is
  not a small version of the app.
- Keep interaction minimal; a click on the content opens the app.
- Let the content decide the height at the fixed width, and do not scroll inside the card.
- Use the system's text colours over the vibrant card, and do not draw a background of your
  own.

## Variants and states

<Example name="system-cards" both hideCode />

| Variant | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Widget | `title`, optionally `icon` | 26 pt header band with a 14 pt icon and the name in upper case, then the content |
| System card | no `title` | The card alone |
| Switch row | `TodayWidgetRow` in a card | 42.5 pt row: a 16 pt glyph, a semibold label in upper case, the control at the trailing edge; a divider from the label between rows |
| Date | `TodayWidgetDate` | Two lines of 34 pt thin text straight on the panel |
| Summary | `TodayWidgetSummary` | 12 pt paragraphs straight on the panel, with an optional glyph beside the first |

The parts have no states of their own. Catalina blends the card with vibrancy, so one tint
cannot reproduce every backdrop: the fill is fitted to the cards at the top of the panel over
the sky of the default picture, and lower down, over the sea, the captured cards are darker
than this card would be.

Measured: the card, its edge, corner, header band, row heights, divider and the text colours,
in both appearances. The corner is drawn with 7 pt, which matches the captured corner; the
spec's figure of 4 pt was read from a smaller crop. Provisional: nothing drawn; the glyphs of
the rows are the application's.

## Sizes

One size: 322 pt wide, as tall as the content. `--mac-today-widget-width` changes the width of
the card, the date and the summary.

## Accessibility

- The card is a `<section>`: a `region` named by its title. A card without a title needs an
  `aria-label`.
- The icon and the glyphs are decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
- A switch row is a `<label>`: its label names the control inside it, and a click anywhere on
  the row operates the control. The control keeps its own keyboard behaviour and focus ring.
- The date and the summary are plain text.
- In forced-colours mode the card has a border.

## Visual comparison

`visual-switches` reproduces the card of switches at the top of the Today view over the panel
colours measured around it. The row labels are masked (the fallback font off Apple platforms)
together with the two glyphs, which are Apple artwork; the switches are compared. Results in
Chromium on Linux, at a colour distance of 0.05:

| Comparison | 2x | 1x |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Switches card, Aqua | 0.42% of pixels differ | 0.98% |
| Switches card, Dark Aqua | 0.53% | 1.16% |

What differs is the outline of the second switch, which the capture draws half a point higher
in its row. `visual-header` checks the header band and the body of a titled widget against
the card's tokens composited over the panel colour, because the captured widgets with a header
lie over the part of the picture that one tint does not reproduce (see Variants and states).

Not compared: the date and the summary, which are text alone, and the content of the Calendar,
Stocks and Weather widgets, which is the application's.
