AppKit windows can be merged into tabs; the bar appears when a window has two or more. It is
built on [Base UI's Tabs](https://base-ui.com/react/components/tabs): the bar is the Tabs root
and its list in one element, so it takes `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange` and
supplies the tab semantics and arrow keys. It renders no panels: the window's body shows the
selected tab's content.

## Usage

```tsx
import { WindowTab, WindowTabBar } from "macalicious/window-tab-bar";
import "macalicious/window-tab-bar.css";

<WindowTabBar aria-label="Tabs" value={tab} onValueChange={setTab} onNewTab={addTab}>
  <WindowTab value="recents">Recents</WindowTab>
  <WindowTab value="home">Home</WindowTab>
</WindowTabBar>
```

Place it directly under the [Title Bar](/components/title-bar), and give that bar
`divider="bar"`.

## Guidelines

Not verified: this section paraphrases Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and was not checked
against Apple's text.

- Show the bar only when there are two or more tabs.
- Title a tab as its window would be titled.
- Tabs share the width equally; a long title is cut at the end.

## Variants and states

<Example name="states" both hideCode />

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selected tab | its value is the bar's value | Lighter fill, the toolbar's colour, with a darker title |
| Other tabs | – | Darker fill, dimmer title |
| Under the pointer | `:hover`, also over the tab's close button | Fill between the two; the close button, if the tab has `onClose` (provisional) |
| Keyboard focus | `:focus-visible` | Focus ring, in the key window only (provisional) |
| Disabled | `disabled` on a tab | provisional |
| Inactive window | `data-window-state="inactive"` on an ancestor | The bar flattens and its titles dim |
| New-tab button | `onNewTab` | A 24 pt cell with a + at the trailing end |

## Sizes

One size: the bar is 25 pt high and as wide as the window. The tabs share the width equally.

A tab is a flex item with `flex: 1 1 0`, and `style` on a `WindowTab` goes to it, so a tab
can be given a share or a width of its own: `style={{ flexGrow: 2 }}` for twice a share,
`style={{ flex: "none", width: 361 }}` for a fixed width. The Finder Home captures need this:
their three tabs are 371, 360 and 348 pt wide in Aqua and 361, 349 and 338 pt in Dark Aqua,
falling by about 11.5 pt from the first tab to the last in both. No other capture shows window
tabs and the cause is not established, so equal widths stay the layout and the captured widths
are set per tab, as the comparison examples do.

## Accessibility

- A `tablist` of `tab`s; name it with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`. The bar renders no
  `tabpanel`: if the body should be announced as the tab's panel, give it `role="tabpanel"` and
  `aria-labelledby` yourself.
- One tab stop. The arrow keys move focus between tabs without selecting, so passing over a tab
  does not swap the window's content; Space or Return selects. `activateOnFocus` makes the
  arrow keys select as they move.
- A tab with `onClose` closes with Delete or Backspace while it has focus, and announces that
  shortcut (`aria-keyshortcuts`). It also has a close button: a native button named "Close"
  and the tab's title (`closeLabel` replaces "Close"). A button cannot sit inside a tab, and a
  tab list holds only tabs, so the buttons are rendered beside the tab list, after it, and
  laid over their tabs. They are out of the tab order, because Delete does the same from the
  keyboard, and visible only under the pointer or with focus.
- A tab that is closed while it has focus leaves the focus on the page: move it to the tab you
  select in its place.
- The new-tab button is a separate native button after the tab list, named "New Tab"
  (`newTabLabel`).
- Control-Tab, Command-T and Command-W are the application's to handle.

Measured: the 25 pt height, both fills, the dividers, the bottom rows under selected and
unselected tabs, the new-tab cell and its glyph, both title colours. The capture's tabs are
370.5, 360 and 348 pt wide; the component divides the width equally, as the spec concludes, so
the comparison example sets the captured widths. Provisional: hover, the close button, focus
and disabled, which no screenshot shows. Dragging a tab out into its own window is not
implemented.

A close button is kept over its tab by script, because it cannot be inside the tab. The bar
lays the buttons out again when a tab is added, removed or moved, when any tab or the bar
changes size, and before every frame while the pointer is in the bar. One case is left: a
style that moves the tabs without changing the size of any (a wider `gap` between tabs of
fixed widths) while the pointer is outside the bar. The close buttons then stay where they
were until the pointer enters the bar, which lays them out before one can be shown or pressed;
they are not visible in between.
