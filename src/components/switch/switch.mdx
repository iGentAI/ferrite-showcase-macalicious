AppKit calls this control a *switch* (`NSSwitch`, new in macOS 10.15). It is built on
[Base UI's Switch](https://base-ui.com/react/components/switch), which supplies the `switch`
role, the keyboard handling, the hidden input that takes part in forms, and the `render` prop.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Switch } from "macalicious/switch";
import "macalicious/switch.css";

<Switch aria-label="Do Not Disturb" checked={on} onCheckedChange={setOn} />
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add switch`. See
[Installation](/installation).

A switch has no title of its own. Put a `<label>` beside it and connect the two with `id` and
`htmlFor`; the `id` goes to the switch's input, so a click on the text toggles the switch:

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines for switches, as the measured spec summarises them.
Not verified: this text was not checked against Apple's page.

- A switch is on when its accent colour shows and off when it is colourless. Use it for a
  setting that takes effect at once.
- It has more visual weight than a checkbox. Use it for a setting that stands for a group of
  options, in the body of a view, and do not put it in a window frame.
- Do not replace existing checkboxes with switches. Catalina's own preference panes still use
  checkboxes; the only switch in the reference screenshots is Do Not Disturb in Notification
  Center.

## Variants and states

Pressed and keyboard-focused are transient, so this gallery pins them. In an application they
appear on their own.

<Example name="states" both hideCode />

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Off | – | Colourless capsule with an inner rim, knob at the leading end |
| On | `checked` or `defaultChecked` | Capsule filled with the accent colour, knob at the trailing end |
| Pressed | `:active` | The knob darkens (lightens in Dark Aqua) |
| Keyboard focus | `:focus-visible` | Focus ring in the accent colour around the capsule |
| Disabled | `disabled` | The whole control at half opacity |
| Window not key | an ancestor has `data-window-state="main"` or `"inactive"` | No accent: an on switch is a neutral grey, darker than off; no focus ring |

The knob slides in 0.2 s and the accent fill fades in with it; with
`prefers-reduced-motion` the knob jumps and the fill changes at once. The window state is not a
prop: see [Building a window](/building-a-window).

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `regular` | 38 x 21 pt | 20 pt, of which 19 pt fill | measured |
| `small` | 32 x 18 pt | 17 pt | provisional |
| `mini` | 26 x 15 pt | 14 pt | provisional |

Catalina's `NSSwitch` has the regular size only; small and mini switches arrived with macOS 11.
They are offered here because every sized control in the library takes `size`, and are drawn
in the Catalina style with the proportions of the regular one.

## Accent colour

The on fill follows the accent colour: set `data-accent` on any ancestor. See
[Theming](/theming). No screenshot shows a switch that is on, in any accent: the fill is the
one measured on the checkboxes of System Preferences › General, which the spec assigns to the
switch so that the two controls match.

## API

<PropsTable name="SwitchProps" />

| Attribute | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-size` | `regular`, `small`, `mini` | Reflects `size`. |
| `data-checked`, `data-unchecked` | present or absent | Set by Base UI for on and off. |
| `data-disabled`, `data-readonly`, `data-required` | present or absent | Set by Base UI. |
| `data-force` | `pressed`, `focus` | Documentation and visual tests only: pins a transient state. |

The knob is a `<span class="mac-switch-thumb">` (Base UI's `Switch.Thumb`) inside the root.

| CSS variable | Default (Aqua, Dark Aqua) | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--mac-switch-track-top`, `--mac-switch-track-bottom` | black at 8.6% to 4.1%, white at 7% | Fill of the off track, top to bottom. The values are measured on Notification Center's vibrant panel; on an opaque window the spec suggests, without a measurement, black at 9% and white at 10%. Set both on the switch or on any ancestor. |

## Accessibility

- The root has `role="switch"` and `aria-checked`; a hidden checkbox input carries the form
  value. Space and Enter toggle it; Tab reaches it.
- A switch needs an accessible name: a `<label>` connected with `htmlFor` or wrapped around
  it, `aria-label`, or `aria-labelledby`. Every example on this page has one.
- The state is carried by the knob's position as well as by colour, so it survives a window
  that is not key, the graphite accent and forced-colours mode, where the capsule gets a
  border in a system colour.
- A disabled switch leaves the tab order and has `aria-disabled`.
- The knob cannot be dragged, as it can in AppKit; a press anywhere on the switch toggles it.

## Visual comparison

One example reproduces the Do Not Disturb switch of Notification Center's Today view, off, the
only switch in the reference set, and is compared with it in both appearances by
`npm run visual` (`switch.visual.json`). The crop is
`10-15-Catalina-<Light|Dark>-Notification-Center-Today.png` at x=3234, y=178, 92 x 60 px. It
holds no text. A pixel counts as differing at a colour distance of 0.05, half the default.

| Crop | Aqua, 2x | Aqua, 1x | Dark Aqua, 2x | Dark Aqua, 1x |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Do Not Disturb, off | 0.13% (mean Δ 0.99) | 8.48% (mean Δ 3.52) | 0.40% (mean Δ 1.09) | 9.86% (mean Δ 5.69) |

What remains at 2x is antialiasing on the knob's edge. The mean Δ of about 1 comes from the
panel behind the switch, a vibrant material that the example approximates with two gradients.
The 1x scores are high because the switch sits on a half point in the screenshot (its top is
at y=187 px), so at 1x the browser must round its position, and because there is no 1x
reference: the 2x screenshot is averaged down ([Fidelity limits](/fidelity)).

No screenshot shows a switch that is on, pressed, focused, disabled or in a window that is not
key, or the small and mini sizes. Those states are checked in a real browser against values
from the spec and the tokens instead (the `expect` comparisons of `switch.visual.json`):

| State | What is checked | Source of the values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On | Fill colour, knob at the trailing end; blue, purple and graphite, each in both appearances | Fill measured on checkboxes; that the switch uses it, and the knob's travel of 17 pt, inferred |
| Off on a window | Track colour over the window background | Alpha measured on the Notification Center panel |
| Pressed | Knob fill `#f0f0f0`, `#e0e0e0` in Dark Aqua, while the mouse is held down | Inferred |
| Keyboard focus | Ring colour, and that the band ends 2.5 pt beyond the track; both appearances | Measured on a text field's ring |
| Main and inactive window | On is drawn in neutral grey; no focus ring; both appearances | Inferred |

The pressed knob, the main and inactive window and the purple and graphite accents are checked
in Dark Aqua as well as in Aqua. The dark values are computed from the same tokens: the neutral
on track is white at 25% over the off track, `#707070` on the window background `#323232`.

These checks hold the component to the spec; where the spec infers a value, they do not show
that Catalina looks that way. Not checked anywhere: the disabled look, the small and mini
sizes, and the on state's rim.
