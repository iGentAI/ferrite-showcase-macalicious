<Example name="basic" both />

AppKit's `NSStatusItem`. The extras sit in a group at the trailing end of a
[menu bar](/components/menu-bar). The group is a
[Base UI Menubar](https://base-ui.com/react/components/menubar) of its own, as the status
menus are a second menu bar in macOS; an extra's menu is a [`Menu`](/components/menu) attached
under the bar.

The example shows the extras in both appearances. A menu opened from one renders in a portal
and takes the appearance of the page, not of the bar it hangs from.

## Usage

```tsx
import { MenuBar, MenuBarMenu } from "macalicious/menu-bar";
import { MenuBarExtra, MenuBarExtraMenu, MenuBarExtras } from "macalicious/menu-bar-extra";
import { MenuItem } from "macalicious/menu-item";
import "macalicious/menu.css";
import "macalicious/menu-item.css";
import "macalicious/menu-bar.css";
import "macalicious/menu-bar-extra.css";

<MenuBar
  extras={
    <MenuBarExtras>
      <MenuBarExtraMenu title={<BatteryImage />} aria-label="Battery">
        <MenuItem>Show Percentage</MenuItem>
      </MenuBarExtraMenu>
      <MenuBarExtraMenu title="Stephen">
        <MenuItem>Login Window…</MenuItem>
      </MenuBarExtraMenu>
      <MenuBarExtra aria-label="Search" active={searching} onClick={toggleSearch}>
        <MagnifierImage />
      </MenuBarExtra>
    </MenuBarExtras>
  }
>
  <MenuBarMenu title="Notes" application>…</MenuBarMenu>
</MenuBar>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add menu-bar-extra menu-item`.

Pass the group to the bar's `extras` prop. `MenuBarExtraMenu` is an extra with a menu;
`MenuBarExtra` is one without, which acts when clicked and can be kept highlighted with
`active` while the panel it opened is showing. An extra's content is an image, a text, or
both; draw images with `currentColor` (template images), so that they take the bar's text
colour and turn white while the extra is highlighted. The system's own images (Wi-Fi,
battery, Spotlight and the others) are Apple's and are not included.

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines,
[Menu Bar Extras](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503161847/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/extensions/menu-bar-extras/):

- Use a template image, so that the extra looks right in both appearances and while it is
  highlighted.
- Show a menu, not a popover, when the extra is clicked, unless the content cannot be a menu.
- Do not rely on the extra being visible: the system hides extras when the application menus
  need the room. Offer the same commands elsewhere.
- Let people decide whether the extra is shown.

## Variants and states

| Part or state | Look | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cell | As high as the bar; the content and 7 pt on each side; cells abut and are packed against the trailing edge | Measured: the system's cells leave 4.5 to 11 pt beside the image |
| Image or text | The bar's text colour; 14 pt text with figures of equal width | Measured |
| `emphasized` | Bold text, for an extra that should stand out. The user name is not bold: in the screenshots it has the clock's regular weight, or close to it | Provisional: no screenshot shows a bold extra |
| Menu open, or `active` | The cell filled from top to bottom, square, content white. In the blue accent the fill is lighter than that of an open menu title | Measured (battery, Time Machine and user menus) |
| Other accents | The highlight of an open menu title | Provisional |
| Keyboard focus | The same highlight | Provisional |
| Disabled | Content in the disabled menu item colour | Provisional |
| No room | The group gives way to the titles and is cut off from its leading side | Provisional: macOS hides whole extras |

## Sizes

One size. Set `--mac-menu-bar-extra-padding` on an extra (or on the group) for the space
beside its content, or give the cell a `width`, as the system's extras differ.

## Accessibility

- The group is a second `menubar`, named "Status menus" unless `aria-label` says otherwise.
  Its extras are `menuitem`s; one is in the tab order, and Left and Right Arrow move between
  them, the ones without a menu included.
- An extra with a menu has `aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded`; Down Arrow, Return or
  Space opens it. An extra without a menu announces none and acts on Return, Space or a click.
  If it shows a panel, name the relationship yourself (`aria-controls`, `aria-expanded`).
- An extra whose content is an image needs an `aria-label`.
- While one extra's menu is open, moving the pointer to another extra switches to its menu.
  The titles and the extras are separate menu bars, so the pointer does not switch between a
  title's menu and an extra's.
- Extras that are cut off for want of room stay in the keyboard order.

The images are stand-ins of the size of Apple's, so the whole-crop scores mostly measure
artwork and text that differ by design. "Drawing only" masks them, which for the row of extras
leaves the bar itself, and halves the colour threshold. In the battery crop the ends of the
neighbouring images are masked as well; what remains is the highlight, the bar and the first
row of the attached menu under the cell.

The Time Machine menu hangs under its extra at the cell's leading edge; the fast user
switching menu hangs at the trailing edge of the user name, because it would not fit the
screen otherwise. Its two user rows (34 pt, a round picture and a name) are the application's
own content, composed in the fixture inside ordinary menu items; the pictures are stand-ins.

In Dark Aqua these two menus are held to pixels of their screenshots, not to a score of the
whole crop: the open cell's highlight, the highlighted row's top and bottom, and the picture
just outside the menu's leading and trailing edges. The reason is the shared menu material.
Over this corner of the Dark picture, which is nearly black, it comes out about 16 levels
lighter than the capture (`#2c3750` where Catalina has `#1c2e49`), more than a comparison of
the drawing may differ by. Over the lighter sky at the other end of the bar the same material
is within 4 levels ([Menu](/components/menu)).

Two more checks use real input: with the user menu open the pointer moves to the battery
extra, which must take the highlight; and a click on an extra without a menu must leave it
without one.

What is not exact: the desktop picture is replaced by a gradient of its colours under the
bar; the material and the vibrant content colour are as on the [Menu Bar](/components/menu-bar)
page; Siri's coloured orb is a template image like the others.

Provisional: the highlight in accents other than blue, keyboard focus, the disabled look,
and what happens when there is no room.
