<Example name="basic" both />

The window Command-Space opens in the upper middle of the screen. The component is the window
and its keyboard handling; what is found, and what the preview shows, is the application's.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Spotlight, SpotlightGroup, SpotlightResult } from "macalicious/spotlight";
import "macalicious/spotlight.css";

<Spotlight
  value={query}
  onValueChange={setQuery}
  selectedValue={selected}
  onSelectedValueChange={setSelected}
  onOpenResult={(value, { reveal }) => open(value, reveal)}
  onClose={close}
  completion="macOS"
  preview={<Definition of={selected} />}
>
  <SpotlightGroup label="Folders">
    <SpotlightResult value="os-x" icon={<FolderIcon />}>OS X</SpotlightResult>
  </SpotlightGroup>
</Spotlight>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add spotlight`.

The children are the results. Without children, or with an empty list of them, the window is
the field alone; render the groups once there is something to show. Listen to `onValueChange`, search, and render what was
found; select the top hit by setting `selectedValue` (or `defaultSelectedValue`) to its
`value`. The window does not place itself: put it in a fixed layer, 210 pt from the top of the
screen and centred, in the same page as what it should blur.

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines for
[Search and Spotlight](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503193417/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/system-capabilities/search-and-spotlight/):

- Order results by relevance and put the most likely one first, selected, so that Return opens
  it without further typing.
- Group results by kind and name each group, so that people can skip to the kind they want.
- Show a useful preview of the selected result, so that many searches end without opening
  anything.
- Open the window on a shortcut and close it on Escape; never make people reach for the
  pointer to search.

## Variants and states

<Example name="collapsed" both hideCode />

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collapsed | no children, or an empty list | The 56 pt field alone, with the placeholder in the secondary colour |
| Expanded | children | A divider, then the list (289 pt), a divider and the preview (390 pt), 374 pt high |
| Note | `note`, while expanded | A divider, then one 24 pt line of 11 pt secondary text under the list and preview: what the search left out |
| Completion | `completion`, once text is typed | An em dash and the completion in 13 pt secondary text after the typed text |
| Top hit | `topHit` | The top hit's icon in a 28.5 pt box at the trailing end of the field |
| Selected result | `selectedValue`, a click, the arrow keys | Full-width accent fill with white text |
| Selected result, window not key | `data-window-state` of `main` or `inactive` on an ancestor | Grey fill, text unchanged |

The window is the vibrant panel material: it blurs and tints whatever is behind it in the same
page, and falls back to an opaque colour with `prefers-reduced-transparency` or where
`backdrop-filter` is not available.

Measured: the expanded window, bands, rows, dividers, selection and text colours in both
appearances; the rim, outer hairline and shadow in Dark Aqua. Provisional: the collapsed
window, the outer hairline in Aqua (the Aqua capture is over the desktop picture, where no
line can be told from the shadow) and the selection of a window that is not key; no capture
shows them.

## Sizes

One size: 680 pt wide, 56 pt high collapsed and 430 pt expanded (454 pt with a note). `--mac-spotlight-width` and
`--mac-spotlight-body-height` change the width and the height of the results for a smaller
screen; the field, bands and rows keep their heights.

## Accessibility

- The field is a `combobox` built on Base UI's Input, named by `placeholder`; it controls a
  `listbox` of `option`s in `group`s named by their category. The keyboard focus stays in the
  field and `aria-activedescendant` names the selected result, and only while that result is
  among the children: a selection whose result is gone is dropped (`onSelectedValueChange`
  with `null`) and Return opens nothing.
- The list is not Base UI's Combobox or Autocomplete: in Base UI 1.8 their highlighted item
  cannot be set by the application or by a click, which the top hit, the preview and the
  Command-arrow jumps need, and pressing an item writes it into the field. The roles, states
  and keys are those of the WAI-ARIA combobox pattern with a listbox.
- Down and Up Arrow move the selection and stop at the ends; Command-Down and Command-Up jump
  to the first result of the next and previous category; Return opens the selection and
  Command-Return reveals it (`onOpenResult` with `reveal`); Escape clears the text, then calls
  `onClose`. Keys pressed while an input method composes are left to it.
- A click selects a result and takes the keyboard focus to the field, wherever it was; a
  double click opens the result. A result's `id` is the window's: the field refers to it.
- The completion and the icons are decorative and hidden from assistive technology: the top
  hit is announced as the selected result.
- The preview is a `group` named by `previewLabel`. Moving the focus into it is the
  application's: give what it holds a tab stop.
- A `note` is a `note` after the list and the preview, outside both: the arrow keys and the
  field never reach it, and a screen reader finds it after the results. Use it for a limit the
  person should know of, such as part of their data that was not searched.
- While the field has the keyboard focus in the key window, a line in the focus colour runs
  under its text, besides the insertion point. Catalina shows the insertion point alone; the
  line is the library's own.
- In forced-colours mode the window has a border, the focused field an outline, and the
  selection uses the system highlight.

## Visual comparison

`visual-list-dark` reproduces the top-left 300 x 250 pt of the Dark Aqua window, which was
captured as a window with nothing behind it; `visual-top-light` the top-left 200 x 98 pt of
the Aqua window, which was captured over the desktop picture, on gradients that stand in for
the blurred picture. Text and the result icons are masked: text is set in the fallback font
off Apple platforms and the icons are Apple artwork. Results in Chromium on Linux, at a colour
distance of 0.05:

| Comparison | 2x | 1x |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field, bands, selection and dividers, Dark Aqua | 0.40% of pixels differ | 0.46% |
| Field, band and selection, Aqua | 1.45% | 1.24% |

What differs in Dark Aqua is the magnifying glass, an original drawing, and the corner. In
Aqua it is also the backdrop: the stand-in gradients follow the picture only roughly. Two
state checks run real input: Down Arrow in the field moves the selection to the second result
and the focused field shows its focus line, and a window that is not key shows the grey
selection (inferred).

Not compared: the query text (San Francisco Display Light at about 27 pt; the fallback font
at weight 300 is the nearest), the completion, the preview's content, which is the
application's, and the window's shadow.
