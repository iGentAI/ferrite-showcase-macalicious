The panel in the playback bar of Music and Podcasts, called the LCD since iTunes. Idle, it shows
a mark in the middle; with an item loaded it shows two centred lines of text over a 3 pt
progress line. It is custom chrome with no AppKit class of its own. The progress line is
[Base UI's Progress](https://base-ui.com/react/components/progress), or
[Base UI's Slider](https://base-ui.com/react/components/slider) when it can be dragged.

## Usage

```tsx
import { NowPlayingLcd } from "macalicious/now-playing-lcd";
import "macalicious/now-playing-lcd.css";

<NowPlayingLcd
  title="Morning Walk"
  subtitle="The Placeholders — First Album"
  artwork={<img src={cover} alt="Cover of First Album" />}
  elapsed={62}
  duration={215}
/>
```

The display is as wide as its container and 45 pt high; in a playback bar that container is the
`MediaAppChromeDisplay` of [Media App Chrome](/components/media-app-chrome). Without a `title`
it is idle and shows its children, centred: pass a mark of your own. The library draws no
logo.

## Guidelines

- Show what is playing: the title on the first line, and on the second the artist and album,
  or the show and date, joined by an em dash.
- Keep the display idle, not empty, when nothing is loaded: the placeholder in the artwork
  well and a quiet mark in the middle.
- Give artwork an `alt` text. The title beside it does not describe the picture.

## Sizes

There is no `size` prop. The panel is 45 pt high with a 44 pt artwork well (44.5 pt in the
Music variant, as measured) and takes its container's width: 400 pt in Music's 1220 pt window
and 351 pt in Podcasts' 1137 pt window, 40% of the bar in both.

## API

<PropsTable name="NowPlayingLcdProps" />

| Attribute | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data-variant` | `music`, `podcasts` | Reflects `variant`. |
| `data-state` | `idle`, `playing` | `playing` when a `title` is set. |
| `data-empty` | present or absent, on `.mac-now-playing-lcd-artwork` | The well shows the placeholder. |
| `data-remaining` | present, on the second `.mac-now-playing-lcd-time` | The remaining-time label. |

## Accessibility

- The root is a `group` named "Now Playing"; `aria-label` changes the name. The title and
  subtitle are plain text inside it.
- Without `onSeek` the progress line is a `progressbar` named "Playback position"
  (`seekLabel` changes it) whose value text is the position and the length, for example
  "1:02 of 3:35".
- With `onSeek` it is a `slider` with the same name and value text, in the tab order. The Left
  and Right arrow keys move one second, Page Up and Page Down fifteen, Home and End go to the
  ends. `onSeekCommitted` reports where a drag or a key press ended.
- The time labels are hidden from assistive technology: the value text carries the same
  information.
- The placeholder glyph is decorative. Artwork you pass needs its own text alternative.

## Visual comparison

`now-playing-lcd.visual.json` compares the idle display with Music in both appearances and with
Podcasts in Aqua, and the loaded display with Podcasts in Dark Aqua, the one capture that shows
an item loaded. Apple's logo and the Apple Podcasts mark are masked, and so is the show's
artwork. The loaded display is scored twice: with its two lines of text, which are set in the
fallback font off Apple platforms, and with the text masked, which scores the drawing alone.

Scores from `npm run visual -- now-playing-lcd` in Chromium on Linux: the share of pixels that
differ by more than the comparison's threshold, and the mean colour difference out of 255.

The 1x scores are higher because the half-point border cannot match its averaged counterpart.
What remains at 2x in the Music comparisons is the placeholder glyph, an original drawing of
two beamed notes in the measured box.

Provisional, because no screenshot shows them: the progress colours in Aqua, the time labels
and the scrubber knob, the Podcasts placeholder colour and corner in Dark Aqua, the divider
after the Podcasts well in Dark Aqua, and the inactive-window look. Two pixel checks move
keyboard focus to the knob, press Home or End, and hold the focus ring to its token on every
side of the panel; no pixel check covers the hover state yet.
