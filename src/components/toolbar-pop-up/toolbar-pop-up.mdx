AppKit's `NSPopUpButton` with the textured rounded bezel, and `NSMenuToolbarItem`. Three
components, each a [toolbar button](/components/toolbar-button) with a chevron:

- `ToolbarPullDown` opens a menu of commands below itself. Built on
  [Base UI's Menu](https://base-ui.com/react/components/menu) with the library's
  [menu](/components/menu).
- `ToolbarPopUp` shows the current choice and opens the choices over itself. It is a
  [pop-up button](/components/pop-up-button), on Base UI's Select, drawn as a toolbar button.
- `ToolbarSplitPullDown` is a main part that acts when clicked and a narrow part that opens a
  menu, in one bezel: the two segments of a momentary
  [toolbar segmented control](/components/toolbar-segmented-control).

## Usage

```tsx
import { MenuItem, MenuSeparator } from "macalicious/menu-item";
import { PopUpButtonItem } from "macalicious/pop-up-button";
import { ToolbarPopUp, ToolbarPullDown, ToolbarSplitPullDown } from "macalicious/toolbar-pop-up";
import "macalicious/styles.css";

<ToolbarPullDown image={gearGlyph} aria-label="Action">
  <MenuItem onClick={newFolder}>New Folder</MenuItem>
  <MenuSeparator />
  <MenuItem onClick={getInfo}>Get Info</MenuItem>
</ToolbarPullDown>

<ToolbarPopUp value={font} onValueChange={setFont} aria-label="Font" style={{ width: 120 }}>
  <PopUpButtonItem value="helvetica">Helvetica</PopUpButtonItem>
  <PopUpButtonItem value="menlo">Menlo</PopUpButtonItem>
</ToolbarPopUp>

<ToolbarSplitPullDown image={penGlyph} aria-label="Highlight" onClick={highlight}
  menuLabel="Highlight colors">
  <MenuItem onClick={yellow}>Yellow</MenuItem>
</ToolbarSplitPullDown>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add toolbar-pop-up`.

The children are the menu: menu items for the pull-down and the split button, pop-up button
items for the pop-up. `image` is a glyph of your own, about 14 pt high, drawn with
`currentColor`; no Apple artwork is included. Inside a [toolbar](/components/toolbar) the
buttons join its arrow-key navigation; they also work on their own.

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines,
[Toolbars](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503121036/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/windows-and-views/toolbars/)
and
[Pull-Down Buttons](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503154252/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/buttons/pull-down-buttons/):

- Offer an Action pull-down button in the toolbar for the commands of the selected item,
  instead of a contextual menu for the toolbar; the same commands should be in the menu bar.
- Use a pull-down button for commands and a pop-up button for one choice out of several.
- Keep 8 pt between a toolbar pop-up and the items beside it.

## Variants and states

<Example name="kinds" both />

| State | How it is triggered | Look | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | – | The toolbar button's bezel, 22 pt high; glyph or title, then the chevron 5 pt from the trailing edge | Measured (Finder's Group and Action buttons) |
| Open | click, Space, Return or Down Arrow | The toolbar button's "on" bezel while the menu is open | Provisional |
| Disabled | `disabled` | The bezel stays, glyph and chevron dim | Measured (Preview's Highlight button) |
| Split | `ToolbarSplitPullDown` | One bezel, a 1 pt divider, a 19 pt menu part with the small heavy chevron | Measured (Preview's Highlight button) |
| Compact | `compact` | 5 pt before the content and the chevron 4 pt after it, 7 pt wide | Measured (Preview's View button) |
| Keyboard focus, window not key | as the toolbar button | See [Toolbar Button](/components/toolbar-button) | – |

The pull-down's chevron is 7.5 x 4.5 pt with a 1.5 pt stroke; the split button's is 7 x 4.5 pt
with a 1.75 pt stroke. A pull-down with a 14 pt glyph is 46 pt wide, as in Finder: 10 pt, the
glyph, 9.5 pt, the chevron, 5 pt.

### Compact

Preview's View button is narrower than Finder's buttons: 36 pt with a 15 pt glyph, laid out as
5 pt, the glyph, 4 pt, the chevron, 5 pt, and its chevron is 7 pt wide. `compact` on a
`ToolbarPullDown` or a `ToolbarPopUp` gives that layout; the bezel and the states are unchanged.

## Sizes

Toolbar controls have one size: 22 pt high. A pull-down button is as wide as its content, 46 pt
with a 14 pt glyph, or 36 pt with a 15 pt glyph when it is `compact`; give
a pop-up a `width`, because its title changes with the choice. `style` on the split button
sizes its main part.

## Accessibility

- A pull-down is a `<button>` with `aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded`; a pop-up has
  `role="combobox"` with a `listbox`, as the pop-up button.
- A button that shows only a glyph needs an `aria-label`. The split button needs two names:
  `aria-label` for the main part and `menuLabel` for the part that opens the menu.
- Down Arrow, Return or Space opens the menu; the arrow keys move, Return or Space chooses,
  Escape closes and returns focus to the button.
- In a toolbar, Left and Right Arrow move between the items and the toolbar is one tab stop;
  the two parts of a split button are two items. On its own a split button is one tab stop
  with arrow keys between its parts.
- The chevron is decoration and hidden from assistive technology.

## Visual comparison

`npm run visual -- toolbar-pop-up/` compares three examples with the reference screenshots in
both appearances (`toolbar-pop-up.visual.json`): the Group and Action buttons of a Finder
toolbar, the disabled Highlight split button of Preview and Preview's compact View button. The
glyphs are masked, because they
are stand-ins drawn for this library; a pixel counts as differing at a colour distance of 0.05.
Share of pixels that differ at 2x, in Chromium on Linux:

| Crop | Aqua | Dark Aqua |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finder, Group and Action | 0.44% | 0.34% |
| Preview, Highlight split button (disabled) | 0.46% | 0.49% |
| Preview, View button (compact) | 1.22% | 1.12% |

What differs on the View button is the antialiasing along the chevron's arms: Apple's chevron
is a little sharper than the scaled one drawn here. Preview's glyphs are also lighter than
Finder's, on which the toolbar button's text colour is measured (`#737373` on `#f6f6f6` where
Finder has `#565656`; `#fbfbfb` on `#686969` in Dark Aqua, where the token gives `#f2f2f2`), so
the example sets `--mac-toolbar-button-text` to Preview's ink and the comparison scores the
compact layout, not that colour.

The open state is checked in a real browser (the `expect` comparisons): while the mouse is held
down on the Action button it has the "on" bezel and the button beside it is unchanged.

Provisional, or not compared:

- **The open look**: no screenshot shows a toolbar pop-up with its menu open.
- **A pop-up with a title**: the screenshots show none in a toolbar; its paddings are those of
  the pull-down.
- **An enabled split button, and the Disk Utility View button with its label below**: not
  compared. A label below an item is a
  [toolbar labelled item](/components/toolbar-labelled-item).
- **Glyphs** are not included; the examples draw simple stand-ins.
