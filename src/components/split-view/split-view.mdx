<Example name="basic" both />

AppKit's `NSSplitView`: the sidebar beside the content of nearly every Catalina window, the
three panes of Mail and Notes, the table above the detail pane in Console.

## Usage

```tsx
import { SplitView, SplitViewDivider, SplitViewPane } from "macalicious/split-view";
import "macalicious/split-view.css";

<SplitView style={{ height: 400 }}>
  <SplitViewPane defaultSize={200} minSize={150} maxSize={400} collapsible>…</SplitViewPane>
  <SplitViewDivider aria-label="Resize sidebar" />
  <SplitViewPane>…</SplitViewPane>
</SplitView>
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add split-view`.

Panes and dividers alternate as direct children of the split view. A pane with `size` or
`defaultSize` keeps that size; panes without one share what is left, so give every pane but
one a size. A divider resizes the sized pane before it, or else the sized pane after it (an
inspector on the trailing side). A divider adds its thickness to the layout: 200 + 1 + the
rest.

Control a pane to store its size or to collapse it from a toolbar button; a size of 0 is the
collapsed pane:

```tsx
const [sidebar, setSidebar] = useState(200);

<SplitViewPane size={sidebar} onSizeChange={setSidebar} minSize={150} collapsible>…</SplitViewPane>
```

Beside a translucent sidebar the Aqua divider is a little lighter than between opaque panes.
Set it on the split view: `style={{ "--mac-split-view-divider-color": "light-dark(#cfd0d0, #000000)" }}`.

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines for
[split views](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503170954/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/windows-and-views/split-views/):

- Prefer the thin divider. Use a thick divider or a pane splitter only when the panes need a
  stronger visual separation.
- Set reasonable minimum and maximum sizes so that a pane cannot be dragged until its content
  is useless, and so that the dividers stay visible.
- Let people hide a pane when that helps them concentrate, and give them a way to bring it
  back: a toolbar button or a menu command, not only the divider.
- Keep the panes related: selecting something in one pane changes what the next shows.

## Variants and states

<Example name="variants" both hideCode />

| Variant or state | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thin divider | default | A 1 pt line: `#d5d5d5` between opaque panes in Aqua, black in Dark Aqua. Its hit area is the line and 2 pt on each side |
| Thick divider | `dividerStyle="thick"` | A clear 9 pt band that shows what is behind the split view, with a 6 pt dimple at its centre, as between the two lists of the Sharing pane |
| Pane splitter | `dividerStyle="pane-splitter"` | A filled 10 pt band with the same dimple |
| Side by side | default | Vertical divider lines, resize pointer |
| Stacked | `orientation="vertical"` | Horizontal divider lines |
| Dragging | pointer held on a divider | No change of look; the panes resize as the pointer moves |
| Collapsed pane | `size` 0; dragging a `collapsible` pane under half its minimum; double click or Return on its divider | The pane is not rendered (`display: none`); the divider sits at the edge |
| Keyboard focus | Tab to a divider | Focus ring around the divider, in a key window only |
| Fixed divider | `disabled` on the divider | The same line; it cannot be dragged or focused |

A divider looks the same in a key, main and inactive window. The lighter dividers the Mail
window shows while it is inactive come from its inactive sidebar material, not from the
split view.

Measured: the thin divider's thickness and its colours between opaque panes and beside a
sidebar, in both appearances and both orientations; the thick divider's 9 pt band and its
dimple's size, shading and place across the band, in both appearances. Inferred: that the
dimple is centred along the divider (in the capture it lies under the middle of the two
lists, so the split view is taken to reach below them). Documented by Apple: the 10 pt
thickness of the pane splitter and the 5 pt hit area. Provisional (no screenshot shows them):
the pane splitter's fill and its dimple, which is taken to be the thick divider's (the dimple
is drawn on the pane splitter too, and is provisional there, though measured on the thick
divider); the focus ring on a divider; and the collapse threshold of half the minimum size.

## Sizes

A split view has no control sizes. It is as large as its root element: give it a height, or
place it in a flex or grid cell that has one.

Each part is a `div` and takes its attributes, `ref`, `className` and `style`. A divider's own
`onPointerDown` and `onKeyDown` run first and can cancel the resize with `preventDefault()`.

| Attribute | On | Values | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `data-orientation` | root | `horizontal`, `vertical` | Reflects `orientation`. |
| `data-divider-style` | root | `thin`, `thick`, `pane-splitter` | Reflects `dividerStyle`. |
| `data-sized` | pane | present or absent | The pane has a size of its own. |
| `data-collapsed` | pane | present or absent | The pane's size is 0. |
| `data-dragging` | divider | present or absent | The pointer is dragging the divider. |
| `data-disabled` | divider | present or absent | Reflects `disabled`. |

| CSS variable | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--mac-split-view-divider-color` | `var(--mac-color-split-divider)` | Colour of the thin divider line. |
| `--mac-split-view-splitter-dimple` | `var(--mac-split-view-dimple)` | The pane splitter's dimple, a colour or a gradient. Kept from before the dimple was measured. |
| `--mac-split-view-dimple-forced` | `CanvasText` | The dimple's colour in forced-colours mode. |

## Accessibility

- A divider is a focusable `separator` (the window splitter pattern) with `aria-orientation`
  and, as its value, the size in points of the pane it resizes (`aria-valuenow`,
  `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax`); `aria-controls` names that pane by its `id`, which is
  generated unless you give the pane one. Give each divider an `aria-label` that says what it
  resizes.
- A divider between two panes without a size resizes nothing. It is then disabled
  (`aria-disabled="true"`) and not a tab stop until one of the panes beside it has a size.
- Keys on a focused divider: Left and Right Arrow (Up and Down in a stacked split view) move
  it 10 pt, Home and End take the pane to its minimum and maximum, Return collapses a
  collapsible pane and opens it again at its previous size. In a right-to-left layout the
  arrow keys follow the direction the divider moves on screen.
- AppKit's dividers are not in the tab order; here they are, because the web has no other way
  to resize with the keyboard. That is a deliberate difference.
- A collapsed pane is removed from layout and from the accessibility tree. Provide another
  control to open it again; the divider can do it too, but it is a small target.
- In forced-colours mode the divider is drawn in the system text colour.
