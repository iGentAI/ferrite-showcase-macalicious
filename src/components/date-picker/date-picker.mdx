AppKit calls this control a *date picker* (`NSDatePicker`). It comes in two styles: textual,
a field whose elements (month, day, year, hour, minute, second, AM or PM) are selected and
changed one at a time, by default with a stepper beside it; and graphical, a month calendar
and a clock face. The library has one component for each form:

| Component | AppKit | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DatePicker` | `.textFieldAndStepper`, `.textField` | The field of elements, with or without the stepper. |
| `DatePickerCalendar` | `.clockAndCalendar`, date elements | One month as a grid of days. |
| `DatePickerClock` | `.clockAndCalendar`, time elements | The clock face. It displays a time and is not operated. |

Base UI has no date primitive, so the elements are written here after the WAI-ARIA Authoring
Practices: each element of the field is a
[spin button](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/spinbutton/) and the calendar is a
[date grid](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/examples/datepicker-dialog/). The
field's edge and the calendar's frame are the library's text field bezel, the stepper is the
library's [Stepper](/components/stepper), and props are merged with Base UI's `mergeProps`.
The value is a JavaScript `Date` in local time; there is no date library.

## Usage

```tsx
import { DatePicker, DatePickerCalendar, DatePickerClock } from "macalicious/date-picker";
import "macalicious/stepper.css";
import "macalicious/text-field.css";
import "macalicious/date-picker.css";

<DatePicker aria-label="Due" elements="yearMonthDay" defaultValue={new Date(2019, 9, 3)} />
<DatePicker aria-label="From" elements="hourMinute" value={from} onValueChange={setFrom} />
<DatePickerCalendar aria-label="Due" value={due} onValueChange={setDue} />
<DatePickerClock value={now} />
```

Or copy the source into your project: `npx macalicious add date-picker`. See
[Installation](/installation).

`value` with `onValueChange` controls a picker; `defaultValue` only starts it. A picker with
neither starts at 1 January 2001, midnight (the reference date of AppKit's `NSDate`), on the
server and in the browser alike: the components never read the clock while rendering. For a
picker that starts now, pass `new Date()` from the client, as state or from an effect.

`elements` names what the field shows, with AppKit's names: `yearMonthDay` or `yearMonth`,
`hourMinute` or `hourMinuteSecond`, or one of each separated by a space.

## Guidelines

From the macOS Human Interface Guidelines for
[date pickers](https://web.archive.org/web/20200503145009/https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/macos/selectors/date-pickers/),
as the project's measured specification records them (the archived page itself could not be
opened again when this page was written):

- A date picker lets people choose a date, a time, both, or a range of dates.
- Use the textual style where space is constrained and people enter a specific date or time.
  People select an element, then type, press Up or Down, or use the stepper.
- Use the graphical style where browsing days in a calendar helps, for a range of dates, or
  where a clock face suits the interface.
- The order of the elements follows the user's locale.

## Variants and states

A selected element is transient, so this gallery pins it. In an application it appears when
the element has the keyboard, together with the focus ring around the field.

<Example name="states" both hideCode />

| State | How it is triggered | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | – | White field (near black in Dark Aqua) with a square hairline edge; the stepper 4 pt beside it |
| Element selected | `:focus` on an element | The element on the selection colour with white text; focus ring around the field |
| Disabled | `disabled` | Fainter edge, text at 25%; the calendar dims its title and its three glyphs and keeps its days and its selected day |
| Day selected | `aria-selected="true"` | A 19 x 16 pt tile in the selection colour with white text |
| Day of another month | `data-outside` | Text at 25% |
| Window not key | an ancestor has `data-window-state="main"` or `"inactive"` | Grey selection with the label colour; no focus ring |

The selection follows the accent colour: set `data-accent` on any ancestor. See
[Theming](/theming). The window state is not a prop; a window component sets
`data-window-state` on itself.

Not in this component: the range mode (`NSDatePicker.Mode.range`), the era and time zone
elements, a 24-hour clock and other orders of the elements. The field is set as the
screenshots show it: month, day and year, a 12-hour clock.

## Sizes

<Example name="sizes" />

| Size | Field | Stepper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `regular` | 23 pt high, 13 pt text (measured) | 13 x 22 pt |
| `small` | 20 pt high, 11 pt text (provisional) | 11 x 19 pt |
| `mini` | 17 pt high, 9 pt text (provisional) | 10 x 16 pt |

The field is as wide as its text: the text starts 2.25 pt inside the edge and ends 3.5 pt
before it. Set a `width` on the picker to make the field wider; the stepper keeps its size.
The calendar is 137.5 x 147 pt and the clock 120 pt across; they have one size.

## Accessibility

- The textual picker is a `role="group"`; name it with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
  Each element is a `role="spinbutton"` named Month, Day, Year, Hour, Minute, Second or
  AM/PM, with `aria-valuenow`, its bounds and `aria-valuetext` (the day's upper bound is the
  length of the month).
- Tab, Left and Right move between the elements. Up and Down step the selected element; it
  wraps inside its own range and does not carry (December steps to January of the same
  year), and the day is held back to the last one of a shorter month. Digits type a value: a
  digit joins the ones typed before it while the result fits, and the next element takes the
  keyboard when no further digit could. A and P set AM and PM. Keys held with Command,
  Control or Option are left to the page.
- The stepper changes the selected element, or the first one while none is selected, and
  gives that element the keyboard. It is out of the tab order, because the keys work on the
  elements; its halves stay reachable as buttons named Increment and Decrement.
- The calendar is a `role="group"` holding three buttons (Previous month, Today, Next month)
  and a `role="grid"` named by its title, with a column header for each weekday (named in
  full, shown as two letters) and a `role="gridcell"` for each day. The selected day has
  `aria-selected="true"` and is the grid's one tab stop.
- In the grid the arrow keys move the selection by a day or a week, and Page Up and Page
  Down by a month, to the same day or the last one of a shorter month. While the focus is in
  the grid it stays on the selected day, also when a click on a day of another month turns
  the month. The calendar shows the month of its date and no other, as `NSDatePicker` does:
  the buttons and the keys change the date, the Today button to the current date and time.
  Home and End are not handled.
- The focus ring is drawn around the field or the calendar while something inside has the
  keyboard; a focused button also shows a ring of its own.
- A disabled picker has `aria-disabled` on its elements or days and disabled buttons, and
  nothing in it is in the tab order.
- The clock is a `role="img"` named with its time on a 24-hour clock ("22:56:59"); pass
  `aria-label` for another name. Its hands cannot be dragged: change the time with a textual
  picker that shares its value.
- The names (Month, Previous month, the weekday names) are English and cannot be replaced
  yet.
- In forced-colours mode the selection uses the system highlight colours and the calendar's
  glyphs the button text colour; the clock keeps its own colours.

Provisional, and checked nowhere against Catalina:

- the look of a selected element (the selection colour with white text, a 2 pt corner) and
  everything about a window that is not key;
- the enabled calendar: its edge (a text field's), its Dark Aqua background (the field's
  `#2a2a2a`), its title at the label colour and its glyphs at the secondary label colour;
- the dimming of days outside `min` and `max`, and the focus ring of a button;
- the small and mini sizes;
- that the previous, today and next glyphs change the date (as `NSDatePicker`, which has no
  displayed month apart from its date, is taken to do), and that the stepper selects the
  first element;
- the text inset of the field (2.25 pt before, 3.5 pt after), fitted to three fields whose
  text is San Francisco.

Measured, with a known difference: the numerals of the clock are placed by a rule fitted to
the screenshot (within 0.75 pt of each numeral's place), not by the artwork; the rim is 5.5 pt
all round, where the screenshot's is 5.25 pt at the sides; the screenshot's spec calls the
numerals bold, and their stems measure as regular, which is what is drawn.
